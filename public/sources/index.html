Workshop manuals

Haynes Felicia manual: consulted copy

Mark Coombs and R. M. Jex. Skoda Felicia Service and Repair Manual. J. H. Haynes & Co. Ltd. Manual 3505; title-page copy identifier 3505–336–11AF3; title-page copyright 2003; ISBN 9780857337498.

The title page states coverage of hatchback and estate versions, including special editions, with 1.3- and 1.6-litre petrol engines and the 1.9-litre diesel. It also includes the principal mechanical systems of the Van, Pick-up, and Fun. The engine construction and dimensions cited in Engines were checked against this copy.

This is a privately held commercial publication. Public articles cite its bibliographic identity and printed chapter/page locators; its text and illustrations are not published here.

Haynes Felicia manual: digital product listing

Haynes. Skoda Felicia Petrol & Diesel (95–01) Haynes Repair Manual — Digital Edition. Commercial workshop manual; the link leads to the publisher’s product listing, not to a copy of the manual. Accessed 26 September 2026.

The publisher lists coverage for hatchback and estate versions with 1.3- and 1.6-litre petrol engines and the 1.9-litre diesel. Features specific to the Van, Pick-Up, and Fun are excluded. Its specification context is UK/European vehicles; market and equipment differences need separate checks.

This product listing describes the publisher’s current digital edition and has a different coverage statement from the consulted copy. It is not evidence for that copy’s page numbers. Ownership of a copy does not grant permission to republish its text, photographs, or diagrams.
