Manufacturer publications

  • Škoda Auto (22 October 2024). Celebrating 30 years of the Škoda Felicia: the brand’s first model under the VW Group’s wing. Press release, PDF; also published as an HTML article by Škoda UK. Manufacturer source for historical background, body styles, engine offerings, and the stated production total. Accessed 26 September 2026.
  • Volkswagen AG. Caddy Pickup — Konstruktion und Funktion, Self-study programme 184, publication 640.2810.03.00, technical status 07/96; pp. 1, 4–5, 10–20, 22–26 and 28–36. Manufacturer publication retained by Volkspage.net; the imprint identifies July 1996 technical status, not a national sales or build cutoff. Consulted 7 October 2026. AEE/AEF technical selections and source-specific transmission, heating, electrical, body and optional-equipment architecture.

FIA manufacturer technical records

  • Škoda. A-5528 Felicia homologation forms. Original Group A form valid 1 December 1994, normal evolution of type 13/02 ET valid 1 March 1997, and other extensions. The FIA Historic Database download is named Group N but also contains the Group A original and extensions. System specifications, construction and gearsets are specific to their form or extension.
  • Škoda. A-5551 Felicia 1.6 homologation forms. Original Group A form valid 1 March 1996 and rally kit variant 04/01 VK valid 1 April 1996, among other extensions. Original Magneti Marelli and kit Jenvey specifications are distinct. Validity stamps identify FIA events, not road manufacturing or parts cutoffs.

Period sales brochures

  • Škoda Auto. Škoda Pickup. German-language international edition, March 1998, FCP 038 00 3/98. Four MPI/diesel offers, open/covered loading specifications, and a carburettor export footnote. Its international imprint is distinct from a German national sales list.
  • Škoda UK. Felicia Fun. March 2000 sales brochure. UK 1.6 MPI offer, cabin mechanism and dimensions/loading; its output panel has an inconsistent unit label.

Supplier service and accessory publications

  • Bosch Automotive Aftermarket. Spark plugs and glow plugs, 2017–2018. Product-number key and engine-specific supplier applications.
  • BRISK Tábor. Car spark-plug catalogue, 2017. Felicia MPI supplier selections. Its engine-code notation requires qualification against the technical B/M identifiers.
  • MANN+HUMMEL. MANN-FILTER product/application catalogue. Engine-selected service filters, product dimensions and application conditions. Supplier intervals do not establish factory production cutoffs.
  • Brembo. Felicia II hatchback service applications. Axle-specific braking products and technical dimensions; hydraulic alternatives require further selection.
  • Truckman. AeroTop for Felicia Fun. Undated accessory leaflet; distinct S and SL hardtops and construction. It does not establish installation on a particular vehicle.

Workshop and technical manuals

Jiří R. Mach. Opravy automobilů Škoda Felicia, Felicia Combi, Pickup: diagnostika závad, montážní postupy oprav, seřizovací hodnoty. Grada Publishing, 1st edition, Prague, 2001, ISBN 80-247-0189-8. The body-shell construction and production chronology is on printed pp. 132–133.

Haynes Felicia manual

Mark Coombs and R. M. Jex. Skoda Felicia Service and Repair Manual. J. H. Haynes & Co. Ltd. Manual 3505; title-page copy identifier 3505–336–11AF3; title-page copyright 2003; ISBN 9780857337498.

The title page states coverage of hatchback and estate versions, including special editions, with 1.3- and 1.6-litre petrol engines and the 1.9-litre diesel. It also includes the principal mechanical systems of the Van, Pick-up, and Fun.

Haynes Felicia manual: digital product listing

Haynes. Skoda Felicia Petrol & Diesel (95–01) Haynes Repair Manual — Digital Edition. Commercial workshop manual, publisher’s product listing. Accessed 26 September 2026.

The publisher lists coverage for hatchback and estate versions with 1.3- and 1.6-litre petrol engines and the 1.9-litre diesel. Features specific to the Van, Pick-Up, and Fun are excluded. Its specification context is UK/European vehicles.

The digital product listing has a different coverage statement from manual 3505 identified above.
