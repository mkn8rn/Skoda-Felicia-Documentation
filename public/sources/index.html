Manufacturer publications

  • Škoda Auto (22 October 2024). Celebrating 30 years of the Škoda Felicia: the brand’s first model under the VW Group’s wing. Press release, PDF; also published as an HTML article by Škoda UK. Manufacturer source for historical background, body styles, engine offerings, and the stated production total. Accessed 26 September 2026.

Workshop manuals

Haynes Felicia manual

Mark Coombs and R. M. Jex. Skoda Felicia Service and Repair Manual. J. H. Haynes & Co. Ltd. Manual 3505; title-page copy identifier 3505–336–11AF3; title-page copyright 2003; ISBN 9780857337498.

The title page states coverage of hatchback and estate versions, including special editions, with 1.3- and 1.6-litre petrol engines and the 1.9-litre diesel. It also includes the principal mechanical systems of the Van, Pick-up, and Fun.

Haynes Felicia manual: digital product listing

Haynes. Skoda Felicia Petrol & Diesel (95–01) Haynes Repair Manual — Digital Edition. Commercial workshop manual, publisher’s product listing. Accessed 26 September 2026.

The publisher lists coverage for hatchback and estate versions with 1.3- and 1.6-litre petrol engines and the 1.9-litre diesel. Features specific to the Van, Pick-Up, and Fun are excluded. Its specification context is UK/European vehicles.

The digital product listing has a different coverage statement from manual 3505 identified above.
