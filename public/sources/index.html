Sources and bibliography

Sources and bibliography records publications and resource entry points. Article-specific claims belong beside numbered citations in the article that uses them. A bibliography listing does not imply that a source covers every model or that its contents may be redistributed.

Manufacturer publications

  • Škoda Auto (22 October 2024). Celebrating 30 years of the Škoda Felicia: the brand’s first model under the VW Group’s wing. Press release, PDF. Public publisher-hosted source for historical background, body styles, and the manufacturer’s stated production total. Accessed 26 September 2026.

Parts catalogues

The parts catalogue and diagram index records resource-specific descriptions and access conditions for 7zap, partslink24, ASMET, Skoda-Parts.com, and Škoda Auto’s accessory catalogue. Use its named sections when linking a resource description; cite the actual assembly or product entry when making a technical claim.

Workshop manuals

Haynes Felicia manual

Haynes. Skoda Felicia Petrol & Diesel (95–01) Haynes Repair Manual — Digital Edition. Commercial workshop manual; the link leads to the publisher’s product listing, not to a copy of the manual. Accessed 26 September 2026.

The publisher lists coverage for hatchback and estate versions with 1.3- and 1.6-litre petrol engines and the 1.9-litre diesel. Features specific to the Van, Pick-Up, and Fun are excluded. Its specification context is UK/European vehicles; market and equipment differences need separate checks.

The product listing above describes the publisher’s digital edition. An article consulting another edition must identify that edition and cite its chapter, section, and page or other locator. Ownership of a copy does not grant permission to republish its text, photographs, or diagrams.

Community reports

No owner repair or upgrade reports have yet been entered in this bibliography. Future entries should identify the author or username, publication date, original report URL, vehicle configuration, and which observations the report actually supports.
