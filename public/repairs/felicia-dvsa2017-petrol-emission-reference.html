Felicia petrol emissions reference, DVSA 2017

The nineteenth-edition DVSA reference distinguishes petrol Felicia body/engine selections for an in-service emissions check. Its recorded idling ranges and exhaust concentrations are source-specific test criteria; they are not engine design dimensions, service wear limits or proof of a particular calibration product.[1]

Selecting the body and engine record

The Škoda section identifies the engine designation stamped on the engine block. Select the stated body and full written engine code together. Its short VIN labels EEF and EFF occur with both B and M engine versions; either label alone therefore leaves the engine version unresolved. The reference does not specify the positions of those three characters in a complete VIN, or a manufacturing interval.[1]

Passenger Combi and Vanplus are separate applications despite the shared broad type number 795. Ordinary Pickup and Fun likewise remain separate. No national trim, right- or left-hand steering, exact gearbox, restraint option, physical engine assembly or original parts limit follows from an emissions row.

Recorded test conditions

For the Felicia entries consulted, the reference gives a normal-idle CO maximum of 0.5% by volume. At fast idle it gives CO at most 0.3% by volume, hydrocarbons at most 200 ppm, lambda 0.97–1.03 and engine speed 2,500–2,800 rpm. The minimum oil temperature is 80 °C. Normal-idle speeds are selected separately below. These are the recorded conditions of the December 2017 edition; a measured reading does not identify the original engine-control or catalyst product.[1]
