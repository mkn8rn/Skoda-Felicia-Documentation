Genua and Perfekt price and sales-plan descriptions in May 1999

The contemporary launch report gives a Genua offer price and an importer forecast for Perfekt. These are distinct historical descriptions, not actual production/sales totals.[1]

Historical descriptions

4 May 1999 Polish launch report — different kinds of historical quantity
DescriptionRecorded value
Genua 1.3 offer price30,690 zł[1]
Perfekt 1.3 announced price expectationNo more than35,000 zł; final price not specified at launch[1]
Importer planned Perfekt sales500 cars; planned sales, not established actual production or transactions[1]

Applicability

The reported launch was at the beginning of May. Neither publication/launch timing nor an importer sales plan provides manufacturing/VIN boundaries or a verified produced-unit count.
