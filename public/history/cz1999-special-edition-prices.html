Contemporary reported range

The 4 March 1999 newspaper account gives engine-dependent ranges of 262,900–329,900 CZK for hatchbacks and 313,900–375,900 CZK for Combi. These reported endpoints differ from the manufacturer sheet’s individual entries and do not identify which engine produced either endpoint. The report describes a new red test car without selecting its precise engine/option configuration.[3]
