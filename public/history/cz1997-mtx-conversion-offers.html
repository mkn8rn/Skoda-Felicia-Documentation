Czech-priced MTX offers, 1997

The MTX RS leaflets date the Family accessory offer to 1 March 1997 and the Cabrio introductory offer to 1 September 1997. Price validity is separate from vehicle production and installed-part applicability.[1][2]

The sheet advertises an introductory 15% discount but does not explain whether another calculation should be applied to the printed prices. It shows no GLX 1.3 40 kW offer. This is a dated offer, not a statement that such a configuration never existed.[2]
