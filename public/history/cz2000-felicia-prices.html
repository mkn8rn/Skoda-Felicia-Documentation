Felicia Czech July 2000 prices

The Czech factory price list FC 019 15 07/00 took effect on 1 July 2000. Its published basic-equipment prices are in Czech koruna (Kč), including VAT. They identify dated catalogue offers, rather than the price of an individual sale or a vehicle build boundary.[1]

Restraint-option charges

The equipment matrix lists three separately priced restraint choices for all four grades of hatchback and Combi. Each includes front-belt pretensioners. The basic price row does not select one of these options.[2]

Listed restraint-option charges, including VAT
Restraint selectionAdditional catalogue charge
Driver front airbag with front-belt pretensioners9,800 Kč[2]
Driver and front-passenger airbags with front-belt pretensioners19,600 Kč[2]
Front and side airbags with front-belt pretensioners28,500 Kč[2]
