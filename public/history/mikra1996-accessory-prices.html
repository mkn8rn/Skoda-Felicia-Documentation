Historical offers

MIKRA’s June 1996 list states effectiveness from 1 June 1996. The amounts below select a single-unit offer, using the 1–4-unit tier where printed. The five-piece, supplemental and four-piece insulation amounts are Felicia entries; the list does not separately price Combi insulation. These Czech-koruna amounts are historic VAT-inclusive offers, not present values, manufacturing cutoffs or original factory-equipment prices.[1]

MIKRA five-piece bonnet sound-insulation kit was offered at CZK 470.[1]

MIKRA supplemental sound-insulation kit was offered at CZK 190.[1]

MIKRA four-piece bulkhead sound-insulation kit was offered at CZK 274.5.[1]

MIKRA Felicia luggage protective liner was offered at CZK 650.[1]

MIKRA Felicia Combi luggage protective liner was offered at CZK 750.[1]

MIKRA anti-corrosion kit was offered at CZK 650.[1]

MIKRA pedal-area protective mat was offered at CZK 79.3.[1]

MIKRA right empty luggage pocket was offered at CZK 240.[1]

MIKRA left empty luggage pocket was offered at CZK 240.[1]

MIKRA left luggage pocket with first-aid kit was offered at CZK 340.[1]

MIKRA under-parcel-shelf storage bag was offered at CZK 1,952 complete with a parcel shelf, or CZK 1,490 against exchange of an undamaged shelf.[1]

MIKRA parcel shelf was offered at CZK 372.1.[1]

MIKRA passenger-side storage pocket was offered at CZK 295.[1]

MIKRA adhesive reflective rear-body film was offered at CZK 225.[1]

MIKRA textile parcel-shelf lifting cord was offered at CZK 15.[1]

MIKRA Pickup interior kit was offered at CZK 1,041.4.[1]

MIKRA additional sunshade was offered at CZK 60.[1]

MIKRA 37 × 37 cm sun-control film was offered at CZK 66.[1]

MIKRA 115 × 44 cm sun-control film was offered at CZK 99.[1]

MIKRA protective self-adhesive strips was offered at CZK 238.[1]

MIKRA bulk engine-compartment insulation material was offered at CZK 396.5.[1]

The first-aid-equipped left pocket uses the listed 5% VAT category; the ordinary entries use 22%. These historic tax/packaging distinctions do not establish present certification, interchangeable physical products or original vehicle quantities.[1]
