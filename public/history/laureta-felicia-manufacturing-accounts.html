J. Laureta manufacturing and demonstration accounts

The J. Laureta programme produced special-purpose vehicles derived from the Felicia Pickup. Retrospective production recollections and a contemporary demonstration report have different dates, units and scopes.[1][2]

Maker recollection

In the 2020 interview Jaroslav Jelínek traces the project to discussions in 1997 and describes independent J. Laureta manufacture after Škoda did not adopt the project. Hearses were followed by ambulances and service vehicles. The maker says the project ended in 2001 when Pickup supply ended. The recalled order for 2,000 donor Pickups is an order statement, not a completed-vehicle production count.[1]

April 2001 demonstration

The municipal newspaper reports that Laureta auto presented workshop, ambulance, hearse and utility forms at Benátky castle on 5 April 2001. The utility form had two places for additional passengers. Its caption identifies Škoda Auto chassis, without providing the specific model, engine, type approval or a complete seating total for each displayed vehicle.[2]

Dates and production quantities

The publication’s lead describes about 200 J. Laureta vehicles during 1998–2001. Jelínek’s quoted recollection instead reports nearly 200 ordinary superstructures, about 40 hearses and 25 ambulances over two years. Body units, vehicle counts and the different periods cannot be summed into a verified unique production total or assigned to a particular engine or body revision.[1]
