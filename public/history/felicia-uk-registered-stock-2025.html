Felicia — United Kingdom registered stock, 2025

At the end of 2025, the selected UK register cohort contains 680 Licensed and 2,620 SORN entries under Felicia model names, a combined 3,300. The cohort comprises the published Cars and Light goods vehicles groups with a declared manufacture year from 1994 through 2001.[1]

Cohort and meaning

The count groups the publisher’s SKODA make and SKODA FELICIA generic-model records by their written detailed model name and licence status. Licensed and SORN are retained as separate administrative states; SORN denotes a Statutory Off Road Notification. The units are vehicles. These are year-end UK stock figures, separate from worldwide manufacture, first-registration totals and Great Britain-only quarterly figures.[1][2]

Earlier declared manufacture years and unavailable year values are outside this cohort; this separates the recorded 1960s Felicia entries from the selected modern-year group. The source’s Buses and coaches, Motorcycles, Heavy goods vehicles and Other vehicles groups are also outside it. A zero below is the arithmetic total of the selected published values, without an inference about an edition’s production or existence.[1][2]

The names are register labels. GLI, GLXI, L, LX and other written forms are retained distinctly; no unproved trim alias, body or powertrain equivalence follows from them.[1][2]

Interpretation

DfT describes these fields as administrative data and identifies inconsistencies involving model, fuel and engine size. A registered fuel/body group or detailed name does not establish original manufacture, an unchanged engine, factory trim or component fitment. SORN registration does not establish that a complete, restorable vehicle physically survives. The selected year field is a recorded declaration, without a factory production/VIN boundary.[2]
