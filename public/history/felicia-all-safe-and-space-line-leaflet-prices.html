Felicia All Safe and Space Line leaflet price descriptions

The two manufacturer leaflets use separate historical currencies and offer scopes. Recommended or comparative prices do not establish paid transaction prices or manufacturing/parts limits.

The price note describes importer-stock recommendations including statutory VAT. Metallic paint carries an unspecified surcharge; neither the printed currency equivalent nor the publication date supplies a factory component code or individual invoice.[1]

Space Line comparison, September 2000

The sales description states a CZK 36,000 comparison advantage against an LX with the listed equipment ordered separately. This is the stated comparison basis, not an unconditional cash rebate, a price for each engine or a production count.[2]
