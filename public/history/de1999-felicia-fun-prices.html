German Felicia Fun price account, 1999

Škoda Auto Deutschland’s 2020 history gives an approximate 1999 new-car price of €14,000 for the German Fun 1.6 MPI offer and €639 for optional partial-leather upholstery. These are retrospectively reported amounts; the account does not provide an exact price-list effective date, VAT basis or original currency conversion method.[1]
