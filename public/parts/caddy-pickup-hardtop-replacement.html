Caddy Pickup load-top replacement requirements

A replacement load top for the Felicia-derived Caddy Pickup must match the commercial load platform, cab clearance, tailboard travel, sidewall capping, fastenings and weather seals. The Caddy name alone does not identify those interfaces.

Recipient selection

Select the Felicia-derived Caddy and actual body/capping revision. Its period service programme does not identify complete mounting dimensions, fastener or sealing sets. Vehicle and top load limits require separate checks; a conversion or another Caddy body is not a direct replacement.
