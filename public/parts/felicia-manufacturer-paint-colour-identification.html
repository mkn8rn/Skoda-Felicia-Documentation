Felicia — manufacturer paint-colour identification

A colour designation identifies a declared body finish. It does not identify an orderable paint product, its supplier, formulation or complete coating system.

Coating and panel selection

The recorded descriptions do not supply pigment or metallic-flake content, binder chemistry, mixing proportions, primer/clearcoat identities, layer count, film thickness or curing conditions. Those properties and orderable product identifiers are Unconfirmed. A declared body colour does not establish the finish of a bumper, interior trim or a later replacement panel. Paint suppliers’ aliases and formulas require their own application evidence; the four-digit code alone does not establish chemical or visual equivalence.

Registered dark-red description

The Combi LXi 136B register selection 3063-008-01 records a dark-red body colour. It supplies no maker paint code or metallic/non-metallic classification. The current registered description does not identify the original coating or establish equivalence with the separately recorded maker colour 9885.[3]

UK 1999 finish descriptions

The UK leaflet dated 30 July 1999 uses a general metallic-paint description. The 13 December leaflet names metallic or Black Magic paint. Neither price face supplies a factory colour code, paint supplier, formulation or coating-layer specification. The Black Magic name alone does not establish a code or chemical equivalence with another market’s paint.[4][5]

Swedish Black Magic finish

The Swedish Black Magic advertisement describes a black-painted Felicia. Its three black-colour slogans do not establish three separate paint formulations or order codes. No metallic classification, paint code, supplier or coating specification is given; the edition name does not identify the UK price-list finish or establish a shared paint product.[6]
