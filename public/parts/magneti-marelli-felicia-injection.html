Magneti Marelli Felicia injection

The authorised February 1997 Felicia technical specification identifies Magneti Marelli 1AV multipoint injection for the 1.6 MPI 55 kW engine. The original FIA A-5551 Felicia 1.6 form names Magneti Marelli and describes its model as Multipoint; it does not give the 1AV designation. These are differently detailed identifications and do not establish one physical controller stock number.[1][2]

Metering and control

A-5551 specifies electronically controlled manifold injection with four effective fuel outlets and a throttle passage of 46 ± 0.25 mm. It lists oxygen, air-pressure, air-temperature, knock, Hall and coolant-temperature inputs. The coolant-temperature input is described as NTC, meaning a temperature-sensitive resistance that decreases as temperature increases. Controller, throttle, injector and fuel-pressure-regulator entries identify the principal control components.[2]

Component selection

The 46 mm passage belongs to the original A-5551 entry. It is not a dimension for the separately homologated Jenvey kit system. A-5551 does not identify an AEE engine code, a 55 kW rating, a national sales grade or a production/VIN limit. Original injector, controller, regulator and sensor identifiers, fuel pressure and connector/calibration variants are Unconfirmed for the homologation entry.
