Felicia cabin accessory fitment

A removable cabin accessory must fit the actual console or storage recess and remain secure without obstructing controls. A broad family listing does not establish that every Felicia has the same holder.

Recipient requirements

Identify the actual drink-holder recess, its depth and supporting profile, and the clearance needed to insert, remove and open the accessory.

Keep an inserted accessory distinct from the moulded holder, console, original ashtray and their mountings. Their separate original identities and build applications do not follow from the accessory listing.
