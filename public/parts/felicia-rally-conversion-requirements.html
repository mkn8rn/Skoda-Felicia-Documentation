Felicia rally-conversion requirements

Select the actual recipient shell, power unit, transmission and competition equipment before planning a rally conversion. A named grade or engine displacement alone does not establish complete-assembly fit.[1]

Recipient identification

Identify real shell and attachment revisions, engine/gearbox assemblies, steering, brakes, wiring and existing safety equipment. Competition modifications require their own fitment and approval selection; they are separate from standard original parts.
