Felicia Fun LX and vehicle descriptions in the manufacturer retrospective

The manufacturer account identifies a base Fun 1.3 MPI in the LX grade. Its engine-choice paragraph gives the sole stated 1.3 output as 50 kW. It does not establish a complete LX grade-equipment/parts matrix or a delivery/build class.[1]

LX identification

The written base designation is Fun LX 1.3 MPI. Full engine, injection/ECU calibration, gearbox, individual LX trim, steering and manufacturing/chassis selectors remain Unconfirmed. The report does not assign LX to the 1.6 or diesel offer by implication.[1]

Series body and seating

Fun uses a movable rear-cabin wall and folding additional seating. Complete original LX panel, seat, floor and attachment products remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Separately scoped vehicle ratings

The 1.6 description gives 1598 cm³, 55 kW, 163 km/h and 7.3 litres/100 km combined consumption under an unidentified period test-standard revision. Its recorded vehicle mass is 1035 kg and maximum payload 445 kg. Those 1.6 ratings do not select the 1.3 LX offer, other equipment or an original product.[1]

Physical selection

The Swiss publisher and the historically quoted Czech-crown price do not establish delivery country, steering side, a complete factory option matrix or price-effective manufacturing limits. Original engine/body/seating/wiring/trim products, quantities and chassis/option applications remain Unconfirmed.
