Pierburg 7.02256.02.0 electric switchover valve

7.02256.02.0 is a Pierburg electric-pneumatic switchover valve listed for AEF Felicia EGR-control service applications. It is an actuator-control product, not the complete exhaust-gas valve or a continuously regulating pressure transducer.[1]

Control function and interfaces

The maker distinguishes switchover valves, which change pneumatic actuator states, from pressure transducers used for continuous pneumatic regulation. The catalogue’s EGR classification identifies the intended control role; it does not supply this Felicia installation’s electrical or hose-routing diagram.[1]

Application limits

The maker’s I/II classifications and overlapping ranges do not establish factory body revisions or chassis cutoffs. The combined 6UF, 6U7 Pickup row does not separately identify Fun and ordinary commercial applications. The Cube Van label does not establish a particular Vanplus factory configuration. A service range ending in 2002 does not prove Felicia vehicle production continued to that date.[1]

Exact national grade, steering side, emissions package, physical valve connections, original equipment and production/VIN selection are Unconfirmed. A generic secondary-air technical-information reference alongside the product is not proof that the AEF diesel has a petrol secondary-air system.[1]
