Bosch FR 7 DC+ spark plug (0 242 235 666)

Bosch FR 7 DC+ is a spark-plug service product identified by Bosch number 0 242 235 666. Bosch’s 2017–2018 catalogue lists it for several Felicia 1.3-litre petrol applications.[1]
