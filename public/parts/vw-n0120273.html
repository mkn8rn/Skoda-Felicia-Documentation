Volkswagen spring ring — N 012 027 3

Volkswagen Classic Parts identifies N 012 027 3 as spring ring, with quality class Genuine Volkswagen Quality. Its current service associations do not establish historical original installation in a particular Felicia-derived Caddy.[1]

Caddy catalogue applications

The product record includes Caddy Mk2 Pickup model years 1997–2001. Those catalogue years are not a build/VIN or original-installation boundary.[1]

Physical selection

Confirm actual selector or load-top fixing, mating fastener, ring section and revision. Required mating parts, historical original installation, full engine/gearbox/body/steering/options and production/chassis selection remain Unconfirmed. A Caddy Pickup association does not establish fit to the Caddy van, ordinary Felicia Pickup, Fun, hatchback or Combi.
