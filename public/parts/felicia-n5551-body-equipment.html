Felicia N-5551 body-equipment specifications

The complementary N-5551 original form specifies selected cabin and exterior equipment; these are source-scoped classes, not full trim part numbers.

Contents

  1. Seating and luggage equipment
  2. Exterior and HVAC
  3. Application
  4. References

Seating and luggage equipment

The rear seat is described as a separate bench and is foldable; front and rear headrests are indicated. The rear luggage ledge is present and plastic. Exact bench division, seat/headrest quantities, covers, frames, latches and luggage-ledge stock numbers are Unconfirmed.[1]

Exterior and HVAC

A rear wiper is present; the form does not identify its motor, arm or blade. Air conditioning is explicitly absent. The spare wheel is located in the luggage-compartment floor; that location does not identify the retention hardware or rim product.[1]

Application

The equipment applies to original N-5551. Its statements do not identify a national trim, option code or production/VIN interval.
