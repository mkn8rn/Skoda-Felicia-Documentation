Felicia Hungarian dealer component descriptions

The November 1998 display-vehicle advertisement distinguishes nominal engine classes, named finishes and a Pickup with driver airbag and steering assistance. These are advertised selections without complete original component identifiers.[1]

Physical selection

A colour name does not identify a formulation, shade code, panel finish or production paint SKU. An advertised driver airbag does not specify a passenger airbag, pretensioners, steering side or the complete restraint system. Steering assistance does not identify a gear, drive or pump/control product. The advert does not establish factory rather than dealer installation of these features.[1]
