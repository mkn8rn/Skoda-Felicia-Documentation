Felicia A-5528 safety-cage specifications

The manufacturer records distinct Škoda steel safety-cage specifications in A-5528 options 01/01 VO and 10/06 VO. Their shared manufacturer does not identify one physical cage product.

The strength units above preserve the form’s written kg/mm² fields. The masses include fastening items and are not bare-tube weights or quantities of cage members.

Attachments and selection

The accompanying photographs define body attachment areas, foot plates and member junctions. Tube lengths/bends, complete plate/fastener dimensions, weld specification, member quantities and original product numbers are Unconfirmed here. These values alone are insufficient fabrication or installation instructions.

01/01 VO is valid from 1 December 1994. 10/06 VO is valid from 1 February 1996 and its model field carries a Felicia 1.6 annotation while retaining A-5528. That annotation does not transfer this cage to A-5551 or establish an engine assembly.[1][2]
