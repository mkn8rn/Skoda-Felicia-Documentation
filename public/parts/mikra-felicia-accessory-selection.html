MIKRA Felicia accessory selection

Select the actual bonnet, bulkhead, carpet, dashboard/restraint, shelf, seat or luggage-space interface first. A sound-insulation kit requires verified heat, fire, fluid, movement and attachment clearance. A storage pocket or floor mat requires secure retention and clearance from pedals, belts, seats, glazing and restraint deployment. A reflective or sun-control film requires its actual body/glass and approval selection.

Recipient requirements

Resolve body, steering side, installed equipment, physical material/geometry, fixing and applicable production/approval limits. A supplier’s universal application or a historical price does not identify a factory part, demonstrate a direct match or establish the quantity originally installed.
