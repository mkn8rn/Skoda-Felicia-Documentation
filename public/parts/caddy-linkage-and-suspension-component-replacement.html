Caddy linkage and suspension component replacement requirements

Replace the selected linkage or mounting component only after identifying the actual gearbox, steering or rear-suspension assembly. Similar dimensions or a shared vehicle name do not establish matching load, motion or retention.

Recipient requirements

Gear linkage

Match the selector mechanism, pivot and mating linkage. Retain the actual gearbox and mechanism revision; a listed selector piece is not the complete linkage.

Steering and joint protection

Confirm steering assembly and mounting side, bush retention and the gaiter’s joint seating. A right mounting position is not proof of right-hand-drive applicability.

Rear axle and suspension

Match the axle attachment, rear suspension and vehicle load specification. Establish mating geometry, contacts and the product-specific chassis selection. A named helper spring is not automatically equivalent to another bump stop or damper component.
