Škoda Felicia Morzine booster-seat accessory

The January 1999 Morzine notice identifies this accessory class for both passenger and estate body offers. It supplies no manufacturer or complete product identifier, so the edition name does not establish one physical product shared by every Morzine vehicle.[1]

Accessory description

The named accessory is a child booster seat. The notice does not identify its approval, child size/mass range, seat-belt routing, mounting arrangement or approved cabin positions; the edition description is not an installation instruction.[1]

Product selection

Manufacturer, original number and suffix, supply origin, production/option limits and physical interchangeability are unconfirmed. A later service accessory or another dated Morzine package is not identified as the same product.[1]
