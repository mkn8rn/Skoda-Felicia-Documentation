Left side support — reference 6U0807201

The May 2016 institute request specifies 6U0807201 for left side support in its AEF 47 kW LX hatchback chassis class. The reference records an intended service selection; it does not establish genuine manufacturer supply or original factory installation.[1][2]

Application and interfaces

The recipient class is TMBEHH613X0......, reported 1999 production and 47 kW. The six dots omit individual serial characters; they are not a parts-range selector. Full geometry, mounting/connection details and factory option, production or VIN application are Unconfirmed. A written reference alone does not identify every manufacturer’s alternative as one physical part.[1]

The one-item order quantity is a procurement quantity. It does not establish a factory-installed quantity or a complete delivery specification.[1]
