Felicia filter replacement

A replacement filter must match its fluid or air circuit, housing, seal and connections as well as the engine application.

Contents

  1. Recipient requirements
  2. Documented alternatives
  3. See also
  4. References

Recipient requirements

For an oil filter, match mounting thread, seal contact diameters, available clearance and the specified valve arrangement. Equal external dimensions alone do not prove interchangeability.

For a fuel filter, match fuel type, connection diameters, routing and mounting. For an air-filter element, match the installed housing and its sealing profile; SPI and MPI descriptions must not be conflated.

The MANN records distinguish supplier identities and selected engine applications. The 40 kW grouping does not expressly resolve 135M, and the air-filter record’s B-coded labels are qualified by an MPI condition. Those mappings remain Unconfirmed.
