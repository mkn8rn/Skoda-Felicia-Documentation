Electrical distribution and diagnosis

The hinged central fuse/relay assembly is below the passenger side of the instrument panel. The service diagnostic connector for V.A.G 1551 is nearby. The publication does not establish its pin allocation, diagnostic protocol or complete connector/loom product.[1]

On the diesel, extra fuses and glow-plug relay J52 sit at the left front strut tower beneath a protective cover. The air-conditioning package and ABS have separate additional fuse/relay provisions; those option circuits are not standard-fit claims for every car.[1]

Ignition load reduction

The ignition switch has no dedicated X contact. Relay J59 provides load reduction using the ignition/start terminal signals 15 and 50. It interrupts the X-fed consumers while the starter operates and restores their supply afterward. Contact rating, physical relay product, terminal connector and wire specification remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Petrol and diesel immobilisation

Immobilisation is standard in this source. Both architectures use a key transponder and the J362 immobiliser-control function. The petrol version authorises the engine-management controller; the diesel version acts through the J366 fuel-cutoff-control function. These identifiers do not specify matched controller hardware, coding, key generation or replacement products.[1]
