Felicia front-wing joint sealing

The front-wing mating faces form a sealed body-panel joint. The authorised repair description requires primed mating surfaces and the specified sealing material during wing installation. This is a service-joint application, rather than proof of a particular factory-installed sealer.[1]

Recipient joint and preparation

Select the actual front wing and its corresponding body mating faces. The cited repair section specifies primer on those faces before sealing. Joint condition and coating preparation therefore form part of the service application, independent of the wing’s production part number.[1]

Substitution and physical-selection limits

The repair specification does not give the exact panel identifier, sealant quantity, chemical preparation schedule, cure conditions or national/body/production/VIN boundaries. It is not evidence of original factory application. A replacement material needs verified adhesion to the actual primer and coating, joint movement and environmental compatibility, and an appropriate application/cure process.
