Škoda wheel-centre cover 5E0 601 151 FOD

The wheel-centre cover 5E0 601 151 FOD is a separately identified Škoda service/accessory product with a Škoda badge. The manufacturer lists Felicia (1994–2001) among its vehicle families; that family listing does not identify a particular original Felicia wheel, grade or chassis application.[1]

Felicia application

The manufacturer identifies a decorative wheel-centre cover with the newer Škoda badge and lists Felicia at family level. The record does not identify a Felicia rim number, body/engine split, market, production interval, option code or VIN range. Factory installation of this badge variant remains Unconfirmed.[1]

Wheel interface and selection

The specified 56 mm dimension describes the receiving opening for the cover. It is not a wheel-to-hub centring-bore specification. Retaining-clip geometry, engagement depth, overall cap diameter and clearance behind the cover remain Unconfirmed; the opening dimension alone does not establish a secure fit.[1]

Škoda wheel-centre cover 6U0 601 151 L MHB is a different recorded product. Neither the common badge function nor a Felicia family listing establishes interchangeability or supersession between the two covers.
