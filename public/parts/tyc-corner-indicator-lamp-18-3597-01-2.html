TYC right corner indicator lamp 18-3597-01-2

TYC identifies 18-3597-01-2 as a right corner indicator lamp in its 2018 lighting catalogue. It is a supplier product identity, separate from its listed associated references and from original factory fitment.[1]

The supplier’s two 1998 date ranges overlap in January–March. They do not identify a factory styling, panel, chassis or VIN change. Actual lamp shape/mounting, traffic/beam and aiming interfaces require separate selection. An electric-control symbol is not evidence that a motor is included or that a particular car has electric levelling.[1]

The catalogue separately numbers the -01-2 Unit variant and the -05-2 variant. It does not establish identical delivery contents or a supersession between them.[1]

Associated references

The catalogue associates this supplier number with 098788186A, 098788214A, 6U0953042A. These are the source’s cross-references; they do not prove a one-to-one physical identity, factory supersession or original installation.[1]
