Catalyst approval marking 441.0.7591-435.6

441.0.7591-435.6 is recorded as an identifying mark for an exhaust catalyst alternative in the cited Swiss Felicia approvals. The mark identifies the approved description; it does not by itself establish a manufacturer stock number, service supersession or unchanged physical design.[1]

Identification

The exact recognition string is 441.0.7591-435.6. Manufacturer, complete factory catalogue identity/suffix and documented aliases are Unconfirmed. The approval count prefix is separate from the identifying mark.[4]

Specification

The records classify this alternative as exhaust-treatment type K, meaning catalyst. Substrate, conversion chemistry, housing material, dimensions, connections and pressure limits are Unconfirmed. The classification does not supply a manufacturer repair or machining specification.[5]

Physical selection

The records establish approval descriptions and component counts, rather than a full original parts catalogue. A common recognition mark does not prove interchangeable joints, body routing, production revision or calibration. Alternative approval markings do not establish which unit a particular chassis received.[4]
