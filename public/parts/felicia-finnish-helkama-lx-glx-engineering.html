Finnish Helkama LX and GLX leaflet engineering

The original leaflet’s technical table uses LX and GLX and assigns both the same stated petrol-engine output. These grade labels and source-recorded values do not identify an injection system or a complete original component.

Powertrain description

The table describes a front-mounted transverse inline four-cylinder engine. It gives 1,289 cm³, 50 kW at 5,500 rpm and 100 Nm at 3,750 rpm, with a petrol octane requirement of 95. Injection architecture, compression version, full engine code, calibration and gearbox product remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Performance and consumption descriptions

The stated maximum speed is 150 km/h, and the stated 0–100 km/h time is 14 seconds. Recorded fuel consumption is 5.4 litres/100 km at 90 km/h, 7.4 at 120 km/h and 7.9 for the urban entry. The table does not identify a test-standard revision, tolerance or guaranteed operating result.[1]

Physical selection

The leaflet names LX and GLX; equivalence to LXi or GLXi remains unconfirmed. An importer imprint identifies the publication’s Finnish context, rather than an individual car’s delivery country, steering side or build. Full engine, gearbox, trim, option, original identifiers/suffixes, quantities and production/chassis selection remain Unconfirmed. A publication date from a different brochure is not an applicability limit for this leaflet.
