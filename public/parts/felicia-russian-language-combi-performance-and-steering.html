Russian-language Felicia Combi performance and steering

The undated manufacturer brochure reports advertised performance for four Combi engine classes and expressly identifies steering assistance for its diesel offer. Russian language alone does not establish a delivery country, steering side or manufacturing period.[1]

Test loading and a fully readable measurement-method definition are not established by the consulted passage; the diesel’s 90 km/h consumption is not a mixed-cycle figure.[1]

Diesel steering assistance

The diesel paragraph specifies steering assistance as standard for the described Combi diesel offer. It does not identify a hydraulic or other assistance architecture, rack manufacturer, pump, hoses, mountings, quantity or complete product. This statement is not transferred to the petrol offers or another national/grade programme.[1]

Physical selection

The engine code, gearbox product and ratios, steering side, body revision, grade/equipment, option code and production/VIN application remain Unconfirmed. A nominal performance match does not establish physical interchangeability.[1]
