Felicia A-5551 kit valves and manifold specifications

The 04/01 VK extension records separate intake and exhaust assemblies for the Felicia 1.6 kit.

Contents

  2. Exhaust valves and manifold

Intake valves and manifold

The intake manifold has two light-alloy elements. The inlet valve has a maximum head diameter of 40 mm, a 7 mm stem with +0/−0.2 mm allowance, and length 107 ±1.5 mm.[1]

Exhaust valves and manifold

The exhaust manifold is one steel element with a recorded internal outlet dimension of 68 mm. The exhaust valve has maximum head diameter 35 mm, a 7 mm stem with +0/−0.2 mm allowance, and length 107 ±1.5 mm.[1]

Head interfaces and physical selection

The manifold/head-interface drawing records 32.2 mm for the intake and 36 mm for the exhaust, with −2/+4% dimensional allowance. The valve/head dimensions are homologation fields, not machining, wear or replacement acceptance limits.[1]

Material grades, full valve/manifold identifiers, port coordinates, guide/seat interfaces, spring/retainer selection, fasteners and complete production application remain Unconfirmed. Shared nominal stem diameter does not establish one valve SKU.

Source application

The application is A-5551 04/01 VK, valid from 1 April 1996. It does not establish fitment to road AEE engines or another competition kit.
