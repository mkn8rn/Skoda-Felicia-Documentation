Felicia charging-system specification

The period repair specification describes a belt-driven alternator in a 12 V, negative-earth electrical system. Alternator current depends on the model and equipment; the manual gives a range rather than assigning a complete product to each configuration.[1]

Period rating scope

The stated alternator range is 55–90 A. The manual does not map each current rating to a full stock number, national grade, body revision or option code. The system’s nominal battery voltage is distinct from an alternator’s regulated-output designation.[1]

Source-scoped brush inspection

The manual specifies a brush minimum of 5 mm, with a printed tolerance of +1 mm/−0 mm. Its inspection measures the free brush length to the shortest point of the curved contact end; where the brush has a manufacturer’s measurement emblem, that mark is the reference. It calls for module renewal below the limit and inspection of the slip-ring surfaces for wear, burning or pitting.[1]

This repair specification does not identify the brushes, holders or regulators in a particular Magneton service assembly. It supplies no brush material, complete dimensions, spring force or slip-ring machining limit. A component-specific manufacturer limit remains necessary when the actual alternator is outside the manual’s demonstrated application.

Product selection

Identify the fitted alternator and equipment before selecting its rating or internal service parts. Matching a current rating does not establish mounting, pulley alignment, regulation, terminal allocation or interchangeable internals.
