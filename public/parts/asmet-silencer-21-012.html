ASMET 21.012 rear silencer

ASMET 21.012 is a rear silencer identified in the supplier’s Felicia service application catalogue.[1] It is a service-product identity; the corresponding original factory-installed component remains Unconfirmed.

Selection conditions

Match the body, engine description and exhaust layout as well as the joints, pipe route and hanger positions. A shared centre section does not make the complete hatchback, Combi and Pickup systems interchangeable.
