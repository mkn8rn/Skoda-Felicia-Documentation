Bosch eXchange nozzle-and-holder product 0 986 430 187

Bosch lists 0 986 430 187 as an eXchange nozzle-and-holder service product. It is a separately numbered service identity from Bosch assembly 0 432 217 240, not an alternative spelling for that number or evidence of a Škoda factory supersession.[1]

Catalogue identity

Bosch describes its eXchange programme as a repair-service alternative; factory inspection assesses returned diesel components against stated condition criteria. Engine-mounted attachments and fixing bolts are outside the exchange scope. This characterises the programme; it does not establish the history or condition of an individual unit.[1]

On printed page A13, Bosch associates 0 986 430 187 with several nozzle-and-holder product numbers, including 0 432 217 240. The catalogue does not establish a one-to-one supersession from that standard product to the eXchange number. Bosch's selection note explains that holder and nozzle specifications alone do not always identify an eXchange product, so the vehicle application also matters.[1]

Fitment limits

The catalogue identifies engine, rated output, vehicle label, supplier interval and quantity. It does not give a Škoda OE identifier, gearbox, market, steering side, grade, factory equipment, option code, or vehicle build/VIN boundary for these entries. The Bosch application therefore supports service-product selection within the stated catalogue scope, not a claim of original factory installation on an individual Felicia.[1]
