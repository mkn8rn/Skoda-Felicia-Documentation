Blaupunkt Verona — Felicia brochure selection

The Irish brochure names Verona among three Blaupunkt radio selections. The written name does not identify a full stock number or hardware generation.[1]

Contents

  1. Product-name scope
  2. Programme application
  3. See also
  4. References

Product-name scope

The programme describes theft-deterrence equipment and a built-in amplifier for its radios. The Verona revision, exact mechanism, chassis dimensions, connectors, electrical ratings and amplifier specification are unconfirmed. A radio with the same commercial name from another generation is not established as the same product.[1]

Programme application

The brochure offers a retailer radio choice but does not map Verona to a particular grade/body, factory original part or production/VIN range. Mechanical and electrical fitment therefore require the actual unit and car interfaces.[1]
