Felicia homologation brake systems

The original manufacturer forms for FIA A-5528 and A-5551 specify dual-circuit hydraulic service brakes with front discs, rear drums and an ATESO vacuum servo. Each records a tandem master cylinder and no braking regulator. The mechanical parking brake operates the rear wheels from a lever on the centre tunnel.[1][2]

The recorded master-cylinder and rear wheel-cylinder bores differ between the two forms. Their common front-disc and friction dimensions do not establish a shared physical part, hydraulic calibration or interchangeability.[1][2]

Hydraulic control

Both original systems use hydraulic actuation in two circuits, a tandem master cylinder and vacuum assistance identified as ATESO. The regulator field explicitly selects no regulator. It does not identify an ABS unit, the hydraulic circuit routing, master-cylinder product number or servo diameter.[1][2]

The A-5528 form prints 22 mm for both master-cylinder bore entries; A-5551 prints 22.2 mm. These are the recorded values, not two names for a confirmed common master cylinder. Seal sizes, piston assemblies, port threads, pushrod setting and mounting details remain Unconfirmed.[1][2]

Front discs

Both original forms select non-ventilated front discs. The outer diameter is 236 ± 1.5 mm and the new-disc thickness is 12.9 ± 1 mm. The recorded outer and inner diameters of the pad contact area are 234 ± 1.5 mm and 158 ± 1.5 mm respectively.[1][2]

These are homologation dimensions and form tolerances. They are not worn-disc limits, permissible machining dimensions or a complete manufacturing drawing. Disc height, hub-centre diameter, fixing geometry, material grade and surface finish remain Unconfirmed.

Front calipers and pads

Each original form specifies one cast-iron caliper and one hydraulic cylinder per front wheel, with a 54 mm cylinder bore. There are two pads per front wheel. The recorded overall pad length is 111 ± 1.5 mm.[1][2]

A cylinder bore is not the finished piston diameter or running clearance. The forms do not supply caliper or pad product numbers, pad thickness and profile, friction compound, piston finish, seal-groove dimensions, carrier fixing measurements or bolt torques. Those selection fields remain Unconfirmed; the common dimensions alone do not identify an equivalent caliper or pad.

Rear drums and friction linings

Both original forms specify rear drum brakes with a working internal diameter of 200 ± 1.5 mm. Each rear wheel has two friction linings, each with a developed length of 190 ± 1.5 mm and width of 30 ± 1 mm.[1][2]

The developed length follows the curved lining; it is not the straight distance between its ends. The lining count is recorded separately from the steel shoe, its springs and adjuster. The forms do not identify whether the lining is supplied separately or as part of a complete shoe. Lining thickness, friction material, attachment method, shoe profile, drum material and hub interfaces remain Unconfirmed. The stated internal diameter is not a maximum service diameter.

Parking brake

Both forms specify mechanical cable operation, a centre-tunnel control lever and action on the rear wheels. They do not identify the number or lengths of separate cables, end fittings, equaliser geometry or lever product number; those fields remain Unconfirmed.[1][2]

A-5528 02/02 VO

The front disc alternatives are 255, 258, 268, 280 and 300 mm, each ±1.5 mm, with corresponding rubbing inner diameters 171, 174, 184, 196 and 216 mm. Disc thickness is 10 or 20 ±1 mm; pad length alternatives are 74, 83 and 112 mm. Ventilation is optional. Rear discs are 230, 235, 240, 247 and 252 mm, each ±1.5 mm, with inner rubbing diameters 153, 153, 156, 163 and 168 mm. Rear thickness is 8 ±1 mm, pad lengths 34.5, 39 or 68 mm, and ventilation is excluded.[3]

One caliper and two pads per wheel are recorded. Either cast iron or alloy is permitted for the caliper-material field. The written cylinder-count fields are front 2+1 and rear 2+4; these are retained as fields rather than resolved to one inferred piston arrangement. Front bores are 34, 38.1 or 41.3 mm; rear bores 31.8, 34, 36 or 41.2 mm.[3]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5528 03/03 VO

One front-disc specification gives 295, 304 or 315 ±1.5 mm, inner rubbing diameters 197, 206 or 217 mm, thickness 28 ±1 mm and pad length 130 ±1.5 mm. A further front specification gives 266.7, 290 or 304 ±1.5 mm, thickness 25.4 ±1 mm and pad length 150 ±1.5 mm. The multiple inner-diameter alternatives on that further entry do not establish a unique disc pairing. Both select ventilation.[4]

Each front specification records one alloy caliper and two pads per wheel. Their separate cylinder fields are 2+2 with bores 38.1/44.5 mm, and 3+3 with bores 26.98/31.75/38.1 mm. These separate entries are not collapsed into one universal caliper.[4]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5528 08/05 VO rear brakes

Rear discs are 230, 235, 240, 247 or 252 ±1.5 mm, with respective inner rubbing diameters 150, 155, 160, 167 or 172 mm. Disc thickness is 8 ±1 mm and pad length 65 ±1.5 mm. Ventilation is excluded.[5]

The form records one alloy caliper and two pads per rear wheel, cylinder field 1+1 and bore 31.8 mm.[5]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5528 12/07 VO front brakes

Front discs are 266.7, 280, 290 or 304 ±1.5 mm, with inner rubbing diameters 182.7, 196, 206 or 220 mm. Disc thickness is 25.4 ±1 mm and pad length 130 ±1.5 mm. Ventilation is selected.[6]

One alloy caliper and two pads per front wheel are recorded; the cylinder field is 3+3, with bores 26.98, 31.75 and 38.1 mm.[6]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5528 14/08 VO front discs

Front disc and rubbing outer diameters are 330, 340 or 355.6 mm; corresponding inner rubbing diameters are 232, 242 or 257.6 mm.[7]

This entry does not assign a caliper, pad, thickness or complete stock number to the three diameter alternatives.[7]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5528 15/09 VO brakes

Front/rear disc diameters are 236/226 ±1.5 mm. Front thickness is 20–24 ±1 mm and rear thickness 10 ±1 mm. Rubbing outer diameters are 234/224 mm, inner diameters 156/143 mm and pad lengths 111/75.5 ±1.5 mm. Front ventilation is selected; rear ventilation is excluded.[8]

Per wheel, the form records one cast-iron caliper, one hydraulic cylinder and two pads. Front/rear cylinder bores are 54/38 mm.[8]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5551 01/01 VO brakes

Front discs are 255, 258, 268, 280 or 300 ±1.5 mm, with inner rubbing diameters 171, 174, 184, 196 or 216 mm. Front thickness is 10 or 20 ±1 mm and pad lengths 74, 83 or 112 mm; ventilation is optional. Rear discs are 230, 235, 240, 247 or 252 ±1.5 mm, with inner rubbing diameters 153, 153, 156, 163 or 168 mm, thickness 8 ±1 mm and pad lengths 34.5, 39 or 68 mm. Rear ventilation is excluded.[9]

The form records one caliper and two pads per wheel, with cast iron or alloy material alternatives. Cylinder fields are front 2+2 and rear 1+1. Front bores are 34, 38.1 or 41.3 mm; rear bores 31.8, 34, 36 or 41.3 mm. The rear bore and count fields differ from A-5528 02/02 VO.[9]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5551 02/02 VO brakes

Front discs are 295, 304 or 315 ±1.5 mm, with inner rubbing diameters 197, 206 or 217 mm and thickness alternatives 28 or 25.4 ±1 mm. Front pad length is 130 ±1.5 mm. Rear discs are 230, 235, 240, 247 or 252 ±1.5 mm, with inner rubbing diameters 150, 155, 160, 167 or 172 mm, thickness 8 ±1 mm and pad length 65 ±1.5 mm. Front ventilation is selected; rear ventilation is excluded.[10]

One alloy caliper and two pads per wheel are recorded. Cylinder fields are front 2+2, with bores 38.1/44.5 mm, and rear 1+1, with bore 31.8 mm. The source does not pair every thickness alternative with a diameter.[10]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5551 06/05 VO front discs

Front disc and rubbing outer diameters are 330, 340 or 355.6 mm, with inner rubbing diameters 232, 242 or 257.6 mm.[11]

This diameter entry does not independently assign a caliper, thickness or pad specification.[11]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

A-5551 08/06 VO brakes

Front/rear discs are 236/226 ±1.5 mm, with front thickness 20–24 ±1 mm and rear thickness 10 ±1 mm. Rubbing outer diameters are 234/224 mm, inner diameters 156/143 mm and pad lengths 111/75.5 ±1.5 mm. Front ventilation is selected; rear ventilation is excluded.[12]

Per wheel, one cast-iron caliper, one cylinder and two pads are recorded; front/rear bores are 54/38 mm. Coincident A-5528 dimensions do not establish identical physical brakes.[12]

The stated dimensions and allowances are homologation fields, not machining, wear or service replacement limits. Alternative diameters, materials and bores require a selected combination; no complete SKU or unstated pairing is inferred.

02/02 and 01/01 option hydraulics

A-5528 02/02 VO and A-5551 01/01 VO each specify two master cylinders, with listed bore alternatives 15.9, 17.8, 19.1, 20.6 and 22.2 mm. Neither assigns these bores to individual circuits. Both exclude a brake servo, specify a pressure regulator on the centre tunnel and use hydraulic parking-brake actuation. The cockpit regulator is distinct from a road rear-axle load regulator.[13][14]
