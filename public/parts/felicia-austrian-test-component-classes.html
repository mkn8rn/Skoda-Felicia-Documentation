ALLES AUTO describes these classes for its GLX 1.6 test car. The archive assigns model year 1998 and dates the web article to 2 January 2001. The specification does not establish every Austrian GLX fitment.[1]

Running gear and capacity

The tested car has front disc and rear drum brakes, steering assistance, 165/70 R13 tyres and a 42-litre fuel tank. Steering architecture, pump/rack products, tyre load/speed symbols, rim dimensions and original tank identity are Unconfirmed.[1]

Cabin and body

The report describes a facelifted front, a split rear bench that folds and can be removed, storage bins in the front doors and remote release of the rear liftgate. It gives exterior length, width and height as 3,883, 1,635 and 1,415 mm. The text does not provide a factory body code or side-door count.[1]

Application and selection

The application is the Austrian GLX 1.6 test specification. Exact engine/gearbox/body revisions, steering side, restraint/ABS option set, full component identifiers, quantities and factory production/VIN boundaries are Unconfirmed. The model-year label does not establish a build date.
