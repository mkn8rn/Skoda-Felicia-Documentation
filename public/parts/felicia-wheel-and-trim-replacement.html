Felicia wheel and trim replacement

A replacement wheel must satisfy the vehicle’s approval conditions and its actual hub, fastening, brake and body interfaces. A decorative centre cover is selected for the particular wheel opening and retention system; it is not selected solely by car model or badge.

Wheel and tyre requirements

Match the approval number and extension, vehicle type, relevant type key and rough-road equipment to the recorded wheel and tyre combinations. Preserve the paired rim profile, width, diameter, offset and tyre rating. A permitted alternative does not identify the factory wheel product or its trim interface.[3]

Separately verify hub centring, mounting pattern, bolt-seat form, bolt thread and usable engagement, wheel load rating, brake clearance, and body clearance throughout steering and suspension travel. The approval dimensions alone do not supply these physical component fields or confirm a different wheel casting.

Wheel-centre-cover requirements

Select against the actual wheel’s centre-cover receptacle: opening size, retaining lip and clip geometry, engagement depth and clearance behind the cover. The dimension specified for the cover opening is separate from the wheel-to-hub centring bore. Preserve the complete product suffix and required badge/finish specification.

Škoda lists both linked covers for the Felicia family but gives no original Felicia wheel-number mapping. A family listing and a nominal opening dimension are insufficient to establish either cover on Atlanta, Magic or another particular alloy-wheel configuration.[1][2]
