ASMET 21.007 rear silencer

ASMET 21.007 is a rear silencer identified in the supplier’s Felicia service application catalogue.[1] It is a service-product identity; the corresponding original factory-installed component remains Unconfirmed.

Selection conditions

Match the body, engine description and exhaust layout as well as the joints, pipe route and hanger positions. A shared centre section does not make the complete hatchback, Combi and Pickup systems interchangeable. Flange dimensions, pipe diameters, internal construction and approval markings are Unconfirmed unless explicitly specified above.

The supplier retains this rear section across its two 50 kW hatchback intervals, although the centre section changes at November 1998.[1] That catalogue split does not identify a factory change date or permit a road engine-code assignment.
