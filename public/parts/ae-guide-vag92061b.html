AE VAG92061B valve guide

AE VAG92061B is a distinct supplier service selection in the AE catalogue. Its service identity and supplier cross-references do not establish original factory installation or a supersession.

Service-product identity

The supplier designation is AE VAG92061B. Complete original installed identities, factory quantities and supersession history remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Product specification

The source gives nominal length 36.7 mm, internal diameter 8 mm and external diameter 12.08 mm. Its fitting-position markers identify intake and exhaust use. The values are product dimensions, not a prescribed interference fit, finished running clearance or machining limit.[1]

Full material grade, bore/outer tolerances, shoulder geometry, installation depth, stock packaging and original factory identities remain Unconfirmed.

Source applications

The manufacturer service listing associates this product with engine-code entries AEF. The Felicia application descriptions are supplier selections; their intervals do not establish national grades, steering side, individual build/VIN bounds or original installation.[1]

The AEF panel names Felicia I 1.9 D from October 1995 and Felicia II 1.9 D from October 1995 to June 2001. The Felicia II interval begins in October 1995 in this catalogue; it does not establish that the facelift entered production at that date.[1]

Recipient selection

Confirmation requires the actual cylinder-head and valve/guide revision, the selected stem and seat geometry, retention and spring interfaces, and any applicable engine-number restriction. This engine-family association does not certify every body, branded derivative or national equipment configuration. The exact head/valve/guide and original-part selector chain remains Unconfirmed.

The catalogue lists inlet selections with different nominal stem diameters. An AEF code alone cannot resolve those physical alternatives. AEF valve-component selection preserves that distinction.
