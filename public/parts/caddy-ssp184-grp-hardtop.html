Caddy Pickup manufacturer GRP hardtop specification

Volkswagen identifies an optional GRP enclosure for the open-platform Caddy Pickup. It is not identified as a Truckman S/C or a Fun hardtop.

Contents

  1. Enclosure and doors
  2. Body mounting
  3. Repair-service material
  4. Application and selection
  5. References

Enclosure and doors

The enclosure is a one-piece laminate; its symmetric rear wing doors use double-skinned laminate and open through 180 degrees. The glass is bonded into the doors. The glass-fibre reinforced plastic is stated as 2.5–6 mm thick. The laminate has an outer gelcoat and an inner protective coating.[1]

Body mounting

The manufacturer specifies eight M8 ×20 mounting bolts with PUR sealing compound and a 30 kg increase in unladen mass. The bolt grade, thread pitch, tightening torque, coating, mounting coordinates and complete bolt/shell/seal product identities remain Unconfirmed. These eight bolts are scoped to this enclosure joint, not the vehicle’s total fastener count.[1]

Repair-service material

The publication names plastic repair set D 006 600 for the laminate. This is a service material selection rather than an installed original component or a complete repair instruction.[1]

Application and selection

The source-described GRP option does not identify its selected engine, national order code, steering side or production/chassis bounds. Complete shell, hinges, locks, bonded glass and seal products remain Unconfirmed. No equivalence with the separately documented Truckman S or Truckman C is established.
