Volkswagen control-arm gaiter — 6U0 407 377

Volkswagen Classic Parts identifies 6U0 407 377 as a genuine service product. Its Caddy catalogue associations do not by themselves establish the part installed in an individual Felicia-derived Caddy or another Felicia body.[1]

This is a listed association, not evidence that all control-arm joints, gaiters or complete suspension arms interchange.[1][2]

Caddy applications

The product record lists Caddy Mk2 Pickup model years 1997–2001. A catalogue model-year interval is not an individual chassis or original-installation selection.[1]
