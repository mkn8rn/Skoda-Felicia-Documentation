Felicia Combi Czech 2/97 equipment classes

The four package panels in FCK 026 15 2/97 identify equipment classes and separately priced choices. These descriptions do not supply complete original products, factory option codes or build/VIN fitment limits.[1]

Common equipment

All four panels name an upper tailgate spoiler, a folding rear bench divided into one-seat and two-seat groups, a rear-window wiper, height-adjustable head-restraint equipment and immobiliser function. Head-restraint location and original assembly count are not identified by that description. Grouping does not identify the seat, hinge or latch products. The spoiler and wiper names do not specify materials, attachment geometry or original assembly numbers.[1]

Radio equipment

LX SPECIAL, LX SUPER and GLX STANDARD list radio preparation, an aerial and four speakers. GLX CLASSIC lists a stereo radio, aerial and four speakers. Preparation does not prove an installed radio. The speaker count does not specify individual products, door/luggage-space locations or wiring connections.[1]

Classic distinctions

The CLASSIC panel lists electrically adjustable/heated exterior mirrors and a standard set of four 13-inch alloy wheels. Set size and rim diameter do not identify original wheel numbers, finish, width, offset, bolts, tyres or spare-wheel selection.[1]

Security and lighting

LX SUPER, GLX STANDARD and GLX CLASSIC list central locking and front fog lamps. Those functions do not identify actuator, control-unit, lamp, switch or wiring products. No corresponding absence is assigned to LX SPECIAL.

Restraint choices

Each panel separately prices a driver airbag and a driver/passenger airbag choice. Module, control-unit, contact, dashboard and wiring products remain Unconfirmed. Exact pretensioner products/positions and other option combinations require their own selection; the unselected price entry does not establish restraint absence.[1]

ABS choice

All four package panels separately offer ABS. This identifies a selected anti-lock brake-control function, not the modulator, controller, sensors, rings, harness or option code. It does not identify EDS or a combined restraint selection.[2]

Tilting roof-opening choice

All four panels separately offer a tilting roof opening. Panel material, opening geometry, manual/electric operating drive, frame, seals, drainage and body-revision selection remain Unconfirmed. No common physical roof assembly is established by that function name.[2]
