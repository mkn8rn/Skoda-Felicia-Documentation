AE VAG96062B valve guide

AE VAG96062B is a distinct supplier service selection in the AE catalogue. Its service identity and supplier cross-references do not establish original factory installation or a supersession.

Service-product identity

The supplier designation is AE VAG96062B. Complete original installed identities, factory quantities and supersession history remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Product specification

The source gives nominal length 33 mm, internal diameter 7.02 mm and external diameter 15 mm. Its fitting-position markers identify intake and exhaust use. The values are product dimensions, not a prescribed interference fit, finished running clearance or machining limit.[1]

Full material grade, bore/outer tolerances, shoulder geometry, installation depth, stock packaging and original factory identities remain Unconfirmed.

Source applications

The manufacturer service listing associates this product with engine-code entries AEE. The Felicia application descriptions are supplier selections; their intervals do not establish national grades, steering side, individual build/VIN bounds or original installation.[1]

The AEE panel names Felicia I 1.6 from June 1997, Felicia I 1.6 LX and GLX from August 1995 to March 1998, and Felicia II 1.6 from January 1998 to June 2001. These are the supplier’s separately written application intervals, not a revised manufacturer introduction chronology.[1]
