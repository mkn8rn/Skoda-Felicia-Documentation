Felicia Blue Sky leaflet equipment

The July 1998 German importer leaflet describes its Blue Sky hatchback by a folding roof and common equipment classes. The priced programme distinguishes 1.6 L GLX, a version with alloy wheels and a version with ABS. The descriptions below concern that source-defined programme; they do not identify one interchangeable physical assembly or full original stock numbers.[1]

Application scope

The manufacturer illustration shows the five-door hatchback. Its common colour list is Nauticblau, Hot-Chilli-Rot-Metallic and Pazificgrün Metallic; the folding roof is black. Full colour codes, original option codes, body revisions and chassis/production boundaries are Unconfirmed. The separately priced equipment does not identify an unlisted combined ABS-and-alloy offer.[1]

Folding roof

Webasto supplies the electrically operated folding roof. The front description specifies push-button operation, and the colour note specifies black. Opening dimensions, roof cassette and panel identifiers, drive/switch products, operating limits and sealing or drainage products are Unconfirmed. This opening roof does not establish a full convertible body or a Fun rear-passenger canopy application.[1]

Body and cargo equipment

The programme lists body-coloured bumpers, painted electrically adjusted exterior mirrors, front fog lamps and a third brake lamp. Painted mirrors are not expressly assigned the body colour. Heat-insulating glazing is named without identifying each pane. The luggage area has lashing eyes; their count and original products are Unconfirmed.[1]

Cabin equipment

The audio unit is a Clarion radio/cassette combination, without a complete model or stock number. A tachometer has a digital-clock function; the source does not establish a separately replaceable clock product. Central locking and immobilisation are listed functions, without actuator, control or transponder identities.[1]

The rear bench is split, and seating has height adjustment without an express assignment of its adjustment to individual positions. The centre console contains drink holders. Their material, count, front/rear allocation, fastening and full moulding identifiers are Unconfirmed; plastic construction or a removable cup-holder insert is not established.[1]

Steering and braking selection

Power-assisted steering is common listed equipment; the leaflet does not specify its actuation architecture, pump, gear or hydraulic interfaces. The alloy-wheel offer confirms alloy rims without size, profile, offset or complete rim/tyre identities. The ABS offer confirms anti-lock equipment without hydraulic, sensor or controller products. Features unassigned to a priced offer remain Unconfirmed, rather than absent.[1]

The listed two-airbag package does not give individual installed module positions, restraint products or pretensioner selection. It is not a four-airbag Safe-line mapping and does not establish a full control or wiring assembly.[1]

Source-rated vehicle characteristics

The leaflet records a maximum speed of 170 km/h and a 0–100 km/h time of 12 seconds. It gives luggage capacity of 272 dm³ to the parcel-shelf level and up to 967 dm³ after folding the rear bench, with a 42-litre fuel tank. These programme figures do not identify a loose tank, shelf or body part, or separately measured values for each priced option.[1]

Fuel consumption is stated under Directive 93/116/EWG as 9.5 litres/100 km urban, 5.4 extra-urban and 6.9 combined. These are the leaflet’s test-cycle figures, not measured everyday fuel use. The source-rated engine description retains the displacement, power, torque and fuel rating separately from a complete engine-product identity.[1]
