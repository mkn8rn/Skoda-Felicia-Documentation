KYB K-Flex coil spring RA5445

RA5445 is a KYB K-Flex rear coil-spring service selection. The 2022 catalogue places it in the standard column for the applications below; this is a supplier product identity, not a Škoda original-equipment number.[1]

Physical selection

The family and engine-class entries do not identify a national trim, B/M/AEE/AEF engine product, steering side, factory suspension option or complete German type key. Actual seat and end geometry, load rating, spring/damper working travel, package and production application must match. Exact physical fitment and original installation remain Unconfirmed.

The hatchback and Combi standard rear selections are distinct supplier products. The shared heavy-duty entry does not establish interchangeability of those standard springs or equivalence to the manufacturer’s rough-road package.
