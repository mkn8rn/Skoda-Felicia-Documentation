Felicia — undated priced-leaflet equipment packages

The consulted leaflet distinguishes LX Special, LX Super, GLX Standard and GLX Classic. Its dated application, body approval and full component identities are Unconfirmed; the four written package descriptions remain separate from Combi or later programmes.[1]

Common described equipment

The legible columns describe driver-adjustable mirrors, an upper liftgate spoiler, reinforced doors, driver-seat height and headlamp adjustment, adjustable front head restraints, ashtrays, rear wipe/wash, an electrical outlet, a passenger visor mirror, a driver-operated liftgate release and a lights-on audible reminder. They specify three headliner grab handles with hooks at the rear; separate fixing and hook quantities are Unconfirmed.[1]

The audible reminder is a function, not proof of a separate buzzer product. The socket’s voltage and exact connector, mirror mechanism in non-electric columns, door/seat positions and individual hardware identities remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Package-specific equipment

Super additionally identifies silver decorative wheel covers, rear anti-roll equipment, a front-stabiliser entry with engine/ABS qualifiers whose precise application is Unconfirmed, power steering for 1.9 D and luggage illumination. It does not establish ABS presence in every offer.[1]

The legible Standard and Classic columns identify side protective mouldings, a rear bench with a one-seat/two-seat folding split, front fog lamps, central locking, a tachometer with digital clock and variable intermittent wiping. Classic explicitly adds electrically adjusted heated external mirrors. Unreadable lower rows do not establish equipment absence in another package.[1]

Application limits

The written power-unit/package combinations define offer selections. Full engine codes, transmission, original steering side, airbags/pretensioners, unassigned ABS options, exact body revision and production/VIN limits are Unconfirmed. A cover photograph of a hatchback is not a complete per-offer body or homologation record. Seller years are not manufacturer build cutoffs.[1]
