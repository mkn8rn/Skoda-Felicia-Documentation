Netherlands 1999 Felicia Fun wheel and tyre specification

Pon Mobiel’s February 1999 Fun price list specifies RONAL alloy wheels and Michelin tyres. It provides an assembly description rather than a complete wheel or tyre product identity.[1]

Wheel description

The wheel description is 5.5J × 13, painted to match the vehicle. No RONAL model, casting/stock number, complete Škoda identifier, offset, bolt circle, centre bore or fastening specification is supplied. The dimension does not establish physical identity with the wheel in a different national Fun offer.[1]

Tyre selection

Michelin is the named tyre supplier. The printed size notation is malformed and cannot establish a valid tyre designation without correction evidence. Exact size, tyre product, ratings and original quantity are unconfirmed; no corrected size is inferred from the UK Fun descriptions.[1]

Application

This wheel/tyre description applies to the standard equipment of the Pon Mobiel list effective 1 February 1999, including its independently priced options. The list provides no engine, build/VIN or rim-revision selector. It is not evidence of interchangeability, a service supersession or an ordinary Pickup wheel application.[1]
