The German-language genuine-accessory programme describes these separate product classes or variants. Similar function, photograph or family description does not establish a common physical product or interchangeability.

Stainless exhaust outlet trims

Stainless exhaust outlet trims — source-scoped class
PropertyDescription
Written abbreviated identity expressionFDC 071 001 – 4, 6, 7[1]
MaterialStainless steel[1]

Different vehicle types, without a number-to-recipient allocation. The abbreviated range is retained as printed. Individual outlet/finish/mounting variants are not merged into a physical identity; their separate recipient applications, dimensions and original installation are unconfirmed.[1]

Accessory high-frequency loudspeaker

Accessory high-frequency loudspeaker — source-scoped class
PropertyDescription
Offered classHigh-frequency loudspeaker[2]

Felicia-series audio programme. The complete order identity is unconfirmed because the digit/prefix line is not reliably legible in the consulted copy. Impedance, crossover, power, dimensions, attachment and original fitment are unconfirmed.[2]

Basic and expanded MPI trip-computer offerings

Basic and expanded MPI trip-computer offerings — source-scoped class
PropertyDescription
Basic functionsAverage speed, trip duration, outside temperature and battery voltage[3]
Expanded functionsAdditionally instantaneous/average/total fuel consumption, remaining range and fuel level[3]

MPI stated beside the offering; no engine/product allocation. Two function levels do not identify BEA 054 002/003/004/005, their physical calibration, wiring or interchangeability. Complete individual product identities and installed quantities are unconfirmed.[3]

Felicia-series programme. Complete identity, body/side application, geometry, fasteners and original fitment are unconfirmed.[4]
