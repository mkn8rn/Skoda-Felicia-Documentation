Felicia 1995 Czech-language technical-sheet mechanical specifications

The manufacturer hatchback sheet FC 03715 2/95 and Combi sheet FCK 02515 5/95 describe separate passenger-body programmes. Their published mechanical classes do not supply complete physical products or grade-specific original-parts selections.[1][2]

Transmission and clutch

Both sheets specify front-wheel drive and a fully synchronized five-speed manual gearbox with direct shift operation. The clutch has one dry driven disc, a diaphragm spring, asbestos-free lining and mechanical actuation. The wording does not identify a clutch cable, individual clutch products, gearbox code, ratios, final drive, shafts or mountings.[1][2]

Suspension

The front suspension is described as MacPherson struts with lower triangular arms. Front and rear use coil springs, internally fitted telescopic dampers and additional progressive polyurethane spring elements. The last description does not identify an individual bump-stop or auxiliary-spring product.[1][2]

The hatchback’s rear description explicitly names trailing arms and a torsion stabilizer. The Combi description names trailing arms without that additional stabilizer wording. This difference does not establish stabilizer absence in the Combi or physical interchangeability of the two rear assemblies. Rates, dimensions, part identifiers and loose-component quantities remain Unconfirmed.[1][2]

Brakes

The service brake has two hydraulic circuits split diagonally and vacuum assistance. Front disc brakes have single-piston floating calipers. Rear drums use two shoes and self-adjusting clearance. Both front and rear friction linings are described as asbestos-free; dimensions, compound identities, cylinders and original loose-part counts are not specified.[1][2]

The Combi sheet additionally specifies rear brake-pressure limiting. The hatchback row does not establish a corresponding regulator selection or its absence. Neither sheet identifies an ABS controller/modulator configuration or a grade-specific production cutoff.[2][1]

Steering

Both steering descriptions identify a rack-and-pinion arrangement and an articulated safety steering shaft. They do not identify a steering rack/column product, assistance selection, steering side or a service/repair limit.[1][2]

Wheels and tyres

Both sheets list 4½J×13 rims and 165/70R13 tyres. Offset, bead profile, mounting pattern, tyre load/speed designation, wheel and tyre manufacturers, finish, original quantities and spare-wheel selection remain Unconfirmed. These nominal descriptions do not select a particular approved alternative or aftermarket wheel.[1][2]

Charging and battery

The alternator entry is 14 V / 55 A and the starter-battery entry is 12 V / 44 Ah in both sheets. Manufacturer, full product/revision, terminal/case/mounting selection and original quantities remain Unconfirmed.[1][2]

Body geometry and luggage ratings

Both bodies are described as self-supporting steel passenger shells with five doors and five occupant places. This is a seating-capacity statement, not a count or identity of individual seats, panels or glazing.[1][2]

The hatchback sheet additionally specifies a 42-litre fuel-tank capacity and a drag coefficient of 0.346. Its 920/930 kg kerb-mass entry is a written pair, without a grade-specific mapping; 450 kg is the stated payload and 900/400 kg the braked/unbraked trailer rating. Those hatchback figures are not assigned to the Combi.[1]

Physical selection

The common 40/50 kW technical descriptions do not allocate every grade’s trim, safety equipment, options, steering side or national delivery. Publication dates are not manufacturing/parts boundaries. Complete engine/gearbox/body/electrical revisions, original identifiers/suffixes, quantities/positions and equipment/production/VIN selectors remain Unconfirmed.
