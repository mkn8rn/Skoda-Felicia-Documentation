MTX Felicia body and interior equipment

Country, Komfort, Sport and Family have distinct converter-equipment applications. An MTX designation does not identify a universal body kit, establish an original Škoda part number or prove that individually priced items were installed in every converted car.

Component selection

Establish the actual converter specification, donor trim, attachment, material and equipment revision. Front/rear drink holders, different seat trims, separate divider orientations and the different steering wheels remain distinct selections. A source-named brand or a nominal accessory count does not provide an exact physical product, installed quantity or interchangeability. Family’s pet-carrying luggage-space adaptation is not evidence for the Country plastic tray or any particular animal restraint.
