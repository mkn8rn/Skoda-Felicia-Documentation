Parts catalogues and diagrams

Parts catalogues and diagrams is an annotated index of external resources for identifying Felicia components. Diagrams, photographs, manuals, and catalogue tables remain with their publishers. Access conditions below were checked on 26 September 2026.

Illustrated catalogues

7zap

Independent catalogue service offering assembly illustrations and part-number research. Its current model selector lists separate Felicia, Felicia Facelift, and Pick Up entries. These are the provider’s catalogue labels and date ranges, rather than a production chronology.[1]

  • Felicia catalogue — labelled 1995–1997.
  • Felicia Facelift catalogue — labelled 1998–2001.
  • Pick Up catalogue — labelled 1996–2001.
  • Škoda model selector — alternative entry point if a model link changes.

Access: model landing pages were publicly readable. Assembly navigation uses the provider’s interactive interface. The service offers paid access to additional vehicle data; unrestricted access to every diagram, number, or VIN result has not been verified.[2]

partslink24

Commercial original-parts catalogue service operated by LexCom. Škoda’s own service site directs independent workshops to it for identifying and ordering genuine parts.[3] It is a route to manufacturer catalogue information; Felicia coverage inside a paid account has not been checked.

  • partslink24 access and registration
  • Škoda’s description of the service — French.

Access: login and paid access. Available information depends on the service and market; confirm the required model coverage before purchasing access.[4]

ASMET exhaust catalogue

Exhaust manufacturer’s application catalogue. Its Škoda PDF includes Felicia exhaust layouts alongside application and reference information. It is useful for locating exhaust sections and their associated fittings; it concerns ASMET’s product range, not every component on the car.[5]

  • ASMET Škoda catalogue — external PDF; Felicia layouts appear from printed page 8.
  • ASMET online catalogue — interactive manufacturer catalogue.

Access: public PDF and interactive catalogue. The PDF is hosted by ASMET and is not stored in this repository.

Parts listings

Skoda-Parts.com

Retailer’s Felicia catalogue with system categories, product listings, reference numbers, and application descriptions. The parts index links to relevant categories. Treat these as supplier listings; the site is not presented here as a factory exploded-diagram catalogue.[6]

  • Felicia parts catalogue

Access: public catalogue browsing. Product availability and supplier application claims require checking at the time of use.

Škoda Auto accessory catalogue

Manufacturer’s public shopping catalogue. Its vehicle filter includes Felicia (1994–2001). It is a supplementary source for accessories and car-care listings; it is not a complete illustrated service-parts catalogue.[7]

  • Official Škoda Auto catalogue — choose Felicia in the vehicle filter.

Access: public product browsing. Some features depend on region and dealer selection.

Using a diagram reference

A future part or repair article should identify the catalogue, vehicle selection, assembly title or diagram number, item callout, complete part number, and applicable notes. Cite the specific assembly URL when it is stable, together with an access date. Retain a model-selector link when the provider’s selection depends on a session.

For example, an article about an exhaust repair can link to fuel, intake, and exhaust and to ASMET’s resource description. The eventual article must cite the actual application entry it used. A link to this index alone is not evidence of a part’s compatibility.
