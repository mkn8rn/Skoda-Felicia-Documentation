Volkswagen diesel fuel-level gauge — 6U0 919 045 C

Volkswagen Classic Parts identifies 6U0 919 045 C as a genuine service product. Its Caddy catalogue associations do not by themselves establish the part installed in an individual Felicia-derived Caddy or another Felicia body.[1]

The catalogue size and mass fields do not define installed clearances, manufacturing tolerances or a weighing basis. The product description selects AEF. The instrument-board row adds a chassis range starting at 9U-Y-001 001, with an asterisk. The marker is retained without assigning an unseen footnote, calendar date or national-grade application.[1][2]

Caddy applications

The product record lists Caddy Mk2 Pickup model years 1997–2001. A catalogue model-year interval is not an individual chassis or original-installation selection.[1]

Blank engine, option or steering fields establish no unrestricted application. Parenthesised callouts and starred chassis notations are retained without inferring the missing annotation.

Physical selection

Confirm AEF cluster, sender characteristic, connector, instrument revision and chassis selection. Original manufacturing limits, full donor configuration, complete mating-part products and a factory supersession remain Unconfirmed. Caddy Pickup application is not automatically transferred to the Caddy van, Fun, ordinary Felicia Pickup, hatchback or Combi.
