MIKRA Felicia interior and insulation offerings

MIKRA describes separate interior, luggage, protection and sound-insulation offerings. The undated descriptive leaflet and June 1996 price list distinguish intended recipients, constructions and some sales units, without identifying a single common physical product or factory original part.
