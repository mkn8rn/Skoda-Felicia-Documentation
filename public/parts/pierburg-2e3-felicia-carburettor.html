Pierburg 2E3 carburettor for Felicia

Pierburg 2E3 is one of two carburettor types named in A-5528 supply variant 05/01 VF. The written type does not supply a complete stock number or calibration suffix.[1]

Contents

  1. Specification
  2. Application
  3. See also
  4. References

Specification

The variant specifies one double downdraught carburettor with two passages. Its outlet diameters are 28 and 30 mm; the corresponding venturi diameters are 20 and 24 mm, with ±0.25 mm recorded tolerance. Jetting, float level, choke operation, linkage, flange and complete calibration identity remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Application

The source application is A-5528 05/01 VF — Pierburg 2E3, valid from 1 June 1995. The alternative Jikov type is separately named; shared nominal passages do not establish identical assemblies or direct interchangeability. No road engine code, national grade or factory production/VIN range is assigned.
