SMI Felicia homologation hydraulic steering

The manufacturer names SMI hydraulic steering in A-5528 03/03 VO and A-5551 02/02 VO. A supplier name is not a complete steering-rack product identity.

Contents

  1. Architecture and selection
  2. Source applications
  3. References

Architecture and selection

The source specifies hydraulic steering under the named supplier family.[1][2] Pump drive, flow/pressure, rack travel, mounting spacing, tie rods, shaft spline, hoses, ratio and complete product numbers remain Unconfirmed. No interchangeability with an unassisted rack or another supplier is established.

Source applications

The applications are A-5528 03/03 VO and A-5551 02/02 VO. A competition option does not establish original steering fitment for a national road grade or factory VIN range.
