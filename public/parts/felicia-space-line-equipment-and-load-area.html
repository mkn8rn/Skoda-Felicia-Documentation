Felicia Space Line equipment and load area

The 9/2000 manufacturer sales programme describes an LX-derived Space Line exclusively as Combi. It names four engine offers and separates standard equipment from options.[1]

Full engine/gearbox products and calibrations remain Unconfirmed. MPI specifies a metering architecture without identifying injectors, rails or controllers. The diesel designation does not supply an injection-pump or complete engine code.

Standard equipment

The listed equipment includes immobilisation, a tachometer with digital clock, an audible lighting warning, cabin headlamp inclination control, driver-position liftgate release, adjustable front/rear head restraints and a luggage lamp. A driver airbag, bumper-integrated front fog lamps, radio preparation with four speakers, remote central locking and alarm, front electric windows, tinted glazing and split rear seating are included.[1]

The exterior description names body-colour mirrors, handles and lock rings, black B-pillar film, a decorative exhaust end and fourteen-inch light-alloy wheels. A centre console with a box, load lashing eyes and roof-carrying equipment are also described. Individual colour/trim/wheel products, console-box removability, glazing positions and rail/crossbar selection remain Unconfirmed. The programme offers current Felicia colours without a complete named palette.[1]

Intermediate floor, storage and bag

The load area has a removable intermediate floor, storage compartments and a removable folding bag bearing the edition name. The explanatory paragraph refers to two storage compartments; it does not establish two separate bag products. The floor is load-space equipment, distinct from the structural body floor. The bag is not identified with the separate shelf-mounted storage bag used in another programme.[1]

Physical floor/trim products, separate compartment assembly counts, bag quantity, material, dimensions, attachment and load limits remain Unconfirmed.

Tyre repair and inflation equipment

The standard equipment description includes a tyre-repair kit containing rubber-based sealing compound and a compressor. Exact kit/container/compressor products, compound volume and expiry, electrical supply, hoses, valves, operating limits and packaging quantities remain Unconfirmed. Its inclusion does not establish the absence of a spare wheel or a universal puncture-repair procedure.[1]

Optional equipment

The programme separately lists a front-passenger airbag, side airbags and written ABS or ABS+EDS choices. It gives no complete engine/option/hardware map for those braking alternatives. Power assistance is expressly offered for both 1.3 MPI outputs. Air conditioning is listed with an engine restriction; the 1.6 MPI 55 kW application is specified, while other output applications remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Other named choices are heated/electrically adjusted exterior mirrors, a tilting roof, headlamp washing and a roof box with attachment crossbars. The roof-box offer is restricted to Combi. Individual order identities, installation interfaces, compatibility between simultaneous options and build/serial limits remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Radio names and physical identity

The optional radio entry uses Škoda MS 201 and Škoda MS 401. Full stock numbers and physical equivalence to the separately described Grundig Music System 201 and 401 selections remain Unconfirmed. A matching model number does not establish identical hardware, connectors or a factory-installed radio.[1]

Physical selection

The leaflet’s date identifies the sales-support programme, not vehicle manufacture or a complete parts boundary. Engine/gearbox, steering side, body/trim/electrical revisions, original identifiers/suffixes, required quantities and option/build/VIN selection remain Unconfirmed. The driver-airbag description does not establish passenger/side-airbag absence or pretensioner equipment.
