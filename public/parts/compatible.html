Compatible parts

Compatible parts indexes evidenced alternatives for Felicia components: aftermarket replacements, parts shared with other vehicles, and parts used in documented conversions. Each entry links to the component’s canonical article, which records its identity, exact applications, and any installation conditions.

No alternative-part applications have yet been verified and published here. This is an index of subjects awaiting documentation, not evidence that alternatives do not exist.

Replacement parts

Aftermarket replacement articles remain unwritten. Entries will identify the manufacturer and complete product number, the original part being replaced, and the documented Felicia application.

Parts from other vehicles

Interchange articles remain unwritten. Entries will identify both the component and the donor application. A shared brand, similar appearance, or similar part number does not establish an interchangeable part.

Conversion parts

Conversion-part articles remain unwritten. A part requiring fabrication, wiring changes, or other modifications must state those requirements and link to the relevant documented upgrade. A reported conversion is not evidence of direct replacement compatibility.

Compatibility evidence

A compatibility entry states the exact part identifier, the original component or function it replaces, the evidence, and the relevant body, engine, gearbox, production or chassis, market, and equipment limits. Dimensions, connectors, mounting arrangements, and operating requirements are recorded where they determine fit or function. Unresolved applications are explicitly marked, and an unverified fit is not listed as compatible.

Application data belongs in the shared component article, rather than a second article for each model or category. A component already documented under Original parts uses that same article here when the evidence supports an additional application. Distinct products remain distinguishable by their manufacturer identifiers.
