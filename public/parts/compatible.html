Compatible parts

Compatible parts are alternatives to Felicia components, including aftermarket replacements and interchangeable parts from other vehicles. Conversion parts require modifications to the vehicle or its assemblies.

Replacement parts

Aftermarket replacements are products made to replace an original component in a specified vehicle application.

Parts from other vehicles

Interchangeable parts serve the same function in more than one vehicle application. A shared brand, similar appearance, or similar part number alone does not establish interchangeability.

Conversion parts

Conversion parts require fabrication, wiring changes, or other modifications as part of an upgrade or conversion. Their use in a modified vehicle does not establish direct replacement compatibility.

Fit and function

Compatibility depends on dimensions, mounting arrangements, connectors, and operating requirements, as well as the vehicle’s body, engine, gearbox, production or chassis range, market, and equipment. Physical fit alone does not establish correct operation.
