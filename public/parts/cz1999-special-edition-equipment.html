Czech 1999 Safe-line and Color Line equipment

Safe-line and Color Line I/II are distinct equipment applications. Their package descriptions do not identify a common physical kit, a complete original part number, or interchangeability between assemblies.

Color Line II inherits the source-described I equipment. Radio preparation comprises two loudspeakers in the hatchback or four in the Combi. The I electric-window offer instead combines front window operation with radio preparation. No original radio product is established by the preparation alone.[2]

Paint and upholstery

Color Line offers Fun yellow and, at extra cost, Laguna blue metallic. Each exterior colour may be combined with yellow, blue or Harlekin interior trim. The cloth is named Crowns; the Harlekin arrangement uses green, red, yellow and blue seat/door colours. The yellow-interior instrument display is described as yellow. Exact paint formulas, colour/trim codes, stitch patterns, individual seat assignments and upholstery product numbers are Unconfirmed.[2][3]

Physical selection

Package, body, engine/output, exterior/interior choice and fitted extra equipment affect physical selection. No leaflet establishes complete original assembly identities, gearbox product, steering side, wiring/colour codes or production/option/VIN cutoffs. Options are separately offered; a list is not proof of every combined installation.
