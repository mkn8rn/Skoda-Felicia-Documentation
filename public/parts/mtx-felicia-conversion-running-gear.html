MTX Felicia conversion running gear

Country, Komfort and Sport leaflets describe different damper, wheel, engine and transmission modifications. Similar dimensions or names do not establish common physical products or interchangeable assemblies.

Physical selection

The wheel/tyre entries give nominal dimensions, not full wheel identities, load/speed ratings, hub/bolt interfaces or approval for another vehicle. A stated power increase is not a final output without the specified donor baseline. Engine codes, calibrations, gearbox products, complete ratios and production/chassis conditions are Unconfirmed. Changed gears and protective bodywork do not establish a four-wheel-drive conversion. The Sport disc-brake description does not establish ABS installation or absence.
