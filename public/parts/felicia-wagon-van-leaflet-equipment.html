Felicia Wagon VAN leaflet equipment

The Italian September 1998 leaflet describes the two-seat N1 Felicia Wagon VAN LX 1.9 Diesel. Its named equipment and construction describe that offer; they do not identify complete original products, optional-equipment exclusions or factory build limits.[1]

Suspension, brakes and steering

The front suspension uses independent MacPherson struts, triangular lower arms and a stabilising bar. The rear description has interconnected arms and stabilisation. Both axles use coil springs and telescopic dampers. Supplementary polyurethane cushions or buffers have progressive action; that wording does not specify progressive coil springs or identify a particular front/rear bump-stop product.[1]

Service braking has two diagonally divided circuits with vacuum assistance. The front uses discs and a single-block caliper construction. The rear uses self-adjusting drums with two shoes per drum. The friction material is described as asbestos-free. Piston counts, brake diameters, shoe/pad products, total vehicle quantities and wear limits are not supplied.[1]

Steering is power assisted. The two-spoke steering wheel is 370 mm in diameter. The leaflet does not identify an assistance technology, pump, rack, wheel product or restraint module.[1]

Load area and body equipment

The load compartment has four securing eyes and automatic lighting. Its rear liftgate can be released from the driver position. The two-seat interior is pictured with a mesh cargo partition; the illustration does not specify the partition’s product, mesh material or approval limits. Roof rails, body-side protective mouldings and body-colour bumpers are listed.[1]

Two exterior mirrors can be adjusted from inside. Their control drive and side-specific products are not identified. The glazing is described as athermal, without individual pane or optical specifications; the rear window is heated.[1]

Cabin fittings and restraints

The centre console incorporates a preformed can-holder feature. The leaflet does not identify its material, number of recesses or complete moulding. Other fittings include a glove compartment, sun visors with a passenger-side vanity mirror, cloth-covered contoured door panels and cloth seats. The front backrests recline and the front head restraints adjust in height.[1]

The front seat belts are three-point and height adjustable. This description does not establish pretensioners, airbag equipment, belt products or steering-side allocation. Radio space is provided; it does not establish an installed receiver, aerial, speakers or their wiring.[1]

Instruments and electrical functions

The instruments include a speedometer, total/trip distance indication, coolant-temperature and fuel-level information, a tachometer and a digital clock. Dashboard and heating controls are illuminated. The listed acoustic warning concerns lights and doors, without specifying the complete trigger logic or sounder.[1]

The leaflet lists electronic engine-start immobilisation, front-door courtesy-light switches, two-speed windscreen wash/wipe with fixed intermittent operation, headlamp aim adjustment from the driver position, a third stop lamp and four-speed ventilation. The lighter and power connection descriptions do not establish two separate socket assemblies or an electrical rating. None of these functional descriptions identifies a complete controller, motor, key, sensor, bulb or circuit revision.[1]

Physical selection limits

A leaflet equipment class does not establish a shared physical product with another market, grade or body. The publication identifies neither full original identifiers/suffixes nor gearbox, steering-side, option or production/VIN selection. An unlisted feature is not proven absent.[1]
