Laureta body and special-equipment replacement requirements

A Laureta body or cabin component requires the actual donor chassis and converter specification. Shared Felicia ancestry, a body-form name or a photograph does not establish direct assembly interchangeability.

Recipient interfaces

Establish the exact chassis/body identity, reinforcement and joints, closures, seals, glazing and payload selection. Match engine/gearbox packaging, fuel/exhaust routing and suspension/brake interfaces. For cabin equipment, establish attachment loads, cabinet/drawer retention, aisle clearance and restraint anchors. Emergency lighting additionally requires its actual electrical circuit, controller, current demand, roof mounting and applicable vehicle approval.
