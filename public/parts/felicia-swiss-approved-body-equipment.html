Felicia Swiss approved body-equipment classes

Swiss approval descriptions identify body equipment within their own vehicle classes. Equipment classes do not establish one physical product shared by every Felicia.

Older petrol fuel tank

The 1S2509 hatchback and 1S2510 Combi forms each list one plastic fuel tank of 42 litres. Tank stock number, moulding, filler/vent interfaces, mounting and production selection are Unconfirmed. The same stated capacity and material do not identify equal tanks.[1][2]

Exterior mirrors

The older 1S2509/1S2510 and Fun 1SC313/1SC314 approvals describe exterior mirrors on the left and right. Adjustment, glass curvature, housing, steering-side variant, heater and individual original products remain Unconfirmed. A side designation alone is not a complete mirror selector.[1][2][3][4]

Older steering lock and wipers

The 1S2509 and 1S2510 forms specify a steering lock and two front windscreen wipers. They do not identify a combined ignition-lock assembly, key/barrel revisions, blade dimensions, motor, linkage, arms or current sales-unit quantities.[1][2]

Fun roof and covering

The 1SC313 and 1SC314 Fun approvals specify a fixed roof above the front seats and a covering above the rear seats on request. The rear covering’s material, canvas-roof equivalence, frame, catches, mounting, original product and installed quantity are Unconfirmed. Unassigned rear-cover fitment does not mean that a covering was absent.[3][4]

The same two approvals permit a plastic front protection-bar/bumper combination under ECE approval E8*26R-01*2292. That is an approval identifier, not a stock number or proof of installation on every Fun.[3][4]

Physical selection

Approval notation distinguishes manufacturer alternatives from equipment requested by the buyer. Neither notation resolves the complete original part, revision, market/steering application or individual chassis installation.[5]
