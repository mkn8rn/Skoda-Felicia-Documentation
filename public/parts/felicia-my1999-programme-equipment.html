Felicia model-year 1999 programme equipment

The contemporary report distinguishes the Felicia and Combi LX/GLX programme from the following Octavia programme. Named equipment classes do not establish individual original products.[1]

Standard wiping change

The standard wiper intermittent control gains one additional function. The report does not name that function, timing logic, memory operation or a physical relay/controller.[1]

LX option conditions

The expanded LX offer includes power assistance for the stated 1.3 / 50 kW engine, air conditioning, a tilt sunroof, 13-inch Borbet alloy wheels, and remote central locking with an alarm. The Combi LX separately offers a roof carrier. These are individually described options, without a complete joint-ordering or installation matrix.[1]

The report describes power assistance as standard for the written 1.6 / 55 kW and 1.9 / 47 kW engines. Those descriptions do not assign fuel-system/engine codes, individual steering products or a manufacturing boundary.[1]

Radio selections

The introduced optional choices are Grundig Music System 201 and Grundig Music System 401. Their full physical identities and recipient interfaces require individual selection.[1]

ABS and EDS

EDS is offered with ABS in the Felicia programme. The report gives no complete modulator/controller/sensor identity, calibration or body/grade/engine order exclusions; it does not establish ABS or EDS installation on an unspecified car.[1]

Selection limits

Beginning-August 1998 introduction describes the programme rather than an individual vehicle build, VIN cutoff or parts-change boundary. Equipment names do not determine a complete original product, quantity or optional-equipment absence. The separate Octavia dashboard, ISOFIX, side-airbag and other equipment descriptions do not apply to Felicia.[1]
