Felicia catalogue running-gear classes

The following classes are recorded in ADAC’s German catalogue and Quattroruote’s Italian archive. They identify architecture or nominal tyre markings, not one physical assembly, an original production number or interchangeability between vehicles.

Transmission

The inspected German and Italian entries specify a manually shifted five-speed gearbox and front-wheel drive. No gearbox product code, revision, individual gear ratios, differential or clutch identity is established.

Suspension

The German catalogue describes coil springs at both axles. Spring geometry, rate, colour marking, seat products and original quantities are not specified. A shared coil-spring description does not establish a shared spring.

Brakes

The German source classes are front disc brakes and rear drum brakes. Disc ventilation and dimensions, caliper and pad identities, drum diameter and rear hydraulic products are not supplied by those labels. ABS selection remains specific to the individual equipment entry.

Italian catalogue selections

Italian transmission class

The individual Italian records list a mechanically shifted five-speed transmission and front-wheel drive. Gearbox product codes, ratios, clutch assemblies, national approval variants and build/option limits remain Unconfirmed. A matching description does not establish physical interchangeability with the German catalogue or FIA assemblies.

Italian nominal tyre classes

The source lists nominal front and rear tyre markings. Its standard/optional labels are catalogue selections, not original tyre manufacturer/SKU, rim width/offset, valve, spare-wheel or national approval evidence. The load and speed symbols are retained as written; a tyre marking is not a complete wheel identity.

Source-scoped applications

The German applications in the four preceding German tyre classes also specify five-speed/front-drive, coil-spring and disc/drum architecture. The Italian tyre and transmission entries do not establish their spring or brake products. Catalogue periods do not identify production revisions or original replacement limits.

Physical selection

Original gearbox, spring, brake and tyre products, quantities, steering-side variations, body revisions and option/build/VIN limits are Unconfirmed. The catalogue’s stated standard equipment does not establish an unchanged original parts set. German approval alternatives are a separate source scope and do not silently select these tyre or rim products.
