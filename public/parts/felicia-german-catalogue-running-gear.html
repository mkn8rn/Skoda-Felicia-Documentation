Felicia German catalogue running-gear classes

The following classes are recorded in ADAC’s later German catalogue. They identify architecture or nominal tyre markings, not one physical assembly, an original production number or interchangeability between vehicles.

Contents

  2. Suspension

  4. Tyre classes
  5. Source-scoped applications

  7. References

Transmission

The inspected entries specify a manually shifted five-speed gearbox and front-wheel drive. No gearbox product code, revision, individual gear ratios, differential or clutch identity is established.

Suspension

The catalogue describes coil springs at both axles. Spring geometry, rate, colour marking, seat products and original quantities are not specified. A shared coil-spring description does not establish a shared spring.

Brakes

The source classes are front disc brakes and rear drum brakes. Disc ventilation and dimensions, caliper and pad identities, drum diameter and rear hydraulic products are not supplied by those labels. ABS selection remains specific to the individual equipment entry.

Source-scoped applications

Each listed tyre-class application is also a source-scoped five-speed/front-drive, coil-spring and disc/drum catalogue entry. Its model period does not identify production revisions or original replacement limits.

Physical selection

Original gearbox, spring, brake and tyre products, quantities, steering-side variations, body revisions and option/build/VIN limits are Unconfirmed. The catalogue’s stated standard equipment does not establish an unchanged original parts set. German approval alternatives are a separate source scope and do not silently select these tyre or rim products.
