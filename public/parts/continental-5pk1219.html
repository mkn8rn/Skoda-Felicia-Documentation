Continental 5PK1219 v-ribbed belt

Continental ContiTech identifies 5PK1219 as a v-ribbed belt in its 2012/2013 service programme. This is a supplier product identity, separate from a Škoda original factory part number.[1]

The code’s number is a reference length, not an outside circumference. The two dated profile illustrations give different height descriptions; neither is a product-specific manufacturing or wear tolerance. The general workbook’s elastomer descriptions do not identify this belt’s compound.[1][2]
