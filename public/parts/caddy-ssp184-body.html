Caddy Pickup July 1996 cab and load-platform assemblies

The manufacturer describes the two-seat commercial cab and its load-platform hardware; these are distinct from the Fun’s recreational cabin.

Shell and reinforcement

The cab has a double-skinned rear bulkhead. The doors contain steel reinforcement profiles at the window slot and impact tubes in their central region. These descriptions identify reinforcement classes, not stamped panel numbers or common Fun body assemblies.[1]

Cab lining and trim

A moulded headliner follows the roof contour and is retained in part by the sunvisors and grab handles. The firewall insulation is a foam mat with provision for equipment openings. A- and B-pillar trim is polypropylene. The floor carpet and sound-insulation layer form one removable cab-lining assembly.[1]

Service access hardware

The load-platform sidewalls have screwed sheet-metal access covers. A screwed cover in the load floor provides access to the tank’s fuel pump. The instrument-panel insert is fixed by five screws; the screw positions are covered by plugs, the left cover and air outlets. The source does not identify the cover dimensions or full fastener products.[1]

Load platform, spare wheel and glass

The open platform has a flat cover and a tailboard that folds level with the loading floor. Plastic capping protects its edges. The spare wheel is under the rear platform behind the axle, on a support bracket retained by a hex-headed bolt. Windscreen and cab rear glass are mounted in rubber profiles; the separate GRP hardtop door glass is bonded.[1][2]

Corrosion-protection classes

The described shell treatment combines zinc-phosphate preparation, cathodic electrodeposition primer, PVC seam/fold sealing and underbody protection, followed by filler and topcoat. The completed paint system is stated as at least 75 μm thick. Risk-dependent panels receive one- or two-sided zinc coating, with plastic wheel-arch protection in exposed areas. The specification does not identify a universal coating thickness or material treatment for every replacement panel.[1]

Optional front-airbag positions

The manufacturer distinguishes optional driver-only airbags from paired driver/front-passenger airbags. It does not identify modules, control unit, loom, steering-wheel contact assembly, pretensioner selection or side-airbag application for these options.[2]

Towing attachment

A towing attachment is named as optional equipment. Complete towbar/ball/coupling, body reinforcement, electrical connector/loom, approved load and order-code selection remain Unconfirmed.[2]

Body selection

The applications are the July 1996 technical selections. Complete panel, trim, cover, glass, seat, restraint and fastener identities; material thicknesses and attachment drawings; exact national/steering/equipment/build application remain Unconfirmed.
