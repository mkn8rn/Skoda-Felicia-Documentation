Volkswagen rear-axle hexagon bolt — N 010 258 4

Volkswagen Classic Parts identifies N 010 258 4 as rear-axle hexagon bolt, with quality class Genuine Volkswagen Quality. Its current service associations do not establish historical original installation in a particular Felicia-derived Caddy.[1]
