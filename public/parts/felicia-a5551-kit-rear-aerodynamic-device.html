Felicia A-5551 kit rear aerodynamic device

The A-5551 10/02 ER amendment identifies the rear aerodynamic device referenced in 04/01 VK section 9/XVII.

Contents

  1. Application conditions
  2. Physical selection
  3. References

Application conditions

From 1 January 1999, the amendment permits that rear device without requiring the other kit modifications and permits combination with individual VO/VK modifications. This is a specific homologation permission, not evidence that every kit-equipped car used the device or that it is road-legal.[1]

Physical selection

Complete device/attachment identities, material, dimensions, fasteners, incidence and quantity remain Unconfirmed. The independently permitted device selection does not identify its engine, gearbox or other optional competition equipment.
