Control interfaces

The drawing distinguishes programming/diagnostic and pressure-test connectors. The software selection is Felicia_13_98_5wp4_G_002.s19. Its depicted supply branches use fuses marked maximum 7.5 A; that marking is not the controller’s operating-current rating.[1]
