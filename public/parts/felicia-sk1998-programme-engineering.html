Powertrain characteristics

1.3 MPI 40 kW

The four-cylinder petrol engine uses multipoint injection. Its displacement is 1,289 cm³, maximum rated output 40 kW at 4,500 rpm and maximum torque 99 Nm at 2500 rpm. The valve gear is OHV. The stated fuel is RON 91. No engine product code or assembly/calibration identity is supplied.[1]

1.3 MPI 50 kW

The four-cylinder petrol engine uses multipoint injection. Its displacement is 1,289 cm³, maximum rated output 50 kW at 5,000 rpm and maximum torque 106 Nm at 2600 rpm. The valve gear is OHV. The stated fuel is RON 95. No engine product code or assembly/calibration identity is supplied.[1]

1.6 MPI 55 kW

The four-cylinder petrol engine uses multipoint injection. Its displacement is 1,598 cm³, maximum rated output 55 kW at 4,500 rpm and maximum torque 135 Nm at 3500 rpm. The valve gear is OHC. The stated fuel is RON 95. No engine product code or assembly/calibration identity is supplied.[1]

1.9 D 47 kW

The four-cylinder diesel uses indirect injection. Its displacement is 1,896 cm³, maximum rated output 47 kW at 4,300 rpm and maximum torque 124 Nm at 2500–3200 rpm. The valve gear is OHC. The stated fuel is Diesel. No engine product code or assembly/calibration identity is supplied.[1]

Electrical ratings

Each technical column records a 14 V, 70 A alternator. The three petrol columns specify a 12 V, 44 Ah battery; the diesel column specifies 12 V, 61 Ah. Case size, cold-start standard, terminal layout, alternator product/pulley and original physical identities are Unconfirmed.[1]

Running gear and body construction

The transmission is a fully synchronised five-speed manual with final drive. The clutch is mechanically operated, single-plate and diaphragm-sprung with an asbestos-free lining. These descriptions do not identify gearbox, clutch or actuation product numbers, ratios or service clearances.[1]

Front suspension uses MacPherson struts and lower triangular arms. The 1.6 MPI and 1.9 D columns additionally specify a front stabiliser. The rear axle uses trailing arms. Its description mentions a torsion stabiliser for Felicia without a separate body/engine allocation; the exact passenger-body and production application is unconfirmed. Coil springs and telescopic damping are described for the running gear. The steering is rack-and-pinion with a collapsible steering-column shaft; assistance is specified for the 1.6 MPI and 1.9 D. Exact hydraulic units, arm construction and production/revision selections are Unconfirmed.[1]

The passenger bodies are self-supporting steel, five-door and five-seat. The service brakes use two hydraulic circuits with diagonal separation and vacuum assistance. Front brakes are discs with single-piston floating calipers; rear brakes are self-adjusting drums with asbestos-free linings. No ABS, airbag or other offered extra is inferred from these base technical descriptions.[1]

Factory-option conditions

The programme separately offers a driver airbag with belt pretensioners, paired driver/passenger airbags with belt pretensioners, and ABS across the printed grades. A tilting roof is offered on LX special and GLX in either body. These rows establish individual equipment selections; they do not establish every combination of them or the original mechanism/controller products.[2]
