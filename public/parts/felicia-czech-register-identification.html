Felicia — Czech register identification fields

Czech register fields distinguish the declared type, engine, body and national approval from a commercial model name. A current registered description does not establish an unaltered factory configuration.[1]

Vehicle and engine selectors

Register fields used for Felicia recognition
FieldMeaning and selection limit
Type / variant / versionSeparately recorded identity fields; a commercial name does not fill an empty variant or version.[1]
Type approval / EC approvalAdministrative approval identities; they do not supply physical part numbers, production dates or original option fitment.[1]
Engine type / maximum outputEngine designation and rated-output fields. Full gearbox, calibration and component-product identities require separate evidence.[1]
Body / seating capacityRegistered body description and capacity; not a count of loose panels, seat frames, belts or attachment parts.[1]

Missing values and applicability

The publisher states that blank fields have no value available in the register. A blank entry therefore does not prove absence of a component, accessory or approval. Zero placeholders require field-specific interpretation; they are not automatically physical counts or engineering limits. First-registration dates and current registered colour/equipment remain separate from manufacturing and original installation. Later alterations and class-wide production, option or chassis application require their own evidence.[1]
