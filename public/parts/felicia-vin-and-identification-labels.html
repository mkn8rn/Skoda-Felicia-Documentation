Felicia VIN and identification labels

The stamped vehicle identification number, vehicle data label and type plate carry different kinds of identification. The VIN records the shell’s serial identity and coded classifications; the data label adds engine, gearbox, paint, interior and equipment information. The type plate records permitted masses.[1]

Stamping and label locations

The VIN is stamped on the damper-support web in the engine compartment. Škoda’s 2021 statement identifies the right damper-support web when discussing original and replacement VIN markings on Favorit/Felicia and derived variants.[1][2]

Felicia identification-label locations
IdentificationSource-defined location
Felicia and Felicia Combi vehicle data labelUnder the luggage-compartment carpet, to the left of the spare wheel[1]
Felicia Vanplus vehicle data labelOn the floor above the fuel tank[1]
Felicia Pickup vehicle data labelOn the pillar behind the driver, above the ventilation grille[1]
Type plateOn the right side of the front wall of the engine compartment[1]

The VIN legend identifies an engine/transmission class rather than a complete gearbox product. The gearbox code is separately recorded on the vehicle data label.[1]

The engine/gearing legend also pairs E/135 and F/136 with the printed decimal 0.24, G/AEE with 0.26 and H/AEF with 0.30. These are the source’s combined-class notations, not full gearbox product codes or separately identified gear-stage specifications.[1]

Applying the cited field legend, a fixed seventh character 6 selects the body-preparation class designated Z, prepared for airbags. It does not establish fitted airbags, pretensioners, ABS, a physical shell part number or a body production revision. A dot at that position establishes neither prepared nor unprepared construction.[1]

The Pickup record 3SH101 fixes version character 3, the legend’s broad LX designation. That does not define a national equipment package, replace the approval’s engine identification or exclude an independently approved load-platform superstructure.[13][1] The Fun prefixes fix only TMBE; they do not establish a Fun-specific eighth-character code, body-preparation state or version.[11][12] All these records leave the model-year and plant positions unspecified.

The 1SC304 record locates the VIN in the engine compartment at the right damper housing/support.[6] This is vehicle-identification evidence, not a stamping or re-stamping procedure. Compare the actual marking and separate data-label fields before selecting a physical body, engine, gearbox or option application.

The version, year and plant values are the entries in the 2015 legend. They do not prove every combination was manufactured, and its year list does not give 2000–2001 codes. A version label alone does not select market equipment, gearbox, steering side or an original component.[1]

Vehicle data label

The data label records the VIN, vehicle type and model name, engine output, engine code and number, gearbox code, paint/interior designation and optional-equipment codes. Those fields distinguish information that a broad body name or unsuffixed engine-class letter cannot supply.[1]

A recorded option code still requires its applicable equipment definition and production range before it selects physical components. The illustrated label is not an equipment-code dictionary or a complete original-parts inventory.

Type plate

The type plate carries the VIN and the permitted gross vehicle, braked combination, front-axle, rear-axle and unbraked-trailer masses in the cited illustration. These limits are vehicle-specific; the example numbers are not ratings for all Felicias.[1]

Factory-corrected VIN markings

Škoda’s 2021 statement notes that some older Felicias may have had their VIN corrected during original production: the original stamped characters were overstruck with X marks and the corrected VIN was stamped elsewhere in the engine compartment. That possibility does not, by itself, establish the provenance of a correction on an individual car or select its fitted parts.[2]

Federation fleet class observations

The November 2007 Federation government fleet decision records TMBEGF613Y0......, TMBEHH613Y5......, TMBEHH61315...... and TMBEEA61315....... Their final six dots omit body serials, not unknown equipment codes or a manufacturing range. Reported production years do not substitute for a model-year decoder, and the record does not supply vehicle data-label or original-part values.[18]
