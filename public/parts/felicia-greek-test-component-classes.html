Felicia Greek period-test component classes

These component classes belong to the two GLXi 1.6 passenger cars tested by 4Troxoi in June 1996. A common description or nominal rating does not identify one physical product or establish fitment to every Greek GLXi, the priced LXi or the separately described Pickup.[1]

Clutch

The paired technical description specifies a mechanically operated single-plate clutch. Driven-plate diameter, spline, cover/release mechanism and original product identities are Unconfirmed.[1]

Brakes and steering

Both tested cars have ABS, front disc/rear drum brakes and a mechanically operated rear parking brake. Steering is rack-and-pinion without hydraulic assistance. The report does not select the ABS version, hydraulic modulator, controller, disc/drum dimensions or exact rack product.[1]

Wheels and tyres

The paired specification gives nominal rims of 5.5 J × 13 and road/spare tyre sizes of 165/70 R13. Offset, complete rim number, tyre manufacturer and load/speed symbols are Unconfirmed. The nominal spare size does not identify its rim or storage hardware.[1]

Electrical ratings

The source records a 12 V, 44 Ah battery and a 55 A charging generator. Capacity/current measurement conditions, battery case/terminals, cold-cranking rating, generator manufacturer, pulley and complete products are Unconfirmed. These ratings do not select a supplier service product.[1]

Greek 1995 GLXi running gear

The GLXi test describes five-speed front-wheel-drive transmission, front MacPherson struts with triangular lower arms, a semi-rigid rear axle with trailing arms and a rear anti-roll member, unassisted rack steering and front disc/rear drum brakes. Numerical gearbox/rack/brake geometry, exact clutch and suspension products, original quantities and factory selection limits are Unconfirmed.[2]

  • Škoda Felicia GLXi 1.3 — Greek 1995 tests

Greek summer 1998 GLX running gear

The comparison identifies front MacPherson struts, a semi-rigid rear axle with trailing arms, rack steering, front disc/rear drum brakes and fitted light-alloy road wheels. The stated rack travel is three turns from lock to lock; its assistance variant and original product are Unconfirmed. Air conditioning and electric windows are observed fitted equipment, without a specific compressor, regulator, switch or factory option identity. Airbag/ABS option pricing does not establish actual test-car fitment.[3]

  • Škoda Felicia GLX 1.3 — Greek summer 1998 test

Greek November 1999 fitted systems

The test explicitly describes hydraulic steering assistance, ABS, a driver airbag, air conditioning, audio, central locking, front electric windows and electric mirrors. It does not identify steering-rack/pump, ABS modulator/controller, restraint-controller/calibration, compressor, window/lock mechanisms or original equipment products. A named system is not proof of a particular component identity, quantity, supersession or factory fitment beyond the described test car.[4]
