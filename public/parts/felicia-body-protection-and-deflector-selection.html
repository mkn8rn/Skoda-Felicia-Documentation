Felicia body protection and wind-deflector selection

Select the actual door, glazing, sill, wheel arch, rear closure or roof edge before choosing protective trim. A deflector needs window/regulator clearance and its own retention. Spoilers and mouldings need body/paint/attachment selection; a brake-lamp spoiler also needs the correct circuit and approval. Decorative or reflective appearance is not proof of a glazing, photometric, structural or road-use approval.

Recipient requirements

Identify the complete recipient component, actual body/engine/equipment and applicable production/approval limits. Matching appearance or a nominal size does not establish fit. A catalogue accessory application is distinct from historical original installation, a direct service replacement and a conversion.
