Felicia timing-drive replacement

A replacement timing-drive assembly must match the actual engine, drive layout, pulley geometry and tensioning equipment. A supplier application is not proof of original factory installation.[1]

Recipient identification

Identify the actual engine and timing architecture before selecting a camshaft-drive belt or kit. The AEE and AEF supplier applications are separate; grouped 1.3 accessory-belt entries do not select a timing belt. An AEE/ALM note in the supplier table is not proof of a Felicia ALM factory configuration.[1]

Installation requirements

Recipient conditions
Interface / conditionRequired selection
Drive geometryActual pulley/sprocket profile, groove or tooth geometry, alignment and routing
TensioningInstalled adjustment method, tensioner and any guide pulleys; specified tension and procedure
Equipment and mountingActual accessory or timing members, brackets, fasteners, covers and clearance
Production selectionEngine/assembly revision, steering side, equipment/options and any verified build/VIN limits

Equal tooth count alone does not make belts interchangeable. Kit membership does not prove complete mounting hardware, original vehicle quantities or the fitted pump and pulley revision. The installation procedure, timing alignment, tension and any pump sealing requirements must be specific to the actual assembly.[1]
