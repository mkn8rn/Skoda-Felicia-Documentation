Fischer 573-901 rubber exhaust hanger

Fischer 573-901 is a rubber exhaust hanger identified in the supplier’s Felicia service application catalogue.[1] It is a service-product identity; the corresponding original factory-installed component remains Unconfirmed.

Selection conditions

Use the component at the joint or support shown for the matching exhaust layout. Check the installed sealing faces, pipe size or hanger connection before substitution. A nominal clamp diameter or a ring dimension alone does not establish fitment to a different joint.
