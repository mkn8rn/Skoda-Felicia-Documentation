Felicia Pickup load-cover and superstructure classes

ADAC separates covered ordinary commercial Pickup entries from the Fun and Vanplus derivatives. A class name does not identify a shared physical cover, shell or mounting kit.

Flat load-bed cover

The flat-cover designation describes a cover over the load platform, without identifying its textile, panel material, fastening system or sealing interfaces.

  • Felicia Pickup LX Flachplane 1.3 40 kW — 07/99–02/01[1]
  • Felicia Pickup LX Flachplane 1.3 50 kW — 07/99–02/01[3]
  • Felicia Pickup LX Flachplane 1.9 D 47 kW — 07/99–02/01[5]

Load-platform superstructure

The superstructure designation identifies a body fitted above the load platform. The catalogue label does not identify a manufacturer, rigid or flexible construction, door/glass layout, mounting kit or original-product code.

  • Felicia Pickup LX Aufbau 1.3 40 kW — 07/99–02/01[2]
  • Felicia Pickup LX Aufbau 1.3 50 kW — 07/99–02/01[4]
  • Felicia Pickup LX Aufbau 1.9 D 47 kW — 07/99–02/01[6]

Physical selection

Cover/superstructure products, installed quantities, body and mounting revisions, factory-versus-accessory supply and build/option/VIN applicability remain Unconfirmed. These descriptions do not establish interchangeability with Fun canopies or the enclosed Vanplus shell.

Swiss approved superstructure classes

Swiss ordinary Pickup approvals distinguish additional load-platform bodies. The diesel record names AKORD for a metal superstructure and AIR DESIGN/TILLER for two plastic alternatives. The petrol records describe metal/plastic alternatives without a manufacturer name; 3SH103 expressly calls them removable. These names/materials do not identify exact shells, glazing, hinges, mounting kits or stock numbers.
