Genua and Perfekt equipment in the May 1999 Polish launch report

The contemporary report describes distinct Genua and Perfekt offerings. Named equipment and displacement classes do not identify complete original products or every manufacturing selection.[1]

Written engine selections

Genua is reported with 1.3- and 1.6-litre engines without output, fuel-system or code identification. Perfekt likewise offers those two capacities; its 1.6 entry is rated 55 kW. The original 1.3 Perfekt entry is printed as 59 kW, but the report does not establish a corresponding factory engine/calibration or resolve that rating. Exact 1.3 rating and all full engine/gearbox products remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Genua equipment

The listed Genua equipment includes alloy wheels, a sport leather Momo steering wheel, leather gear-lever and parking-brake grips, and a Blaupunkt radio prepared for Škoda. It also lists fog lights, heat-reducing glazing, a nickel-plated exhaust tip, black pillar foil and a withdrawable centre console. The sole reported colour is yellow, described as dynamic yellow. Wheel dimensions, steering-wheel/radio models, glazing/unit allocations, foil positions and stock/paint identifiers remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Perfekt equipment

The reported standard safety equipment is ABS, a driver front airbag, a passenger front airbag, two side airbags and pyrotechnic belt pretensioners. Fog-light equipment and a belt-fastening warning are listed without individual unit/controller or warning-position identification.[1]

Comfort equipment includes central locking and a remotely operated alarm; the report does not separately establish remote control of the locks. Broad body-side protection strips, rubber bumper-protection strips, full wheel covers, luggage-securing attachments and a centre console with a withdrawable box are also listed. Red and silver are the two reported paint choices. Full products, pretensioner locations and original loose-part quantity/position selection remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Body and physical selection

A Škoda-credited photograph depicts a five-door hatchback marked Genua. It is not labelled with its engine, order code or a complete body/engine allocation. The report does not supply a per-engine body/approval map for either edition. Fuel systems, grade/body revisions, actual steering/delivery selection, controllers and manufacturing/chassis limits remain Unconfirmed.
