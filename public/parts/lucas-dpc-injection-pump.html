Lucas DPC injection pump

The Felicia 1.9 D 47 kW specification in the ŠKODA AUTO-authorised technical publication identifies a Lucas DPC distributor injection pump. The data are stated to apply to February 1997. This is a pump-family identification; it is not a complete pump product or calibration number.[1]

  3. See also
  4. References

Engine application

The recorded engine is a four-cylinder, 1,896 cm³ diesel using indirect injection. A distributor pump meters and routes injection deliveries to the cylinders, while the injector holders and nozzles are separate components. The pump family therefore does not identify the nozzle-and-holder assemblies or their service replacements.[1]

Physical selection

Pump suffix, calibration, mounting revision, drive interface and electrical/cold-start connections must distinguish a physical replacement. Those values are Unconfirmed in this family identification. A common DPC designation alone does not establish a direct replacement or the original pump fitted to a particular car.
