Caddy Pickup petrol and diesel heater assemblies

Volkswagen describes different heater architectures for its petrol and diesel Caddy Pickup selections.

Contents

  1. Petrol heater
  2. Diesel heater
  3. Petrol air-conditioning option
  4. Applications and physical selection
  5. References

Petrol heater

The petrol heater has a four-speed blower and a separate fresh-air shutoff flap. Coolant circulates through its heater matrix continuously. The linked temperature flaps control air through the matrix; the blower operates only with the fresh-air shutoff fully open. Rotary controls actuate the flap mechanisms through cables.[1]

Diesel heater

The diesel heater has a three-speed blower. A coolant-control valve, linked to the temperature-flap mechanism, regulates coolant flow through the matrix. There is no separate fresh-air shutoff flap: the distribution flaps perform the air-shutoff function. The rotary controls and flap drive use cables.[1]

Petrol air-conditioning option

The manually controlled petrol option uses R134a and provides fresh-air and recirculation operation. Pushbuttons sit between the rotary controls. The recirculation mechanism is vacuum-operated: engine vacuum from the injection unit feeds a reservoir behind the battery, and a pneumatic actuator moves the linked fresh-air and recirculation flaps. Recirculation can operate independently of refrigeration.[2]

The option’s electrical package names compressor/fan fuses and the J32 air-conditioning and J101 second-stage fan relay functions. Full relay and HVAC product identities, refrigerant charge, oil specification, compressor displacement, hose interfaces and option/build selection remain Unconfirmed.[2]
