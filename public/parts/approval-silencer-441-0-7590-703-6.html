Silencer approval marking 441.0.7590-703.6

441.0.7590-703.6 is recorded as an identifying mark for a silencer in the cited Swiss Felicia approvals. The mark identifies the approved description; it does not by itself establish a manufacturer stock number, service supersession or unchanged physical design.[1]

Identification

The exact recognition string is 441.0.7590-703.6. Manufacturer, complete factory catalogue identity/suffix and documented aliases are Unconfirmed. The approval count prefix is separate from the identifying mark.[2]

Specification

Dimensions, pipe/connection layout, mounting, material, internal construction and intrinsic acoustic characteristics are Unconfirmed. The approval describes an exhaust component, not a current service sales unit.

Physical selection

The records establish approval descriptions and component counts, rather than a full original parts catalogue. A common recognition mark does not prove interchangeable joints, body routing, production revision or calibration. Alternative approval markings do not establish which unit a particular chassis received.[2]
