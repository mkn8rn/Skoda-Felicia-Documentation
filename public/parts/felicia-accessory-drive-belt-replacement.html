Felicia accessory-drive belt replacement

A replacement accessory-drive belt must match the actual engine, drive layout, pulley geometry and tensioning equipment. A supplier application is not proof of original factory installation.[1]

Recipient identification

Identify the driven accessories, including the alternator, power-steering pump and air-conditioning compressor, and whether each is present. Continental’s Felicia entries distinguish equipment combinations, but their source I/II labels and intervals do not identify a national grade, exact mounting revision or factory VIN split.[1]

Installation requirements

Recipient conditions
Interface / conditionRequired selection
Drive geometryActual pulley/sprocket profile, groove or tooth geometry, alignment and routing
TensioningInstalled adjustment method, tensioner and any guide pulleys; specified tension and procedure
Equipment and mountingActual accessory or timing members, brackets, fasteners, covers and clearance
Production selectionEngine/assembly revision, steering side, equipment/options and any verified build/VIN limits

Compare the existing belt marking and pulley profile using the same length basis; a nominal reference length is not an outside-circumference measurement. Matching a written size does not establish compound, tensioning method or installation.[2] The institute’s source-written PK belt request does not identify a manufacturer or select its exact drive layout.[3]
