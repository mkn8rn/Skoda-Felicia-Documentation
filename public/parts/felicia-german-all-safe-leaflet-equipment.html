German Felicia All Safe leaflet equipment

The September 1999 manufacturer leaflet names three All Safe Combi offers. Its programme equipment is separate from a complete physical engine, trim or original-parts selection.[1]

Manufacturer offer identification

September 1999 leaflet — written All Safe Combi offers
Written offerCapacity and output
Felicia All Safe Combi, LX1.3 litres / 50 kW[1]
Felicia All Safe Combi, LXi 1.61.6 litres / 55 kW[1]
Felicia All Safe Combi, LX 1.9D1.9 litres / 47 kW[1]

The leaflet writes LX for the 1.3 and 1.9 D offers and LXi 1.6 for the 1.6 offer. These written grades do not assign fuel-metering hardware, a complete engine code or every country’s LX/LXi equipment.

Restraints and braking

The common equipment list names driver and front-passenger airbags, front side airbags and ABS. It does not identify restraint modules/controllers, pretensioners, side-module count or mounting positions, brake hardware or option/build limits.[1]

Seating and load equipment

A split rear bench, manual front-seat height adjustment, roof rails and load-compartment lashing eyes are listed. Height-adjustment architecture, exact seat positions/products, rail geometry and eye quantities remain Unconfirmed. Manual height adjustment does not identify a particular handle, linkage or electric mechanism.[1]

Security and electric windows

The leaflet independently lists remote-controlled central locking and a remote-operated alarm. The interior text describes controls for the electric front windows. Lock, alarm, remote, regulator and switch products and electrical interfaces remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Exterior and console equipment

A centre console, body-colour exterior mirrors and front fog lamps are listed. Mirror heating or electric adjustment is not specified by this description. The current Felicia colour range is offered, with an extra charge for metallic paint; individual colour codes and a complete palette are not supplied.[1]

Optional radio

The airbag/interior illustration labels its radio as extra equipment. It does not establish a standard installed radio or a complete radio product, revision, loom, aerial or speaker selection.[1]

Physical selection

The imprint VF 37-900/09/99 identifies publication scope. The matching body/capacity/output descriptions in the later German catalogue do not establish an unchanged as-built installation throughout its model period. Full original identifiers/suffixes, quantities, gearbox, steering side, physical revisions and option/production/VIN selection remain Unconfirmed.
