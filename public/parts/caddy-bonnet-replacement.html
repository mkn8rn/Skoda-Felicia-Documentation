Caddy bonnet replacement requirements

The replacement bonnet must match the actual Caddy front-body revision, aperture, hinges, latch, seals and engine-bay clearance.

Recipient requirements

Recipient body

Identify the Felicia-derived Caddy Pickup and its front body. Neither a Caddy van nor a Felicia facelift body is interchangeable solely because a part number has a similar prefix.

Closure interfaces

Establish hinge points, latch engagement, seal path, panel clearances and the actual mating hardware. Catalogue size fields do not define those installed interfaces.
