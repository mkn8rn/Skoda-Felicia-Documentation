Genuine service sealant D 176 001 A3

D 176 001 A3 is a complete sealing-material service identity specified for Felicia front-wing repair in the authorised technical publication. A later Audi manufacturer bulletin describes that same written identity as a silicone adhesive. Neither application establishes its use as the Felicia’s original factory-applied sealer.[1][2]

Material and application selection

The Felicia repair application concerns a prepared body-panel joint. It does not identify a wing panel, hardware kit, original factory sealer, application amount or a complete car’s installed sealant quantity. The Audi use provides a material description without extending its recipient assembly to the Felicia.[1][2]

Front-wing joint sealing requirements concern substrate, preparation and joint selection. A similarly described sealant requires independently established material and process compatibility; a common silicone description alone does not confirm substitution.
