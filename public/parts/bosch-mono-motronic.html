Bosch Mono-Motronic injection

Bosch Mono-Motronic is the single-point petrol-injection family associated with Felicia engine codes 135B and 136B in the authorised technical specification for February 1997. Both codes use this family; their different engine outputs do not establish an identical controller calibration.[1]

Metering and control

The original A-5528 form records electronic fuel metering, one effective fuel outlet and injection into the intake manifold. Its throttle passage is 36 ± 0.25 mm. These dimensions belong to that homologation record, which does not identify the engine by a 135B or 136B code.[2]

The recorded control inputs are a Hall rotation signal, coolant temperature, intake-air temperature, throttle position and exhaust oxygen. The form identifies an electronic controller, the injection unit and its injection valve. Those entries distinguish the single-point system from a fuel rail supplying multiple manifold injectors.[2]

Physical identification

The system name identifies an architecture, rather than one controller, injector or throttle-body stock number. Original assembly numbers, revision suffixes, injector flow, fuel pressure, wiring pinout and calibration identity are Unconfirmed for these recorded applications. The homologation-validity date is not a road-car production or VIN limit.
