Felicia starting-battery specification classes

The November 1997 Russian-market account distinguishes a battery capacity for official deliveries from an unnamed base comparison. These are source-defined specification classes; a capacity does not identify one physical battery product.[1]

The reported package also includes reinforced springs and dampers, rear-window heating and a rear wiper. The article does not assign axle-specific suspension products or identify the unnamed comparison’s body, powertrain or complete battery.[1]

Physical selection

Battery selection requires the actual electrical demand, starting-current convention, case, cable connections, polarity and retention. The period capacities alone establish none of those interfaces. The currently listed Škoda Economy service battery JZW915105C has its own product record; a historic relationship or supersession to either period class is Unconfirmed.
