Felicia 136M — Landi LPG-conversion requirements

A conversion requires the actual recipient, injection hardware, manifolds, controls and equipment to match the specified application. Engine displacement alone does not establish fit.[1]

Recipient selection

  • Landi’s August 1998 recipient and stated equipment

Confirm the actual engine/controller, recipient equipment, shell and steering layout before applying a conversion arrangement. Neither a programme name nor a manufacturing-month field resolves production/VIN or component-revision limits.
