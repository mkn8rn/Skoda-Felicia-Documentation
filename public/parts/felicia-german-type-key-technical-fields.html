Felicia — German type-key technical fields

KBA’s type-key description records an administrative vehicle class, nominal engine output and several maximum fields. It describes the technical state from initial assignment through preparation of the directory; it is not an individual factory-build record or a physical component identity.[1][3]

Meanings relevant to the Felicia type entries
FieldInterpretation
Manufacturer and type keys8004 with the individual three-character key; not a Škoda stock number or factory body number.[1]
Assigned dateDate allocated to the type key; not a vehicle build, delivery, facelift or parts-selection cutoff.[1]
Legacy class / body codes01 / 0200 means the retiring national passenger-car / closed-body classification. It is broader than a factory hatchback, estate or Fun body identifier; it does not prove an enclosed Fun load bed.[2]
Vanplus class and body fieldThe goods directory records class 10 and no body code for keys 055–057. This does not establish a current N1 classification or the absence of a body, roof or partition.[3]
Fuel codes0001 denotes petrol; 0002 denotes diesel. Neither identifies a carburettor, injector, controller or injection-pump product.[2]
Numeric limitsThe axle, driven-axle, seating, displacement and total-mass fields are labelled as maxima. Seats include the driver. These are not counts of loose parts or an individual car’s installed capacity.[1][3]
Total-mass unitThe consulted directories do not print a unit for their total-mass column. Values below remain source values; an individual permitted load and its unit require the vehicle’s applicable approval/document.[1][3]

Physical component selection

A single driven-axle maximum does not name the driven axle. A seating maximum does not establish seat-frame, belt or mounting quantities. The records do not specify gearbox codes, steering side, airbag or ABS fitment, tyre/rim identities, body revision, option codes, market delivery, or a production/VIN application chain. Those physical selections are Unconfirmed. The type keys are distinct vehicle selectors, not orderable engine or body products.

Type-key and body associations

The official type-directory records associate 310/311/315/317 with type 791, 312/313/314/316 with 795, and 321/322/326 with Fun type 797. The manufacturer approval catalogue separately identifies ordinary Pickup 797/H361 and Fun 797/e11*96/79*0074. Keys 052/053/054/058 occur in the Felicia approval group and are written PICK UP by KBA. The same broad number 797 does not merge their physical body or approval applications.[6][7][8]

The insurance directory’s generic Limousine or Pick Up aliases do not establish a saloon body, a closed Fun load bed or interchangeable panels. Its SDI wording for a Fun trade entry does not establish an electronic/direct-injection engine. Engine architecture, calibration, actual body revisions and original component identities require their own application evidence.
