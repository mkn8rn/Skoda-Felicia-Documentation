Felicia Teves Mark 20 ABS family

The June 1995 Combi launch account identifies a four-channel Teves Mark 20 anti-lock-braking family in its extra-cost safety equipment discussion. The family name is not a full modulator/controller product or proof that the driven car had ABS.[1]

Source-defined family

The reported channel count is four. Controller, hydraulic-unit, sensor/ring and loom identities, hydraulic circuits, diagnostic/calibration versions and connector interfaces are Unconfirmed. A common Mark 20 label does not establish interchangeability of those assemblies.[1]

Application conditions

The source describes this as available Combi safety equipment and discusses extra-cost driver/front-passenger airbag classes separately. It does not map a fitted ABS/airbag combination to LX, GLX or SLX, an engine product, market, steering side or build/chassis interval. Its driven GLX observations are not installed-ABS evidence.[1]

Physical identification

Full original product numbers/suffixes, manufacturing labels, original quantities, service supersessions and exact option/production application are Unconfirmed. Neither a family name nor channel count selects a replacement hydraulic unit or controller.
