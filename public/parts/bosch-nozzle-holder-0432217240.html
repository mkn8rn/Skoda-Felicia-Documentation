Bosch nozzle-and-holder assembly 0 432 217 240

Bosch lists product number 0 432 217 240 as a diesel nozzle-and-holder assembly. Its catalogue records supplier service applications in four Felicia derivatives. The number is a Bosch product identifier; the application listing does not identify it as a Škoda factory part number.[1]

Catalogue identity

Bosch's component cross-reference associates this number with nozzle-holder type KCA 30 S 79, nozzle DN 0 SD 297 and an opening-pressure range of 130–138 bar. These are Bosch catalogue characteristics for this product entry. The same table lists a Bosch eXchange service number in a separate column; that is a distinct catalogue identity, not an alias or evidence of a Škoda factory supersession.[1]

Fitment limits

The catalogue identifies engine, rated output, vehicle label, supplier interval and quantity. It does not give a Škoda OE identifier, gearbox, market, steering side, grade, factory equipment, option code, or vehicle build/VIN boundary for these entries. The Bosch application therefore supports service-product selection within the stated catalogue scope, not a claim of original factory installation on an individual Felicia.[1]
