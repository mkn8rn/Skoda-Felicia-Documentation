Felicia homologation clutch specifications

The Group N original and Group A option describe different clutch selections. The source fields do not identify one common physical clutch.

Contents

  1. N-5551 original clutch
  2. A-5551 02/02 VO clutch
  3. Application and selection
  4. References

N-5551 original clutch

The original N-5551 form gives a dry clutch with plate diameter 190 ±2 mm. This diameter alone does not identify plate count, hub spline, pressure plate, release bearing or engine/flywheel interchangeability.[1]

A-5551 02/02 VO clutch

02/02 VO specifies hydraulic actuation and two clutch plates. Plate diameter, spline, master/slave-cylinder products, pressure plate and release geometry are Unconfirmed. The two-plate count is scoped to this option, not the Group N original or road AEE engine.[2]

Application and selection

  • N-5551 original 1.6
  • A-5551 02/02 VO
  • 02/02 VO with corrected gate

A shared nominal diameter or model designation is insufficient clutch-selection evidence.
