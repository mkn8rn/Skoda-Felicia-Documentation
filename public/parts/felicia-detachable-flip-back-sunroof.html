Felicia detachable flip-back sunroof

The Irish importer brochure identifies a detachable flip-back roof opening for the GLXi hatchback and estate. This is a source-defined assembly description, not a complete physical product identifier.[1]

Description

The brochure describes detachability and a flip-back opening. It does not identify panel material, operating drive, manufacturer, complete stock number, aperture dimensions, frame, seals, fixings or drainage. The description does not establish equivalence with a glass tilt cassette or an electrically driven roof.[1]
