Truckman C top — 1997 Caddy Pickup application

The period UK Caddy Pickup programme names a Truckman C load top. The programme name does not supply a complete shell or mounting-kit number.[1]

Contents

  1. Construction and load
  2. Application and selection
  3. See also
  4. References

Construction and load

The reported single-skin roof has a 75 kg load allowance. The launch account specifies even distribution. This is the supplier top’s roof allowance, not the vehicle payload or permission to omit the vehicle’s load restrictions.[1]

Application and selection

The named historical application is the Felicia-derived Volkswagen Caddy Pickup offered in the UK in 1997. Exact shell, glazing, door, sealing and mounting-kit identities remain Unconfirmed. The letter C alone does not identify the same product as later Truckman Aerotop programmes or prove an ordinary Felicia/Fun fitment.[2]
