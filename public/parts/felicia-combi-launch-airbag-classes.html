Felicia June 1995 Combi airbag classes

The Combi launch account describes extra-cost driver and front-passenger airbags of Volkswagen-group origin. The two source-defined module positions are distinct; their reported volumes do not identify products or prove installation on a particular Combi.[1]

Application conditions

The account places these airbags in its surcharge equipment discussion. It does not define single/paired package selection, fitment to a named LX/GLX/SLX or engine class, side airbags, pretensioners, controller/calibration, original quantity, market/steering-side application or production/chassis limits. The adjoining optional ABS description does not establish a required combined package.[1]
