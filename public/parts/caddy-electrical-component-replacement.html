Caddy electrical-component replacement requirements

The recipient electrical circuit, controller function and equipment selection determine a replacement. Caddy Pickup identification alone does not establish pin assignments, current rating, calibration, wiring or a particular load-top lamp.

Recipient requirements

Engine and preheating circuits

Identify the actual engine and installed control function. Match probe and preheating circuits, terminals, switching behaviour and calibration. A combined petrol/diesel diagram heading does not assign every component to both engines.

Instruments and wiper control

Match the cluster/sender characteristic and the actual wiper/wash circuit. Apply each product’s chassis and location conditions independently; a national sales year is not an adequate selector.

Lamp and connector interfaces

Establish the fitted hardtop and lamp aperture, bulb circuit and weather seals. For a contact housing, establish terminal positions, keying and mating parts; a pin count alone is insufficient.

Fixings

Determine the actual attachment, thread engagement, head clearance, material and strength grade. A catalogue association with an assembly does not supply a tightening torque or make one bolt suitable for all its fixings.
