Norwegian period Felicia component descriptions

BIL’s June 1999 account separates a used Felicia test vehicle from general Norwegian-market powertrain and equipment descriptions. Those evidence scopes do not identify one common physical part set.[1]

Tested vehicle

The tested/illustrated owner’s car had a steering-assistance pump replaced under warranty and a leaking intake hose replaced. This confirms those component classes on that car, without identifying their products. The account gives about 25,000 km but no unambiguous model year, national grade, engine code or VIN.[1]

1.6 estate market description

The Norwegian used-market account identifies a Felicia estate with the 1.6-litre Volkswagen engine, reported at 75 horsepower without defining the power-unit convention. It does not identify its engine code, output in kW, grade, transmission product or equipment set.[1]

Selection limits

The same account’s general 1.3-litre description reports 68 horsepower and 100 N·m, but does not map the tested car to an exact fuel system or code. Its family-level 1996-model-year airbag/ABS statement cannot assign those features to a tested vehicle of unspecified year. Steering assistance was not described as universal standard equipment.
