MTX Felicia Cabrio body and roof construction

The MTX RS Cabrio leaflets describe a four-door Felicia conversion with a 2+2 seating arrangement, floor reinforcement and a transverse protective roof hoop. These are converter-defined construction features; they do not establish the same physical body or roof product for every Cabrio build.[1][2]

Body construction

The converter describes additional floor reinforcement and a new targa-style hoop intended to protect occupants in an overturn. Four doors and two front plus two rear seating places distinguish the described body. The leaflets do not specify reinforcement sections, material grades, joints, welding procedures, test results or an approval number.[1][2]

Roof and rear-seat arrangement

The soft roof has three fabric layers and manual operation. It folds into a space behind the rear seats and has a separate fabric cover when stowed. The rear seat can be folded or removed to enlarge luggage space. Fabric composition, frame geometry, sealing details, fasteners and exact seat/roof product identities are Unconfirmed.[1][2]

Technical-sheet power-unit alternatives

The undated sheet distinguishes three power-unit alternatives. Its measurements and performance entries are not engine product identities, rebuild tolerances or proof of a particular dated Standart/GLX build. The sheet gives five transmission speeds without identifying a gearbox product or explicitly defining its shifting method.[3]

The fuel is described as unleaded petrol. The written octane values do not name a measurement scale. The sheet provides neither an engine code/fuel-metering specification nor a mass-testing basis or production/chassis interval.[3]

Application selection

Match the actual donor, converter build and roof/body revision. A Cabrio designation does not resolve the reinforcement, hoop, roof, seat, wiring or engine assembly fitted to an individual car. The September 1997 price offer identifies trims and engine designations separately from this undated technical sheet; it supplies no build-to-sheet or product mapping.[4]
