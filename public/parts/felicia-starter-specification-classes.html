Felicia starter specification classes

The repair specification separates early petrol, later petrol and diesel starter ratings. These are source-defined electrical classes, rather than complete physical part identities.[1]

Starting-system operation

The manual describes a negative-earth electrical system and a pre-engaged starter with an integral solenoid. Engagement moves the pinion into the flywheel ring gear before the motor cranks the engine. An overrunning clutch prevents the running engine from driving the armature at engine-imposed speed.[1]

Physical selection

A starter with a matching voltage or power rating still requires the correct mounting, ring-gear engagement, connections and operating clearance. The manual’s June wording must not be converted to a first-of-month change date, and the rated classes do not identify a particular Magneton or Bosch product. They do not independently establish a Pickup, Fun, Vanplus or national-grade original fitment.[1]
