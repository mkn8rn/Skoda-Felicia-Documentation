Bosch glow plug 0 250 201 032

Bosch 0 250 201 032 is the glow-plug service identity specified for the Felicia diesel in Haynes manual 3505. A glow plug supplies heat for diesel starting; it does not perform the spark-ignition function of a petrol spark plug.[1]
