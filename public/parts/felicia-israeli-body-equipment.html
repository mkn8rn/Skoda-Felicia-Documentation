Felicia Israeli body and equipment records

The Israeli model catalogue distinguishes national selections by manufacturer, model code and recorded year. Its body and equipment fields describe administrative model classes, without identifying original production components or their installation origin.[1]

Body and seating fields

Positive door and seat counts are retained as aggregate counts. They do not assign individual door panels, a rear-seat layout or seat/belt products. Blank fields and zero counts do not establish a body without doors or seats. The returned gross-mass field has no unit definition; its values remain raw administrative entries.[1]

Equipment fields

Air-conditioning, ABS, steering assistance and automatic-transmission values below are the catalogue’s indicators. The returned schema does not define the numeric flag legend or identify whether equipment was installed at the factory, by the importer or later. A positive airbag or electric-window count is an aggregate count, without position or product identification. Zero/default entries are not treated as proof of equipment absence.[1]

Physical-part selection

One or two reported airbags do not assign driver, passenger or side modules, pretensioners, a control unit or its calibration. Two or four reported electric windows do not identify individual regulators, motors, switches or looms. An assistance or ABS indicator does not determine a pump, steering rack, hydraulic modulator, sensor or controller. Those original identities, quantities and position-specific applications remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Matching national model fields does not establish original-part equivalence with a Dutch, Czech, Swiss or other-market configuration. Exact engine/gearbox products, steering side, body revision, installation origin, option codes and manufacturing/VIN limits remain Unconfirmed.
