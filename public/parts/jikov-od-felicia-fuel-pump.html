Jikov OD mechanical fuel-pump specification

N-5528 supply variant 01/01 VF names a Jikov OD mechanically operated membrane fuel pump mounted on the cylinder block.[1]

Contents

  1. Operation and selection
  2. Application
  3. References

Operation and selection

The form records a maximum flow of 1 litre per minute. It gives no test speed, delivery pressure, complete product number, diaphragm dimensions or mounting pattern. The flow value alone does not establish service compatibility.[1]

Application

N-5528 01/01 VF is valid from 1 June 1995. Its reference to A-5528 05/01 VF does not identify a common pump SKU or establish installation on every carburettor road model.
