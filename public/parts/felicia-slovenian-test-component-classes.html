March 1995 GLXi

The technical panel specifies a dry single-plate clutch, five-speed manual transmission and front-wheel drive. The dimensionless ratios are source-scoped to this test specification: first 3.31:1, second 1.91:1, third 1.27:1, fourth 0.93:1, fifth 0.72:1, reverse 2.92:1 and final drive 4.16:1. No physical gearbox family, assembly suffix or clutch spline/diameter is identified.[1]

The panel gives front suspension struts with triangular lower arms, a semi-rigid rear axle, rear coil springs and telescopic dampers, front disc/rear drum brakes and a mechanical parking brake acting on the rear wheels. The disc description does not supply an independently identified ventilation/product selection. Rack steering is stated, but the prose says the steering has no assistance while the panel states servo assistance; the actual assisted/unassisted rack selection is Unconfirmed.[1]

The stated rim class is 4.5 J × 13 and road tyre size is 165/70 R13, with Matador named without a product model, load/speed symbols or production code. The battery is described as 12 V / 44 Ah and the charging alternator as 55 A. Measurement conditions, case/terminal/pulley/offset dimensions, exact products and spare-wheel/tyre applications are Unconfirmed.[1]

June 1995 Combi launch

The grouped table describes front suspension struts with triangular arms and a stabiliser, a semi-rigid rear axle with coil springs, telescopic dampers and a stabiliser, front disc/rear drum brakes, rack steering, 4.5 J × 13 rims and 165/70 R13 tyres. Five gears are listed; the driven GLX 1.6 is separately described with a strengthened synchronised gearbox. No complete transmission, suspension, brake, rim or tyre product is identified.[2]

The driven GLX 1.6 is described without a steering servo. The table’s ABS notation and the separately described surcharge airbag classes do not establish installed ABS on that car or on the LX/SLX offers. The launch account names the Teves Mark 20 family for the described optional ABS system. The Slovenian prices were not established at publication; launch observations are not national grade-wide factory application limits.[2]
