Bosch WR 7 LTC+ spark plug (0 242 235 664)

Bosch WR 7 LTC+ is a spark-plug service product with manufacturer number 0 242 235 664. The Felicia entry in Bosch’s 2017–2018 catalogue specifies it for the AEE 1.6-litre petrol engine.[1]
