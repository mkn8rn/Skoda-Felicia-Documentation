Felicia approved wheel and tyre combinations

The January 2022 wheel and tyre schedule commissioned by Škoda Auto Deutschland distinguishes vehicle type, approval extension, German type key and rough-road equipment. It covers passenger Felicia, Combi, Vanplus, commercial Pickup and Fun through separate approval identities. A permitted combination is not proof of the wheel or tyre originally supplied on a particular car.[1]

Vehicle and approval selection

Selection uses the commercial name, vehicle type, approval number including its extension, and the German type key. The schedule reads the three-character type-key prefix from registration field 2.2; its rough-road footnote additionally lists complete keys. A shared engine output or the common type number 797 does not merge ordinary Pickup with Fun.[1]

Tyre ratings and snow chains

A designation such as 165/70 R13 79T records nominal section width, aspect ratio, radial construction, rim diameter, load index and speed symbol. Preserve the load and speed ratings as well as the dimensions; matching only width and aspect ratio does not identify the same approved tyre specification.[1]

Where chains are permitted, the schedule restricts them to fine-link chains projecting no more than 15 mm. Its G952/H110 and H780 additional 14-inch selections prohibit chains. The separate Fun combinations permit chains under the same projection limit.[1]

Physical component identification

The schedule does not identify wheel stock numbers, casting or pressing revisions, tyre brands or tread products, bolt seats or threads, hub interfaces, centre-cover retention, or originally supplied quantities. Those fields remain Unconfirmed. Approval alternatives must not be assigned as original equipment to a national sales grade, FIA configuration or individual car without its applicable selection record.

N-5528 wheel option 03/01 VO

N-5528 03/01 VO, valid from 1 April 1996, gives two wheel sizes: 13 × 5.5 inches and 14 × 5.5 inches. The form also expresses their diameter as 330.2 and 355.6 mm and the width as 139.7 mm. Offset, profile, bolt pattern, centre bore, stock number and tyre selection are Unconfirmed. These FIA options do not establish road-approval or original rim fitment under the German approval conditions above.[17]

N-5551 original and option wheels

The N-5551 original form gives 13 × 4.5 inch front, rear and spare wheels, expressed also as 330.2 ×114.3 mm. The spare is in the luggage-compartment floor. Profile, offset, bolt pattern, stock number and tyre rating are Unconfirmed.[18]

01/01 VO, valid from 1 March 1997, separately permits 13 ×5.5 and 14 ×5.5 inch alternatives, expressed as 330.2/355.6 mm diameter and 139.7 mm width. These competition alternatives do not establish the rim fitted under a road-grade or German tyre approval.[19]

Russian November 1997 LX tyre class

The period LX road test identifies tubeless 165/70 R13 tyres and names Barum and Matador as suppliers. It gives no complete tyre product, load/speed rating, rim product or original quantity. This is a reported tyre class, not a German approval-key selection, a factory build boundary or proof of interchangeability between supplier products.[20]
