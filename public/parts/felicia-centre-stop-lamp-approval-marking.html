Felicia — centre stop-lamp approval marking

The 1998 manufacturer description expressly records a fitted centre brake-light function with the marking below. The marking is an approval identifier; full original product identity and interchangeability with another lamp are Unconfirmed.[1]

Physical lamp selection

A matching mark does not establish the same supplier stock number, housing, mounting arrangement, wiring connector or revision. Approval of a function is separate from proof of an orderable original part. The record does not identify a lamp unit for every Combi or hatchback, or establish a service supersession.
