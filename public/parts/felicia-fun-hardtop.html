Felicia Fun hardtop fitment

The Felicia Fun’s load bed adjoins a movable rear cabin wall and deployable rear seats. A hardtop must accommodate that body arrangement and its rear closure.

Contents

  1. Recipient requirements
  2. Documented alternatives
  3. See also
  4. References

Recipient requirements

Confirm the canopy’s load-bed mounting points, sealing surfaces and clearances around the movable cabin wall. The Fun-specific designation is essential: its recreational body is not interchangeable with the ordinary two-seat commercial Pickup merely because both use type 797.

The accessory leaflet does not give clamp dimensions, a mounting-kit part number, a roof load limit, or permission to operate the 2+2 seating with either canopy installed. Those technical conditions are Unconfirmed.
