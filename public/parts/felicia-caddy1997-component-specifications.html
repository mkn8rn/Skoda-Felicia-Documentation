1997 UK Caddy Pickup component specifications

The UK Volkswagen Caddy Pickup brochure of January 1997 describes the Felicia-derived commercial vehicle. Its component classes are not shared physical part identities for the Polo-derived Caddy van or the Felicia Fun.[1][2]

Powertrain and running gear

The manufacturer names a 1.9-litre SD diesel, with 64 bhp at 4,400 rpm and 124 N·m at 2,000–3,000 rpm. It specifies five forward gears and front-wheel drive. Engine and gearbox product codes are Unconfirmed. Steering assistance is standard. Braking uses two hydraulic circuits, a servo, front discs, self-adjusting rear drums and load-dependent pressure limitation. The front has triangular arms, spring struts and an anti-roll bar; the rear has coil springs and telescopic dampers.[1]

Body and restraint classes

The brochure specifies a unitary steel shell, double-skinned load compartment, plastic sidewall capping and supported tailboard. Door impact bars and a strengthened cab bulkhead provide protection; the cab has a collapsible column, padded wheel, head restraints and height-adjustable three-point belts with pretensioners. Stereo radio/cassette, internally adjustable door mirrors, cloth upholstery, pollen filter and electronic immobilisation are standard.[1]

Factory option descriptions

The brochure names optional airbags, ABS, steering-height adjustment, headlamp washing and seat heating. The contemporary launch report also lists air conditioning and a factory towbar, and names driver/passenger airbag positions; it does not give their order codes or separate-versus-paired ordering rule.[1][2]

Source and physical selection

The manufacturer gives a 605 kg payload without defining the driver allowance on that entry. Commercial Motor’s November test gives 530 kg with a 75 kg driver; its specification table reports power at 4,300 rpm and torque at 2,500–3,200 rpm, differing from the manufacturer’s entries. These reports do not establish a production powertrain change. The brochure’s illustrated cab is right-hand drive, but it supplies no complete steering, wheel, engine, brake, restraint or build/option/VIN selection chain.[1][3]
