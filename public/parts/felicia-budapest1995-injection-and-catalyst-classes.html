Felicia Budapest 1995 injection and catalyst classes

The Hungarian show report uses a written injection-family name and a three-way catalyst description for its named Felicia offers. It does not identify individual fuel or exhaust products.[1]

Metering designation

The printed passage names Monomotronik injection. It supplies no manufacturer, full metering/controller number, injector dimensions, pressure, wiring or calibration. That written name alone does not map the reported offers to a 135B/136B engine or an identified Bosch Mono-Motronic assembly.[1]

Catalyst class

The range is described with a three-way exhaust catalyst. Its housing, substrate, connection dimensions, original identity, number per car and emissions/production selection are Unconfirmed. No exhaust assembly is identified by the output or injection-family label.[1]
