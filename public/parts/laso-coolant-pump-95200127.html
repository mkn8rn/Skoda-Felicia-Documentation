LASO 9520 0127 coolant pump

9520 0127 is a distinct LASO coolant-pump supplier product in the 2016 catalogue. Its comparison references do not identify an original component installed in a particular Felicia.[1]

Comparison references

The primary comparison reference is 030121008M. The source’s earlier-reference list contains 030121008K, 030121008L, 030121008D, 030121005TV, 030121005T, 030121005NV, 030121005N.[1]

The source additionally prints 030121008DX, 030121005TX, 030121005NX in parentheses; their exchange-programme or historical application meaning is Unconfirmed.[1]

The supplier association does not establish one-to-one physical identity, installation in a particular car or a chronological Škoda factory supersession.[1]
