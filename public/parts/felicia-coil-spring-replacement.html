Felicia coil-spring replacement

Coil-spring selection depends on the actual body, axle, engine and load class, suspension package, seat geometry and working travel. A model name or engine displacement alone does not select a physical spring.

Recipient identification

Identify hatchback, passenger Combi and ordinary Pickup separately. Preserve the complete vehicle/approval key, actual engine and axle-load class, suspension equipment and applicable production restrictions. KYB’s listed engine classes do not distinguish output, injection revision or national grade. Its commercial Pickup entry supplies no Fun or Vanplus application.[1]

Interfaces and operating requirements

Match both spring seats and end orientation, free and installed lengths, wire and coil diameters, lateral clearance and retention throughout suspension travel. Preserve the required load/deflection behaviour, ride height, usable damper travel, bump-stop engagement and tyre/body clearance. A catalogue type code alone does not supply those measurements.

Use a matched axle pair for spring replacement. This service practice does not establish a sales-pack quantity or an original vehicle bill of materials.[1]

Heavy-duty and rough-road distinctions

KYB distinguishes standard and heavy-duty service columns. The heavy-duty rear entry is not mapped to Škoda’s complete rough-road type keys. Neither a package name nor vehicle height establishes the required spring product. Exact package-to-product selection remains Unconfirmed; a heavy-duty service spring is not automatically an original rough-road spring.[1]
