PAL Magneton Felicia N-5551 alternator selection

The original N-5551 form names PAL Magneton as one of two alternator suppliers. This is a supplier selection, not an identified stock number.

Contents

  1. Specification and selection
  2. Application
  3. References

Specification and selection

The form records one belt-driven alternator and a power range of 770–1,260 W. It does not assign a particular point in that range to either supplier. Voltage, test speed, regulator, pulley, terminal layout, mounting and complete product number are Unconfirmed.[1]

Application

PAL Magneton supplier configuration is distinct from the other supplier selection. Neither shared belt drive nor overlapping rating establishes identical products or direct interchangeability.
