Felicia Laurin & Klement and Autotest configuration engineering

The undated Slovak manufacturer leaflet and the two Autotest records describe different source-defined configurations. A matching edition, displacement or output does not establish an identical engine, restraint, trim or physical assembly.

Manufacturer powertrain descriptions

The leaflet pairs Felicia 1.3 BMM with the hatchback and Felicia Combi 1.6 MPI with the estate. These written designations do not provide a full factory engine product/code, gearbox or manufacturing selection.[1]

Manufacturer safety and interior description

The safety face names ABS and two airbags, for the driver and front passenger. The interior face describes leather trim. Those are leaflet equipment descriptions; they do not establish the modules, restraint controller, belt pretensioners, upholstery product codes or all earlier/later national Laurin & Klement specifications.[1]

Tested powertrain descriptions

Both reports describe a 1,289 cm³ OHV petrol four-cylinder rated at 50 kW at 5,000 rpm and 106 Nm at 2,600 rpm. The hatchback report names multipoint injection; the Combi report expressly describes the multipoint system introduced for its 1997 revision. Neither record gives a complete factory engine/gearbox product or build/VIN boundary.[2]

Physical selection

Publication descriptions, recorded masses and visibly equipped test cars do not provide an original-parts catalogue. Full identifiers/suffixes, original quantities, gearbox, steering/market/option/body revisions, serial/build boundaries and service supersessions remain Unconfirmed. The manufacturer leaflet’s date/country and the test pages’ printed year/issue are not inferred from the archive label or centenary imagery.
