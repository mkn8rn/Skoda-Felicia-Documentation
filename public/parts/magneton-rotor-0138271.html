Magneton rotor 0138.271

0138.271 is a separately numbered Magneton rotor in the manufacturer’s alternator service listings. The identifier belongs to the component; it is not a vehicle configuration or a confirmed factory Škoda stock number.[1][2]

Physical selection

The rotor identifier does not give winding data, shaft and slip-ring dimensions or balance limits. Complete-alternator ratings are not a specification for this loose rotor.[1][2]

Identify the complete recipient alternator and its revision before substituting an internal part. A similar role or nominal alternator rating does not prove dimensional or electrical compatibility.
