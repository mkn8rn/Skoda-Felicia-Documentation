Magneton alternator 9517464

Supplier cross-references

The manufacturer lists the following brand/identifier associations: BOSCH 0986040880; ŠKODA AUTO 028903026B; VW 028903026B. These associations do not prove physical aliases, a factory supersession sequence or original installation on a particular vehicle.[1]

Physical selection

Selection requires the actual mounting, shaft/pulley interface, drive alignment, belt, clearances, electrical connections, regulation and equipment load. Published current and speed values do not supply those interfaces or semiconductor, winding and brush specifications.

The manufacturer lists pulley 0138.621 for this alternator.[1] The other separately identified diesel alternator lists pulley 6020.861.[2] The complete assemblies have the same published ratings, but shared internal identifiers do not prove complete-alternator interchangeability or a one-to-one supersession.
