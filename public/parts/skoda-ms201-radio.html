Škoda MS 201 — radio operating-manual selection

The MS 201 instructions supplied in Škoda’s Felicia manual catalogue describe a cassette radio with a detachable control panel and an external CD-changer connection. The complete Škoda order number and hardware revision remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Identity and variants

The manual uses a radio identity card containing the model, serial number and security code. It describes a removable front panel and coded anti-theft protection. Its AM-band instructions expressly apply only to WKC 3301 RDS; that restriction does not identify every MS 201 as the same receiver variant.[1]

Physical equivalence to the separately named Grundig Music System 201 programme selection is Unconfirmed. A shared number in a commercial name does not establish one stock number, circuit-board revision, pinout applicability or interchangeability.

Receiver and audio functions

The described functions include FM reception with RDS, traffic-programme selection and alternative-frequency operation, plus an autoreverse cassette mechanism optimised for chromium-dioxide tape. A compatible external Grundig CD changer is a separate device. The auxiliary-input mode uses a Grundig CDP adapter when no changer is connected; it does not make the radio an integrated CD player.[1]

The stated AUX connection belongs to the described adapter mode; it is not an instruction to bridge unidentified vehicle terminals. The exact compatible changer, adapter stock number and unused-contact assignments remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Mounting and recipient selection

The illustrated installation uses a retaining cage and paired extraction tools. The aerial connection may require an adapter. The manual warns against routing wiring over the unit because it heats during use. It does not provide a complete cage, dashboard-trim, loom, adapter or aerial product identity for a particular Felicia.[1]

Recipient selection requires the actual radio model and revision, matching contact identification, mounting support and vehicle wiring. These manual connections do not establish the pinout of every Felicia loom, or that a unit carrying only the MS 201 sales name has this exact interface.

Named vehicle applications

The February 2000 Czech Family offer names a Škoda MS 201 radio in its standard equipment for the listed hatchback and Combi powertrains. It gives no complete radio stock number or revision mapping to the operating manual.[2]
