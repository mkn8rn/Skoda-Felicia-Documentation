Felicia Czech July 2000 equipment selections

The factory list FC 019 15 07/00 describes equipment by Czech grade and prices separate factory choices. Those descriptions select functions and appearance classes, not complete physical component identities.[1][2]

Exterior equipment

The standard equipment matrix lists body-colour bumpers, a spoiler integrated into the front bumper, a rear-window wiper and a high-level stop lamp for all four grades. Standard wheel descriptions give a steel 4.5J × 13 rim class. The two wheel-cover descriptions distinguish large-area covers for Trumf/GLX from silver-finish covers for the unsuffixed/LX offers. Trumf and GLX also have body-colour mirrors and exterior-handle/lock-cover finishes and standard tinted glazing. The estate-only roof-carrier entry is standard for Combi GLX and separately priced for the other three grades.[2]

The optional alloy-wheel sets are described as four 13-inch wheels, or five 14-inch wheels for GLX. Those sales-set counts do not identify installed wheel positions, a spare-wheel product, offset, alloy design or a factory number.[2]

Cabin trim

The standard unsuffixed and LX offers have a two-spoke steering-wheel description; Trumf and GLX have a four-spoke description. These entries do not select the wheel fitted with an optional airbag. Cloth trim is specified by grade, without a complete seat/cover identity, colour selector or side-airbag-compatible construction.[2]

Standard restraint arrangement

The common matrix specifies height-adjustable front three-point belts, two rear outer three-point belt positions, a rear centre two-point belt position, height-adjustable front and rear head restraints, and rear-door child-safety locks. It does not identify belt, buckle, guide, head-restraint or latch products.[2]

Airbag and pretensioner choices

Three factory-priced restraint selections are marked as available in every grade column for both listed passenger bodies. Each explicitly includes front-belt pretensioners. The side-airbag entry does not specify its module count, seat position or mounting design. These July 2000 conditions do not establish the driver-only pretensioner fitment of a different dated offer.[2]

Steering assistance

The steering-assistance entry expressly associates standard assistance with 1.9 D cars. The price panel lists that engine in LX and GLX hatchback and Combi offers. It gives no assistance hardware or steering-side code and does not establish that all petrol offers had assistance.[1][2]

Security and brake choices

The immobiliser is standard in all four grade columns. The separately priced security choices distinguish an alarm, central locking with remote operation and alarm, and remote operation/alarm for a central-locking system. These descriptions do not select a module, harness or key product.[2]

ABS with 13-inch Continental tyres is listed for all four grades. ABS with EDS and 13-inch Continental tyres is listed for Trumf, LX and GLX. The two 14-inch ABS or ABS/EDS selections are confined to GLX. A listed choice does not establish installation or absence, a tyre stock number, brake/controller identity or its combination with another option.[2]

Physical selection

Exact original identifiers and suffixes, component quantities, steering-side/build/option codes, engine/gearbox products and production/VIN limits remain Unconfirmed. Generic equipment classes do not establish identical or interchangeable assemblies between grades, bodies or restraint selections.
