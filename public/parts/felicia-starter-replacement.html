Felicia starter replacement

A replacement starter must match the recipient engine/transmission installation, ring-gear engagement and electrical circuit. Period rating groups and a supplier service association answer different selection questions; neither, alone, identifies the exact original assembly.

Recipient interfaces

  • Actual engine, gearbox/bellhousing, starter mounting pilot, fasteners and clearances.
  • Flywheel/ring-gear identity, tooth engagement, pinion travel and drive orientation.
  • Supply/earth circuit, current capability, terminals, control connection and cable routing.
  • Starter product/revision and the relevant source-defined operating class.

Internal-component selection

The manufacturer’s separate internal service members belong to its identified starter, rather than to every Felicia starter of similar rating. The actual parent product/revision and component geometry remain necessary selectors.[2]

Original and interchangeability limits

A numerical rating, brand cross-reference, broad engine label or named bearing is not proof of complete-starter interchangeability. Supplier catalogue ranges are not factory production/VIN boundaries. None of these associations supplies a full original-parts inventory, national equipment/steering/option selection, or an exact engine/gearbox revision chain.[1][2]
