Caddy rear-axle and brake-drum replacement requirements

A rear-axle, drum, bearing, seal or retaining part requires the actual recipient assembly and braking equipment. Individual manufacturer service callouts do not establish interchangeability of a complete axle or an original factory bill.

Recipient interfaces

Identify the exact Caddy Pickup axle, hub/drum, bearings, shaft, sealing and retention arrangement. Match braking and ABS equipment, wheel interface, bearing seats, shaft/seal geometry, preload and fastener selection. Establish load/axle rating, revision, chassis/option applicability and the actual repair specification. Dimensions or shared individual products alone cannot establish a replacement axle.
