Felicia radio replacement

Radio selection requires the recipient’s dashboard mounting, wiring, aerial connection and speaker arrangement. A grade or commercial radio name does not establish the physical product or an interchangeable installation.

Recipient selection

The Irish programme does not specify these interfaces for its named units. The dated UK Popular description identifies radio/cassette equipment, but no original part or connector selection. Product revision, mounting/cage, power and ground connections, protection, aerial and speaker connections remain unconfirmed in these applications.[1][2]
