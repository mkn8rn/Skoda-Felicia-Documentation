Felicia sport suspension and exhaust selection

Select the actual axle load, body, engine/exhaust arrangement and approval first. Springs and dampers require their seats, travel, rates, brackets and wheel/tyre clearances. A silencer requires the actual emission/catalyst arrangement, pipe/joint diameter, routing, hangers, outlet and noise approval. A product called a stabiliser still requires its actual mounting and operating architecture; the name alone does not establish an anti-roll bar or a strut brace.

Recipient requirements

Identify the complete recipient component, actual body/engine/equipment and applicable production/approval limits. Matching appearance or a nominal size does not establish fit. A catalogue accessory application is distinct from historical original installation, a direct service replacement and a conversion.
