Jenvey injection for the Felicia 1.6 kit variant

FIA A-5551 extension 04/01 VK specifies a Jenvey Dynamics Ltd multipoint system for the Felicia 1.6 kit variant. It is a competition specification distinct from the original A-5551 Magneti Marelli system.[1]

Metering specification

The kit entry uses electronic fuel metering and manifold injection. It records eight effective fuel outlets and a 45 ± 0.25 mm intake passage at the throttle. The outlet count describes the homologated metering arrangement; it does not establish eight identical injector assemblies or their complete stock numbers.[1]

Application and selection

The specification applies to A-5551 kit variant 04/01 VK, whose validity begins on 1 April 1996. It does not identify a factory road-car trim, a road-engine code or a production/VIN range. Injector flow, pressure, controller/calibration, linkage, manifold interface and exact product identities are Unconfirmed. The recorded Jenvey system is not evidence of a direct replacement for the road injection assembly.[1]
