Grundig Music System 401 — Felicia programme selection

The model-year 1999 Felicia programme names this optional radio selection. Its commercial designation does not establish one complete physical stock number or a radio installed in every Felicia.[1]

Application and interfaces

The report introduces the named unit in the Felicia optional-equipment programme, without a complete body/grade/engine ordering matrix. Its complete mounting, electrical, aerial, speaker, trim, option and chassis selection remain Unconfirmed. No A-suffix product, current seller stock number, interchangeability or supersession is assigned.[1]

Space Line written reference

The Space Line manufacturer programme separately names a Škoda MS 401 radio choice. Its stock number and physical revision are not given; equality with the Grundig Music System 401 selection remains Unconfirmed. The matching written number does not establish identical hardware or interchangeability.[2]
