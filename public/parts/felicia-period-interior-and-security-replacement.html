Felicia period cabin and security accessories

Select the actual dashboard, steering side, seat/belt geometry, trim and attachment first. A shelf, floor mat or refrigerator needs its own mounting/clearance and electrical requirements. A lock or alarm needs its closure/linkage geometry, key/controller, loom, trigger logic and protection. Historical child-seat descriptions are not a current certification or proof of a particular belt/seat installation. A roof opening requires its own panel/structure/seal selection.

Recipient requirements

Identify the complete recipient component and vehicle configuration, including body/equipment, mounting, electrical/mechanical interfaces and applicable production/option limits. Complete written accessory numbers preserve identity; shared shape, brand, displacement or nominal dimension does not establish a replacement match.

Period accessory selections

The catalogue names these Felicia-series accessory applications. They are intended accessory selections whose exact recipient fit still needs the interfaces above; they are not confirmed historical original parts or unrestricted replacements.

Original-fitment distinction

Catalogue association establishes an offered accessory identity. It does not establish installation on a dated national grade, a factory option/product code, original quantity, production/VIN boundary, direct supersession or present availability.
