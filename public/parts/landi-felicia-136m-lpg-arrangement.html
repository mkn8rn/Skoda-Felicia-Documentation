Control and pressure arrangement

The reducer attaches at the driver-side engine support; the metering unit uses two brackets at the bonnet crossmember. Distribution attaches at the head. The L.I.S. controller uses two brackets ahead of the battery. Its pressure branch joins the petrol-regulator vacuum line; the reducer has a separate manifold take-off. Relief discharge is routed outside the engine compartment, clear of heat/electrics, with a downward outlet.[1]
