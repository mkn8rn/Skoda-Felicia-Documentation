Felicia Portuguese dealer equipment classes

The Portuguese Carlar advertisement describes common equipment and optional safety systems for its hatchback and Break offers. The equipment labels do not identify individual assemblies.[1]

Steering assistance

Steering assistance is advertised as standard for both bodies. The statement does not identify a rack, assistance mechanism, hydraulic pump/reservoir/hoses, electrical actuator or their original numbers. Equal advertised assistance does not establish equal components across the 1.3 and 1.9 offers.[1][2]

ABS and restraints

ABS and an unspecified airbag are advertised as optional. The text supplies no airbag position/count, pretensioner application, ABS manufacturer, hydraulic unit, sensors, original option codes or installed combination. It does not identify a driver-only, paired-front or side-airbag configuration.[1]

Later range promotion

The October 1997 range advertisement mentions multipoint injection and new diesel engines, without a body/grade/capacity mapping. It does not select the fuel system or engine code of a particular 1.3 GLXi or 1.9 GLXD offer.[3]
