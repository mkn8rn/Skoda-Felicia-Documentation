Pitagora 160 injector emulator

The 2001 Landi front-kit sheet names Pitagora 160. That written selection does not identify every hardware or harness revision.[1]

AEB code-160 specification

AEB specifies code 160 for three- or four-cylinder multipoint applications, separately from suffixed harness products. Its manual gives 10–14 V DC supply, 11–16 Ω injector emulation, three selectable emulation modes and approximately 0–1 second adjustable petrol/gas overlap. Injector channels are interrupted and emulated separately; grouped and sequential systems are described.[2]

The described emulator leaves the electric petrol pump operating during gas use. Its stated fallback reconnects the original injector circuits after gas-electrical failure. These are the AEB manual’s descriptions, not independently measured behaviour of the pictured Landi unit.[2]

Dated product selection

Physical correspondence between the Landi-branded selection and the later AEB code-160 description, actual connector/circuit revision, recipient injector resistance and changeover calibration are Unconfirmed. Similar names do not prove interchangeable hardware.[1][2]
