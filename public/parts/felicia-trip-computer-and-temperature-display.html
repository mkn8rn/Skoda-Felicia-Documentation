Felicia trip computers and outside-temperature displays

An outside-temperature indicator and a trip computer are different equipment descriptions. A stated function does not identify the instrument, sensor, wiring or calibration.

West-Auto Twist equipment

The March 2000 West-Auto offer names an onboard computer among the added equipment. It gives no device name, function list, display position or input connections.[1]

Auto Perfect Twist equipment

The May 2000 Auto Perfect offer specifies an outside thermometer as an addition to LX equipment. Its measuring range, accuracy, probe position, mounting and electrical connections are unspecified.[2]

Device identification

Neither offer identifies a product reference or establishes whether the display is independent or integrated with another instrument. Equivalence between the two descriptions is Unconfirmed. Neither source identifies the devices as one of the separately documented Škoda trip-computer accessories.
