Felicia alternator replacement

A replacement alternator must satisfy the actual drive, mounting, electrical circuit and equipment load. A catalogue’s model association is a service selection lead, rather than an exact original-fitment record.

Recipient interfaces

  • Actual engine/alternator mounting, adjustment mechanism, attachment and clearances.
  • Drive belt, pulley grooves, diameter, offset, shaft connection and alignment.
  • Supply/earth wiring, connector allocation, regulation and control/warning circuit.
  • Fitted electrical equipment, load and relevant alternator operating class.
  • Complete recipient product and revision before selecting a loose internal member.

The period charging-system specification distinguishes electrical-system and alternator rating scope without identifying each original product.[1]

Internal-component selection

Select internal members from the actual complete alternator identity and revision. Shared exact component identifiers may have several parent applications; that does not merge the parent alternators. Assembled service members and separately listed loose members are not automatically additive.

The following component associations retain their actual recipient assembly; they do not identify quantities or the contents of an assembled service member.[2][3][4][5]

Original-fitment limits

Supplier ranges and brand cross-references do not establish original production part numbers, national-grade fitment, VIN limits or factory supersession. A named internal member does not establish its installed quantity, position or complete physical specification.
