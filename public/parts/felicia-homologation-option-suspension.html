Felicia homologation-option suspension specifications

Source-defined competition stabilizers and suspension options require their own homologation selection; a common Felicia name does not establish road fitment.

N-5528 supply stabilizers

N-5528 01/01 VF specifies effective front and rear stabilizer diameters of 22 and 16 mm respectively. Its validity begins on 1 June 1995.[1]

Reinforced and adjustable options

A-5528 02/02 VO and A-5551 01/01 VO describe reinforced front lower arms and rear axles, adjustable spring mounts, front/rear stabilizers and steering-ratio alternatives 21.18, 17.65 and 15.12. The A-5551 spring-mount wording identifies both axles in Czech while its adjacent English description mentions the front; no universal rear-seat product is inferred from that wording.[2][3]

A-5528 03/03 VO names front/rear suspension and damper selections, front/rear stabilizers and a cockpit control. A-5528 07/04 VO separately illustrates reinforced rear-axle and damper options. A-5528 12/07 VO illustrates front-arm and rear-axle/hub options; 19/10 VO identifies reinforced front lower-arm, stabilizer and knuckle selections. Photographic assembly recognition does not identify attachment dimensions or complete stock numbers.[4][5][6][7]

A-5551 02/02 VO identifies front/rear suspension, stabilizer and damper options and flanged joint shafts. Its photographs and the A-5528 03/03 VO damper selections identify remote-reservoir damper architecture without establishing reservoir volume, hose or valve specifications. A-5551 05/04 VO separately illustrates a rear-axle/hub option.[8][9]

N-5551 original suspension

The original N-5551 form records steel front and rear coil springs. Its front steel stabilizer has effective length 1,140 mm ±1% and diameter 20 mm; the rear steel stabilizer has effective length 940 mm ±1% and diameter 14 mm. Spring rates, bar/arm mounting geometry and complete component products are Unconfirmed. These are separate source applications, not inferred A-5528 supply identities.[10]

Physical selection

The N-5528 supply specification does not identify the bars’ products, material, arm geometry, bushes, mountings or production/VIN range. Diameter is not a complete stiffness or interchangeability specification.
