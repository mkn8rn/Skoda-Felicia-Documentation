Felicia 14S gearbox family

The Greek June 1996 test identifies the 1.6 passenger-car transmission as the 14S family, developed by Škoda from the earlier 10S design and strengthened for the larger engine. That family designation is not a complete assembly number or proof of interchangeability with a 10S or another 14S revision.[1]

Architecture

The paired GLXi test specification gives five manually selected forward gears and front-wheel drive, with a mechanically operated single-plate clutch. The report does not identify a differential product, gearbox casing suffix, selector, clutch diameter or driveshaft interface.[1]
