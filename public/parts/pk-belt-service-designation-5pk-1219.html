PK belt service designation 5PK-1219

The institute’s 2015 request names 5PK-1219 as a PK belt selection for the AEF 47 kW LX hatchback class. It does not name a belt manufacturer or full supplier stock product.[1][2]

Drive selection

The two requested items are an order quantity, not proof of two installed belts. The number does not establish which accessories or pulleys are driven, steering assistance, air conditioning or tensioner selection. Equal-looking or nearly equal numerical belt designations are not established equivalents.[1]
