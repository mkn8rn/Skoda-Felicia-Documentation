Felicia radio equipment

Felicia radio descriptions can identify a dealer programme or fitted equipment class without selecting one physical radio. The named products and source scopes remain distinct.

Irish brochure programme

The importer brochure names three Blaupunkt selections without a grade/body or factory part mapping.[1]

  • Blaupunkt Boston
  • Blaupunkt Verona
  • Blaupunkt Montreux

UK Popular equipment

The July 2000 Popular test describes a radio/cassette unit with a removable front panel. It does not identify a manufacturer, full product number, mounting interface or electrical connections. That description does not establish equivalence with a named Irish programme radio.[2]

German catalogue selections

Felicia Hatchback Sound 1.3 50 kW — 05/96–08/96: the catalogue names a Clarion radio with CD changer. This identifies a supplier/equipment selection, not a complete radio or changer model, connector, bracket, aerial or wiring product.[3]

Felicia Hatchback Blue Sky GLX 1.6 55 kW — 08/98–12/99: the catalogue names a Clarion radio. This identifies a supplier/equipment selection, not a complete radio or changer model, connector, bracket, aerial or wiring product.[4]
