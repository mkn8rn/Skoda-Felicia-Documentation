Felicia homologation gearbox specifications

The original A-5528 and A-5551 forms record different five-speed manual gearsets and final drives. Both identify a Škoda gearbox mounted transversely in the engine compartment, front-wheel drive, mechanical selection at the centre tunnel and a mechanically operated clutch with one driven plate.[1][2]

Ratios

The ratios below are the rounded values recorded in the two original forms. They are not universal ratios for every Felicia engine or national market. A-5551’s fourth and fifth ratios differ from A-5528’s, as does its final drive.[1][2]

Lubrication and drive

Both forms describe the gearcase and final drive as retaining their lubricant in the casing. They record steel jointed transverse halfshafts. Neither ratio set specifies an oil grade or fill quantity.[1][2]

A-5528 02/02 VO five-speed set corrected by 09/01 ER

In ascending forward-gear order, the form records ratios 3.000, 2.222, 1.714, 1.375 and 1.148; reverse 2.923. The corresponding written tooth pairs are 39/13, 40/18, 36/21, 33/24 and 31/27; reverse 38/13.[3]

The erratum replaces the five-speed entries of 02/02 VO. The superseded rounded ratios are not a separately established manufactured gearset. Complete gearbox, shaft, gear and selector stock numbers remain Unconfirmed.

A-5528 02/02 VO six-speed set

In ascending forward-gear order, the form records ratios 3.000, 2.125, 1.632, 1.381, 1.200 and 1.074; reverse 2.500. The corresponding written tooth pairs are 39/13, 34/16, 31/19, 29/21, 30/25 and 29/27; reverse 35/14.[4]

The first five synchronisation entries carry an optional X/dash selection; sixth and reverse carry dashes. The form does not identify every permitted physical synchroniser assembly. Complete gearbox, shaft, gear and selector stock numbers remain Unconfirmed.

A-5528 03/03 VO six-speed set

In ascending forward-gear order, the form records ratios 2.769, 2.063, 1.684, 1.450, 1.227 and 1.083; reverse 2.500. The corresponding written tooth pairs are 36/13, 33/16, 32/19, 29/20, 27/22 and 26/24; reverse 35/14.[5]

The synchronisation fields contain dashes. Reverse is selected mechanically by cable; the forward gate has 1/3/5 above 2/4/6. Complete gearbox, shaft, gear and selector stock numbers remain Unconfirmed.

A-5528 19/10 VO six-speed set

In ascending forward-gear order, the form records ratios 2.769, 2.063, 1.684, 1.450, 1.227 and 1.083; reverse 2.923. The corresponding written tooth pairs are 36/13, 33/13, 32/19, 29/20, 27/22 and 26/24; reverse 38/29 × 29/13.[6]

All synchronisation fields, including reverse, are marked X, and the form names a reverse-selection cable. The second-gear ratio 2.063 and written 33/13 tooth pair disagree arithmetically; neither is silently corrected or used to identify a replacement gear. Complete gearbox, shaft, gear and selector stock numbers remain Unconfirmed.

A-5551 01/01 VO six-speed set

In ascending forward-gear order, the form records ratios 2.769, 2.063, 1.684, 1.450, 1.227 and 1.083; reverse 2.500. The corresponding written tooth pairs are 36/13, 33/16, 32/19, 29/20, 27/22 and 26/24; reverse 35/14.[7]

The synchronisation fields contain dashes; reverse selection is mechanical by cable. Complete gearbox, shaft, gear and selector stock numbers remain Unconfirmed.

A-5551 02/02 VO six-speed set

In ascending forward-gear order, the form records ratios 2.857, 2.111, 1.750, 1.500, 1.250 and 1.115; reverse 2.444. The corresponding written tooth pairs are 40/14, 38/18, 35/20, 33/22, 30/24 and 29/26; reverse 44/18.[8]

The original option has dash synchronisation fields and cable reverse selection. Its later 07/01 ER gate correction must be distinguished from this original selector description. Complete gearbox, shaft, gear and selector stock numbers remain Unconfirmed.

Option final drives

A-5528 02/02 VO lists the following final-drive alternatives with the five-speed entries: 3.238 (68/21), 3.450 (69/20), 3.684 (70/19), 4.167 (75/18), 4.412 (75/17) and 4.563 (73/16). Its six-speed entries list 3.222 (58/18), 3.368 (64/19), 3.471 (59/17), 3.611 (65/18), 3.824 (65/17), 4.067 (61/15), 4.188 (67/16), 4.357 (61/14), 4.500 (63/14), 4.523 (68/15) and 4.692 (61/13). The written 4.523 ratio disagrees with 68/15; the arithmetic discrepancy does not establish a corrected manufactured identity.[9]

A-5551 01/01 VO separately lists 3.222 (58/18), 3.368 (64/19), 3.471 (59/17), 3.611 (65/18), 3.824 (65/17), 4.067 (61/15), 4.188 (67/16), 4.357 (61/14), 4.500 (63/14), 4.533 (68/15) and 4.692 (61/13). Similar tooth counts or rounded ratios across forms do not establish common housings, shafts or physical gear products.[10]

A-5551 05/04 VO records 4.125 (66/16), 4.250 (68/16), 4.466 (67/15), 4.600 (69/15), 4.857 (68/14), 4.928 (69/14) and 5.000 (70/14). The selected final drive and its compatible gearbox revision require their own assembly evidence; these independent alternatives are not multiplied into asserted complete cars.[11]

A-5551 corrected 02/02 VO gate

07/01 ER, valid from 1 January 1998, replaces the 02/02 VO reverse-cable selection description with a gate placing 1/3/5 on the upper row and R/2/4/6 on the lower row. It does not provide a complete replacement selector, cable or gearbox product number. This correction changes the selection description; it does not establish a road-production change or an engine-code application.[12]

N-5551 original oil cooling

The N-5551 original form explicitly excludes a gearbox oil cooler and final-drive oil coolers. Blank cooler-type fields are not component identities. This absence is confined to the original N specification and does not select cooling hardware for later VO or VK transmissions.[13]

Assembly identification

A gearset specification is not a gearbox product code. Original assembly numbers, casing revisions, clutch spline dimensions and national-market/engine-code mappings are Unconfirmed in these entries. The original forms do not establish the transmission selected for every later ET, VO or VK extension; each changed configuration needs its applicable transmission record.
