Magneton assembled starter switch 7150.872

Magneton lists 7150.872 as the assembled starter switch in the internal service components of its Felicia-associated starter assembly. This is a separately numbered service part, not an identified original Škoda car-production part.[1]

Recipient assembly

The direct manufacturer relationship is to Magneton’s identified complete starter. It is an internal service association; the parent’s vehicle list does not independently establish original installation of this loose part in a specific Felicia configuration.[1]

Physical selection

The manufacturer identifies an assembled switch. It does not give contact ratings, coil data, terminal geometry, operating travel or a separately approved substitute.[1]

Select the actual starter product and revision before substituting an internal component. A similarly named bearing, cover, switch or motor member is not sufficient evidence of physical interchangeability.
