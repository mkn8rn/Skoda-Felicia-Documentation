Felicia Pickup Freestyle — conversion and replacement requirements

A Freestyle racing conversion requires identification of the actual donor and competition build. Neither the road Pickup name nor a shared Freestyle body label establishes complete-vehicle interchangeability.[1]

Recipient configuration

Identify the donor shell and its modifications, actual engine, transmission, running gear, restraint and safety assemblies. Record the competition build, subsequent alterations and applicable class separately from a road trim. A class entry does not supply these installed identities.
