Škoda TC-6P trip computer BEA 054 003

BEA 054 003 is a complete written Škoda accessory order identity. It identifies the source-offered product, not an established as-built part in every Felicia or a complete configuration selection.[1]

Application and selection

The separately consulted undated Czech TC-6 leaflet identifies this number; it is not listed in the Slovak catalogue. Physical calibration/build selection unconfirmed.[1]

Exact connectors/mountings, production/chassis and equipment restrictions, historical installed identity and quantity remain unconfirmed. A source-offered accessory is distinct from the original part removed from a particular vehicle.

The Czech TC-6 leaflet names BEA 054 002 and BEA 054 003 as TC-6/TC-6P for Bosch Monomotronic, and groups BEA 054 004 and BEA 054 005 as TC-6/TC-6P under MPI. The Slovak catalogue instead assigns 004 to 1.6-litre and 005 to 1.9 D. These source selectors are not silently combined; exact calibration and recipient equivalence are Unconfirmed.[1][2]
