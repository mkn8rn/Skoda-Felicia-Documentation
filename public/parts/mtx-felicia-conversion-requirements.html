MTX Felicia conversion selection requirements

An MTX conversion requires identification of the actual donor and converter build. A package name, advertised power change or wheel size does not identify the installed engine, transmission, structure or complete parts set.

Recipient interfaces

Body and roof

Establish the donor shell, converter reinforcement and protective structure, roof/frame/seal attachments, seat mounts and drainage. A cabriolet conversion is not a direct replacement for an ordinary hatchback roof.

Engine, transmission and running gear

Identify the donor engine, fuel-metering system and calibration; then establish the actual head/manifold/camshaft, mounts, lubrication, gearbox and final-drive products. Match wheel/hub interfaces, suspension travel, brake geometry and recipient approval. An altered gear list does not establish a complete replacement gearbox.

Cabin and added equipment

Match the actual trim, seat and restraint arrangement, attachment geometry, clearance, glazing and electrical circuits. Child restraints require their own product/approval and recipient anchorage selection. A general BRITAX brand entry does not identify a restraint or establish compatibility. Accessory count, appearance or material alone is insufficient.
