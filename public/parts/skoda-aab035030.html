Blaupunkt Škoda Casablanca cassette radio

AAB 035 030 is a complete written Škoda accessory order identity. It identifies the source-offered product, not an established as-built part in every Felicia or a complete configuration selection.[1]

The manual describes autoreverse playback, metal/chromium-dioxide tape selection and Dolby B noise reduction. It does not establish Dolby C operation. Its FM audio-response upper limit differs from the Slovak accessory description above, which also lists Dolby B/C. These are separate source descriptions; neither difference identifies a hardware revision or proves that one specification applies to all Casablanca units.[3]

Receiver identification and security

The receiver uses KeyCard security and can retain two learned cards. The supplied KeyCard 1 can display the receiver name, type reference and serial number. With that card present, hold preset buttons 1 and 6 while switching the receiver on, then press M·L within two seconds; the identity fields cycle on the display. Press any button to leave the display. This identifies the radio, not the vehicle immobiliser or its keys.[3]

External changer and installation

The manual names the CDC-A05 external CD changer as controllable by this receiver. The changer is separate from the cassette mechanism. A radio with a CD-source button is not evidence that the changer was fitted to a particular car.[3]

Loudspeaker outputs must not be connected to vehicle ground. The operating instructions refer installation to separate connection instructions; they do not supply a complete connector pinout, fuse selection, mounting kit or vehicle loom. Recipient identification therefore requires the actual receiver revision and its applicable installation documentation.[3]
