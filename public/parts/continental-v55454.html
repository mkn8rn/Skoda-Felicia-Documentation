Continental V55454 pulley

Continental ContiTech identifies V55454 as a pulley in its 2012/2013 service programme. This is a supplier product identity, separate from a Škoda original factory part number.[1]

Declared applications

The Continental CT1012K1 programme lists this member. Vehicle applicability is limited to the selected kit and its installation; an independent standalone Felicia application or original installed identity is Unconfirmed.[1]

The catalogue intervals are supplier application ranges, not factory build, facelift or VIN boundaries. Its I/II body groups do not map a national grade, steering side or exact accessory layout. The broad 797 label does not establish Fun or Vanplus fitment; grouped 1.3 engine codes do not establish code aliases or an individual code-to-output mapping. A blank role or restriction is not proof of unrestricted fit.[1]

Associated references

The supplier associates V55454 with 028109243D, 028109243G. These references do not prove one-to-one physical identity, original installation or a chronological factory supersession.[1]
