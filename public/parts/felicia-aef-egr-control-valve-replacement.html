Felicia AEF EGR-control valve replacement

Identify the fitted EGR-control solenoid, emissions installation and pneumatic/electrical connections before choosing a replacement. An AEF engine designation alone does not identify the valve, control calibration or complete EGR assembly.[1]

Recipient identification

Confirm the actual Felicia body, engine installation, emissions equipment and existing component marking. Compare the real valve’s electrical connector, hose routing, bracket and operating role. A supplier application supports consideration of a service product; it is not evidence of original installation in a particular Dutch, Polish or other national grade.[1]
