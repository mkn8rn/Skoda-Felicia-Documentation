Felicia homologation engine construction

The original manufacturer forms for FIA A-5528 and A-5551 describe different liquid-cooled, four-cylinder in-line engines. A-5528 records a light-alloy block with wet cylinder liners; A-5551 records a cast-iron block without cylinder sleeves. Both specify one light-alloy cylinder head.[1][2]

The A-5551 kit variant 04/01 VK specifies a different connecting-rod length and different crankshaft and flywheel minimum masses. These are configuration-specific engineering descriptions, not a common part identity or a road-engine code mapping.[3]

Cylinder blocks and heads

Bore and stroke are the dimensions recorded for the original homologated engine. They are not piston clearances, worn-cylinder limits or machining allowances. The material designation light alloy does not identify an alloy grade.[1][2]

Connecting rods

The original forms and the A-5551 kit record specify a split big end. The big-end bore is measured without bearing shells; the rod length is the distance between the small-end and big-end axes. A minimum mass is a homologation limit, not the measured mass of every production rod.[1][2][3]

The kit rod shares its recorded bare big-end bore with the original A-5551 rod, but its length differs. The extension page does not specify its material. The common bore does not establish that the rods, caps, bolts or bearing shells are interchangeable.

Crankshafts and main bearings

The original forms specify one-piece forged steel crankshafts, plain main bearings and steel bearing caps. A-5528 has three main-bearing positions; A-5551 has five. A bearing-position count does not specify the number of separate bearing shells.[1][2]

The kit page specifies the manufacturing method, bearing type and diameter, cap material and minimum bare mass. It does not restate the crankshaft material or main-bearing count. The recorded bearing diameter alone is not a finished-journal tolerance, bearing-shell grade, oil-clearance specification or permission to interchange crankshafts.[3]

Flywheels

The original-form flywheel entries apply to the manual transmission. Their minimum masses include the starter ring. The kit extension records a lower minimum for the flywheel with its starter ring, without specifying the flywheel material on that page.[1][2][3]

ConfigurationMaterialMinimum assembly mass

These limits are not flywheel product numbers or bare-disc masses. Starter-ring tooth count, crankshaft attachment, clutch interfaces, balance and permissible machining must match the actual assembly; a mass value does not establish those interfaces.

A-5528 kit geometry and valves

The two A-5528 kit extensions select wet liners and different engine capacities. Neither restates the block, head or rod material, main-bearing count or flywheel material on these entries; original-form values cannot supply those fields by assumption.

Assembly selection

The original A-5528, original A-5551 and kit-extension assemblies remain distinct. Their casting numbers, full original part identifiers, material grades, heat treatments, surface finishes and manufacturing tolerances are Unconfirmed. The cited dimensions do not resolve engine-code, gearbox-product, production/VIN or national-market application. Original-form construction is not automatically assigned to an MPI evolution or kit variant.
