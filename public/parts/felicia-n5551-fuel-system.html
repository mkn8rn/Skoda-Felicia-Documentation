Felicia N-5551 tank and pump specifications

The original N-5551 form identifies a source-defined tank capacity and VDO pump architecture, without a complete product identity.

Contents

  1. Tank and filler
  2. VDO pump
  3. Application
  4. References

Tank and filler

Total fuel capacity is 42 litres. The filler is on the right rear wing. Tank quantity, material, dimensions, venting, sender and stock number remain Unconfirmed.[1]

VDO pump

The form gives one electric two-stage VDO fuel pump installed in the tank, with maximum flow 2 litres per minute. Pressure and test-speed fields are unfilled. This pump is not identified as the VDO kit-injection assembly or as any road-engine pump merely because the supplier name matches.[1]

Application

The recipient is original N-5551. Pump wiring, strainer, seals, outlet interfaces and factory/build applications require their own selected products.
