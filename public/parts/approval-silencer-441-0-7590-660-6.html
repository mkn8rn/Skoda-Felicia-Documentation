Silencer approval marking 441.0.7590-660.6

441.0.7590-660.6 is recorded as an identifying mark for a silencer in the cited Swiss Felicia approvals. The mark identifies the approved description; it does not by itself establish a manufacturer stock number, service supersession or unchanged physical design.[1]

Identification

The exact recognition string is 441.0.7590-660.6. Manufacturer, complete factory catalogue identity/suffix and documented aliases are Unconfirmed. The approval count prefix is separate from the identifying mark.[5]

Specification

The older approval writes the dimensional triple 450 × 200 × 115 beside this mark. It does not label the axes or a unit in that silencer entry; connections, pipe diameters, mounting, material and internal construction are Unconfirmed. Equal dimensional triples do not merge different recognition marks.[1]

Physical selection

The records establish approval descriptions and component counts, rather than a full original parts catalogue. A common recognition mark does not prove interchangeable joints, body routing, production revision or calibration. Alternative approval markings do not establish which unit a particular chassis received.[5]
