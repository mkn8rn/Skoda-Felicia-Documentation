UK Felicia Fun wheel and tyre specification

The UK Fun information supplied with a July 2000 customer-service letter specifies yellow alloy wheels, but gives two incompatible tyre descriptions. These are manufacturer specification statements, without wheel or tyre product identifiers.[1]

Wheel specification

The equipment list gives a 5.5J × 13 alloy wheel in yellow. Its technical panel also gives 5.5J × 13. Neither entry supplies an offset, bolt pattern, wheel-to-hub bore, wheel manufacturer or complete Škoda wheel identifier.[1]

Tyre descriptions

UK Fun tyre descriptions within the same information enclosure
Source locationStated selection
Equipment list, printed p. 8Michelin, 165/80 R13[1]
Technical panel, printed p. 9165/70 R13, reinforced[1]

The nominal section heights differ, so these entries cannot identify a single fitted tyre size. A tyre model, load/speed rating, original quantity and authority for substituting one size for the other are unconfirmed.

Selection conditions

This specification concerns the right-hand-drive UK Fun offer described in the July enclosure. It establishes neither an original wheel part number nor production, option or chassis limits. Its sizes are not automatically applicable to an ordinary commercial Pickup or another national Fun offer. The technical panel also gives vehicle dimensions that differ from the separate March UK Fun brochure; it is not a reliable basis for silently transferring either panel’s dimensions to the other offer.[1]
