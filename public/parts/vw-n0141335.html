Volkswagen fillister-head bolt — N 014 133 5

Volkswagen Classic Parts identifies N 014 133 5 as a genuine service product. Its Caddy catalogue associations do not by themselves establish the part installed in an individual Felicia-derived Caddy or another Felicia body.[1]

The catalogue size and mass fields do not define installed clearances, manufacturing tolerances or a weighing basis. The written fastener size is M5 × 12 mm. The product description associates it with a fuel pump, while the selected Caddy boards place it in controller and heated-seat groups. These associations do not identify the physical fixing point, material, strength class or tightening torque.[1][2][3]
