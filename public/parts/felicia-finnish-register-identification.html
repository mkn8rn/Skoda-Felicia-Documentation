Felicia — Finnish register identification

Register selectors describe the recorded vehicle classification. They are separate from factory product identities, original national delivery and a complete production/option specification.[1]

Identification fields

The model designation is assembled from type-register information; commercial name is the manufacturer-declared field. Variant and version are type selectors. Recorded displacement and net-power units are cm³ and kW. Forward-gear count is separate from shift type. First-use and registration dates do not identify manufacture or a parts-change boundary.[2]

Brake-actuation coding describes brake force transmission and assistance; it does not establish power steering, ABS, a caliper/master-cylinder identity or a loose-member quantity. A body classification is not a panel or superstructure product.[2]

Physical selection

Recorded displacement does not establish bore/stroke or a different factory engine. Seating capacity is not an original seat/fastener count. Blank fields do not prove absence. The supplied passenger-car chassis field contains only its first ten characters; later plant and serial positions are unspecified. A coded shell’s airbag preparation does not establish installed restraints. Original engine/gearbox, actual body revision, steering side, equipment and production/VIN fitment require separate identification.[2]
