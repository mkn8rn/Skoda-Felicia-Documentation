AEF hatchback front-end service selection

The May 2016 institute request selects front-end service references for TMBEHH613X0......, a reported 1999 Felicia 1.9 D LX at 47 kW. The VIN legend identifies an AEF-class LX hatchback with Z shell preparation; this is not a complete original equipment or parts specification.[1][2]

Recipient and interfaces

Body-panel and lamp selection depends on actual panel/front-end revision, attachment and closure geometry, finish, electrical/aiming equipment and traffic/beam requirements. Engine class or nominal 1999 alone does not identify all of them. Full producer/finish variants, dimensions, source assembly callouts, original quantity and factory option/production/VIN application are Unconfirmed.[1][2]
