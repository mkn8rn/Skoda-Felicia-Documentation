Felicia lighting replacement

Replacement headlamps, indicator lamps and fog lamps must match the actual body/front-end, side, mounting, optical/traffic function, aiming equipment and electrical connections. Supplier model/date labels and cross-references assist selection without identifying an original factory component or a complete conversion.[1]

Recipient identification

TYC’s Felicia I and II entries cover its 6U1/6U5 body cohorts. Their supplier ranges overlap in early 1998; a model-year or offer date alone does not select a lamp. Actual front-end revision, traffic/beam and aiming architecture remain separate conditions. Pickup, Fun and Vanplus applications are not assigned from these passenger-body entries.[1]

Interfaces and operating requirements

Compare casing and body attachment, side-specific lens/beam, bulb and connection requirements, and the fitted adjustment linkage or drive. The catalogue identifies electric-control headlamp products without proving a motor is included. The source does not resolve the recipient’s voltage, beam direction, current adjustment mechanism or loose delivery contents. A new control architecture requires more than an equal reference number.[1]
