Type 002 Caddy Pickup transmission family

Volkswagen’s July 1996 specification identifies the Škoda-developed type 002 manual transmission for both Caddy Pickup engine selections.

Contents

  1. Transmission and drive architecture
  2. Installation and control interfaces
  3. Documented applications
  4. References

Transmission and drive architecture

The five-speed unit has a two-piece aluminium casing and a shared oil fill for the gearbox and final drive. Its inner constant-velocity joints use a tripod arrangement with a pin at the inner joint. The source’s comparison with Sharan 006 does not establish interchangeable joints.[1]

Installation and control interfaces

A flange between engine and transmission accommodates their different mounting-hole patterns. Gear selection uses a rod and a linkage stabilizer. Clutch actuation is mechanical by cable, and the installation includes a pendulum support.[1]

Complete gearbox, flange, clutch, shaft and joint identities; selected ratios/final drive; bearing dimensions; oil grade/capacity; mounting geometry; and build limits remain Unconfirmed. Type 002 identifies a family rather than a complete assembly and suffix.
