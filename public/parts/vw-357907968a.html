Volkswagen six-pin flat-contact housing — 357 907 968 A

Volkswagen Classic Parts identifies 357 907 968 A as six-pin flat-contact housing, with quality class Genuine Volkswagen Quality. Its current service associations do not establish historical original installation in a particular Felicia-derived Caddy.[1]
