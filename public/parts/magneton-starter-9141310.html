Magneton starter 9141310

9141310 is Magneton’s complete starter product identifier. Its manufacturer catalogue associates it with Felicia service applications and separately numbered internal components. Those supplier associations do not identify a factory-installed starter on an individual Felicia.[1]

Supplier cross-references

The record associates this starter with Škoda references 6U0911023B, 443115142350 and 11590400; it also lists Volkswagen references 6U0911023, 6U0911023A, 6U0911023AX, 6U0911023B and 6U0911023X. These are manufacturer-catalogue cross-references, not proof that all suffixes or brands identify one physical assembly. The record supplies no factory supersession sequence or original-build application for them.[1]

Physical and original-fitment limits

Physical selection requires the actual gearbox attachment, pilot and mounting geometry, flywheel/ring-gear engagement, electrical connections and clearance. A power-rating match or an engine-family match is insufficient. The internal part list supplies no bearing A/B location map, per-part dimensions, winding specifications, brush geometry, service wear limits or original-car quantities.[1]
