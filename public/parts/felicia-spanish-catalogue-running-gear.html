Transmission

Each entry specifies a five-speed manual transmission and front-wheel drive. The catalogue does not identify a manufacturer gearbox product, internal revision or factory selection range.[1]

Suspension

The listed front suspension is a MacPherson layout with coil springs. The rear is described as a trailing-wheel arrangement with a torsional element and coil springs. Those classes do not select spring rates, axle-load variants, dampers, bushes or exact structural parts.[1]

Brakes

The entries give front disc and rear drum brakes, with a nominal front disc diameter of 236 mm. Rear drum diameters are given by application below. These are catalogue dimensions, not wear limits or machining instructions. They do not resolve disc thickness, ventilation, hub interfaces, rear cylinder bore or ABS-specific products.[1]

Steering

The specification entries name rack-and-pinion steering but leave assistance type unspecified. A separate positive standard-equipment entry can establish assistance for its own grade, without identifying the rack, pump, pipework or steering-side variant.[1]

Wheels and tyres

Front and rear nominal tyre sizes are 165/70 R13; rim descriptions give 4.5 × 13. No rim-profile letter, offset, complete wheel number, tyre brand or load/speed rating is supplied. A separately priced alloy-wheel choice does not identify the same rim product as the base description.[1]

Physical selection

Full original identifiers and suffixes, required quantities/positions, exact engine/gearbox/body revision and factory equipment/production/VIN application remain unconfirmed. Numerical similarities do not establish equivalence with FIA homologation assemblies, German wheel approvals or another market’s products.

November 1994 launch comparison

Motor16’s 135i/136i engine columns give five-speed manual transmission, front-wheel drive, rack steering, front disc brakes and rear drum brakes. The front suspension is described as independent and the rear as semi-independent. These labels do not establish assistance, a gearbox/rack/brake product, dimensions, wheel fitment or equivalence with the later catalogue applications.[9]

February 1995 GLX running gear and equipment

The test specification gives five-speed manual transmission and front-wheel drive. Its published road speeds at 1000 engine rpm are 7.5, 13.0, 19.7, 26.9 and 34.6 km/h in gears one to five; these are not dimensionless gearbox ratios. Rack steering is described with 3.7 turns from lock to lock and a 10.5 m turning circle, without an explicit assistance variant. Front brakes are discs and rear brakes drums; the original panel does not independently specify a ventilated front product. Front suspension is labelled independent and rear suspension a torsional axle. The nominal tyre size is 165/70 R13 and the panel describes steel 4.5 × 13 rims, whereas test photographs show alloy road wheels. No photographed wheel identity/size or correspondence with the nominal rim is established. The nominal fuel-tank figure is 42 L, not a verified usable or refill volume.[10]

February 1995 GLX equipment classes

The panel identifies central locking, split rear seating, interior luggage release, a rear wiper, front fog lamps, rear head restraints, tachometer, coolant-temperature indication, battery-charge indication and rear-passenger ventilation outlets. Individual actuators, latch/release mechanisms, lamp/control products, restraint positions, instruments and outlet/duct identities are Unconfirmed. Its option discussion includes air conditioning, ABS, airbags, alloy wheels and electric windows; the ABS panel’s negative entry and the separately listed ABS option do not prove universal absence or a fitted ABS assembly. A priced/optional item or photograph does not establish an original option code or complete factory fitment range.[11]

April 1995 test recap qualification

The issue 600 recap of test 591 describes ventilated front discs, while the original panel states only front discs. The recap is not independent evidence of a new factory brake revision, fitted disc geometry or a particular original product. Its wheel and torque summaries do not identify equivalent physical parts.[12]

May 1995 price-list equipment

Central locking is marked as standard for the GLXi offer; the LXi/LXi Plus entries do not independently identify that fitted system. The same three entries price air conditioning, metallic paint, ABS, alloy wheels and a driver airbag. None selects a fitted controller/actuator, hydraulic unit, rim, restraint product, paint code or combined option package. Dash/blank fields do not identify installed equipment or manufacturer option absence.[13]

June 1995 Combi launch component classes

The three Combi columns specify five-speed manual transmission, front-wheel drive, rack steering, front disc/rear drum brakes and independent front/semi-independent rear suspension. Each gives a nominal 42 L tank. The body discussion identifies roof-carrier rails, asymmetric folding and removable rear seating, luggage release from the driver’s position and tool compartments at the luggage sides. These descriptions do not identify complete assemblies, original per-position quantities or an SLX/engine-specific product. The future steering-assistance discussion is not a production cutoff or a fitted system selector. ABS/airbags discussed by version are not a proven installed package.[14]
