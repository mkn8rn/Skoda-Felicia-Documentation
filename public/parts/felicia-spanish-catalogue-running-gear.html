Felicia Spanish catalogue running gear

The seven Spanish catalogue records describe shared running-gear classes. These descriptions do not establish identical physical assemblies or interchangeable products across grades or bodies.

Transmission

Each entry specifies a five-speed manual transmission and front-wheel drive. The catalogue does not identify a manufacturer gearbox product, internal revision or factory selection range.[1]

Suspension

The listed front suspension is a MacPherson layout with coil springs. The rear is described as a trailing-wheel arrangement with a torsional element and coil springs. Those classes do not select spring rates, axle-load variants, dampers, bushes or exact structural parts.[1]

Brakes

The entries give front disc and rear drum brakes, with a nominal front disc diameter of 236 mm. Rear drum diameters are given by application below. These are catalogue dimensions, not wear limits or machining instructions. They do not resolve disc thickness, ventilation, hub interfaces, rear cylinder bore or ABS-specific products.[1]

Steering

The specification entries name rack-and-pinion steering but leave assistance type unspecified. A separate positive standard-equipment entry can establish assistance for its own grade, without identifying the rack, pump, pipework or steering-side variant.[1]

Wheels and tyres

Front and rear nominal tyre sizes are 165/70 R13; rim descriptions give 4.5 × 13. No rim-profile letter, offset, complete wheel number, tyre brand or load/speed rating is supplied. A separately priced alloy-wheel choice does not identify the same rim product as the base description.[1]

Full original identifiers and suffixes, required quantities/positions, exact engine/gearbox/body revision and factory equipment/production/VIN application remain unconfirmed.
