Felicia brake-component replacement

A brake replacement must match the actual axle, hub, caliper or drum assembly and the vehicle’s hydraulic and ABS configuration.

Contents

  1. Recipient requirements
  2. Documented alternatives
  3. See also
  4. References

Recipient requirements

For a front disc, match hub centring, mounting pattern, disc offset/height and caliper clearance. For pads, match the caliper family, pad shape, retention and warning equipment. For a rear drum, match hub/bearing construction, brake-shoe width and working diameter.

Supplier vehicle lists do not resolve every hydraulic split. Do not choose a rear wheel cylinder from one of several bore or brake-system alternatives solely because the body and engine output match.
