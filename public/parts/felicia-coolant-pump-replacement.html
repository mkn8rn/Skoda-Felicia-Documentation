Felicia coolant-pump replacement

Identify the actual coolant pump, drive and engine installation before selecting a replacement. Cooling function alone does not establish the required pulley, mounting, seal or hydraulic interfaces.[1]

Recipient identification

The LASO programme separates Mono-Motronic, carburettor and air-conditioning applications, alongside a further Felicia pump entry without a specific engine restriction. Its broad vehicle dates do not identify a factory engine, option combination or build change. Confirm the fitted engine and cooling/accessory-drive assembly rather than selecting from a grade or nominal displacement alone.[1]
