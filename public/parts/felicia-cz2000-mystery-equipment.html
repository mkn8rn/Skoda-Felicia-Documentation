Felicia Mystery equipment in the May 2000 offer

The contemporary offer describes two passenger bodies, four powertrains, two metallic finishes and two equipment packages. A finish or equipment name does not identify an original physical product.[1]

Powertrain descriptions

Hatchback and Combi are offered with 1.3 MPI at 40 or 50 kW, 1.6 MPI at 55 kW, or 1.9 D at 47 kW. Complete engine/gearbox products, calibration codes and manufacturing/serial boundaries are Unconfirmed.[1]

Colour and interior pairing

The written colour alternatives are Májová zelená (green metallic) and Esprit (violet metallic). Astro Maroni cloth has a corresponding green or violet combination, with textile floor mats coordinated to the interior. Paint formulas, upholstery patterns and full colour/product codes remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Wheel-package distinction

The two package classes distinguish 13-inch steel wheels with full covers from 13-inch Borbet alloy wheels. Formal package names, other package-specific differences, rim width/offset/profile, tyre/approval selection, spare supply and actual original loose-part quantities remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Exterior equipment

Listed exterior equipment includes a rear roof spoiler incorporating a third stop lamp, front fog lamps, tinted glazing with a green upper windscreen strip, and silver exhaust-outlet trim. The roof carrier is confined to Combi. Full spoiler/lamp/glass/trim/carrier products and attachment variants remain Unconfirmed.[1]

Interior and electrical equipment

The list includes a driver front airbag, remote central locking with an alarm, electric front windows and electrically adjusted/heated exterior mirrors. A centre console has a removable storage box; front seats are height-adjustable, rear seating is split, and the rear wiper has intermittent operation.[1]

Audio preparation includes an aerial and two speakers for hatchback or four for Combi. These are preparation complement counts, rather than a fitted radio identity or original loose-product quantity/position map. Further extras are available without a complete ordering matrix in this record.[1]

Physical selection

The common list names equipment classes, not a complete original inventory. Full identifiers, quantities, controller/wiring/trim revisions and option/build/VIN selectors remain Unconfirmed. A driver airbag does not establish passenger/side-airbag absence or belt-pretensioner equipment. Report date and starting prices are not production cutoffs.
