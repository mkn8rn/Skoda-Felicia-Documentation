VDO multipoint injection for the A-5528 Felicia kit

A-5528 kit extension 18/02 VK specifies electronically metered VDO multipoint injection. This is the extension’s competition metering specification, not a road-car engine code or an identified replacement assembly.[1]

  2. Application and selection
  3. See also
  4. References

Metering and control

The form records a 55 ± 0.25 mm throttle passage, four effective fuel outlets and manifold injection. It names intake-air-temperature and absolute-pressure inputs, a controller and a flanged throttle body.[1]

The system diagram additionally identifies coolant-temperature and throttle-position sensing, a Hall/speed sensor, battery, ignition module, spark plugs, high-tension leads, fuel rail, pressure regulator and petrol pump. An effective-outlet count does not establish four identical production injector assemblies.[1]

Application and selection

The application is A-5528 kit 18/02 VK, valid from 1 January 1999. Full controller, throttle-body, injector and sensor identities, flow, pressure, wiring, calibration and production selection are Unconfirmed. VDO and SIMOS 2P names do not establish equivalence, and this specification does not prove a direct road-system replacement.
