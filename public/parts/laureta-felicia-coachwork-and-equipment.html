J. Laureta coachwork and special-purpose equipment

J. Laureta vehicles use an extended Felicia Pickup basis and purpose-specific coachwork. The builder’s fabrication account and the separate illustrated ambulance, service body and workshop interior describe different assemblies; they do not identify one interchangeable body product.[1]

Maker construction account

The 2020 publication describes a wheelbase extension of 600 mm. Jaroslav Jelínek recalls buying most components from Škoda, having skeletons welded and extended body sides and floor sections pressed at a specialist in Uherské Hradiště, passing the bodies through a protective cataphoretic bath at Avia in Letňany, and completing the vehicles at his Mladá Boleslav workshop. This is a retrospective programme-level account, not a body-form-specific drawing or parts application.[1]

Material grades, sheet thicknesses, weld/joint details, treatment chemistry, fixture dimensions, payload and vehicle-specific structural selection are Unconfirmed. An extension dimension does not establish a complete shell, final wheelbase or panel identity.

Physical selection

HP-0026 names an extended Laureta body-replacement conversion; it does not identify every J. Laureta manufacturing or special-purpose assembly.[5]
