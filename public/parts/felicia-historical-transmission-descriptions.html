Felicia historical transmission descriptions

Škoda’s timeline supplies gear-count notation for three separately named vehicles. These descriptions do not identify one shared physical gearbox.[1]

The notation describes forward gears and reverse. It does not specify the selector method, driven axle, ratio set, final drive, manufacturer gearbox product or reverse-selection hardware.[1]

Assembly selection

A gear count cannot select a 14S or type 002 assembly, an FIA option gearset, a clutch, casing, shaft or mounting product. Physical identification and vehicle-specific application remain Unconfirmed; identical notation is not evidence of interchangeability.
