Original parts

Original parts indexes components documented as factory equipment or genuine factory service replacements for the Felicia. Assembly descriptions, identifiers, revisions, and all evidenced applications belong in the component’s shared article.

The production-engine overview is available. Individual original-part numbers, factory supersessions, and complete application tables have not yet been documented.

Engine assemblies

The engine overview records the production families and their mechanical construction. Detailed original component identities and application limits remain to be established.

  • 1.3-litre petrol — 135B, 135M, 136B, and 136M.
  • 1.6-litre petrol — AEE.
  • 1.9-litre diesel — AEF.

Other systems

Original-part articles for fuel and exhaust, cooling and lubrication, transmission, suspension and steering, brakes and wheels, body and interior, electrical systems, heating and ventilation, and consumables remain unwritten. Their scope is recorded in the system index.

Original-part identification

An entry needs evidence of factory fitment or a documented genuine service application. Record the complete manufacturer identifier, including its revision suffix; distinguish it from a seller’s stock code and a diagram callout. A supplier’s name or the term OEM alone does not establish factory fitment.

Factory substitutions and supersessions must retain their stated conditions. Body style, engine and gearbox, production or chassis limits, market, steering side, and equipment belong in the shared article’s applications. A component fitted to several versions is documented once. If it also has an evidenced application in Compatible parts, both indexes link to that same article.
