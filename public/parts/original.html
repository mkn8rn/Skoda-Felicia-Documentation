Original parts

Original parts are the Felicia’s factory-fitted components and genuine factory service replacements.

Original-part selection distinguishes each component position, its physical interfaces and its production application. A component name is not an OE identifier. Unconfirmed original identity must not be replaced by an aftermarket stock code or a diagram position. Equipment-dependent positions, including cup holders and optional air-conditioning or safety equipment, do not assert fitment in every Felicia.

Part identification and compatibility

A manufacturer’s part number identifies a component. A diagram callout identifies its position in an assembly illustration, while a seller’s stock code identifies an inventory entry. Revision letters form part of the identifier. A similar appearance or a shared model name does not establish interchangeability.[1]

Fitment depends on the applicable body style, engine and gearbox codes, production or chassis-number limits, market, steering side, and equipment. A supplier’s name or an OEM label alone does not establish factory fitment.
