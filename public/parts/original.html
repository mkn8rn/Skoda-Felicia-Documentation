Original parts

Original parts indexes components documented as factory equipment or genuine factory service replacements for the Felicia. Assembly descriptions, identifiers, revisions, and all evidenced applications belong in the component’s shared article.

The production-engine overview is available. Individual component articles, original-part numbers, assembly diagrams, factory supersessions, and complete application tables remain unwritten. The system sections below record these gaps.

Shared parts and model applications

Each part has one shared article, regardless of how many Felicia versions use it. Model, maintenance, and repair articles link to the same part page. A component belonging to several systems is linked from each system without duplicating its article.

The part article records its names, documented identifiers, and applicable versions together. Different model applications belong in its applications table. Confirmed aliases and supersessions help identify an existing article; similar names or appearances alone do not establish that two parts are the same.

Part identification and compatibility

An original-part entry needs evidence of factory fitment or a documented genuine service application. A supplier’s name or the term OEM alone does not establish factory fitment. Factory substitutions and supersessions retain their stated application conditions.

Part articles should distinguish the manufacturer’s part number from a diagram callout or a seller’s stock code. Record the complete identifier, including revision letters, and the source’s application conditions. An illustrated assembly can help identify a component, but a similar appearance or a shared model name does not establish interchangeability.[1]

Compatibility entries must state the relevant body style, engine and gearbox codes, production or chassis-number limits, market, steering side, and equipment where those conditions apply. Preserve documented substitutions and supersessions as separate, cited claims.
