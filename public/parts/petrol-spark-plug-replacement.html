Felicia petrol spark-plug replacement

A Felicia petrol spark plug must match the engine’s cylinder-head seating, thread and reach, heat range, ignition connection and specified electrode arrangement. An engine displacement alone does not identify the replacement.

Recipient requirements

Separate the 1.3 SPI and MPI configurations from the 1.6 AEE application. Check the original plug identity and the actual cylinder-head seat; a suffix is part of the product designation. Gap figures belong to the specified product/application, not every plug sold for the car.

The service catalogues below do not establish a factory production plug or an original Škoda supersession. BRISK’s code notation and the technical B/M identifiers are not interchangeable without a verified mapping.

Technical-manual service selections

Haynes 3505 distinguishes the following service selections. These identify written plug types and their service gaps; they do not establish production-installed brands, full manufacturer stock numbers or supersession to the later DC+/LTC+ products.

The AMG-specific selection must not be assigned to every 1.3 engine. The manual’s MPI description does not by itself resolve a national grade, a B/M code mapping or an individual car’s original plug identity.
