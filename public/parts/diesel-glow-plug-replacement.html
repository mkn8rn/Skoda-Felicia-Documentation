Felicia diesel glow-plug replacement

Glow-plug selection must preserve the diesel engine’s preheating circuit and the plug’s seating and reach.

Contents

  1. Recipient requirements
  2. Documented alternatives
  3. See also
  4. References

Recipient requirements

Match the actual engine, preheating supply, installed plug identity, cylinder-head thread and tip clearance. A matching manufacturer name or visible connector does not establish the electrical rating or insertion depth.

Haynes’s cited service specification supplies a Bosch product identity but no substitute dimensions or supersession. A different glow-plug number remains unconfirmed until those conditions and the application are established.
