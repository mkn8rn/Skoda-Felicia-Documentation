Valve, guide and stem-seal replacement

A replacement valve, guide or stem seal must match the actual cylinder head and its installed valve gear. Engine code alone does not establish the selected head revision, guide bore, valve-stem geometry or seal arrangement.

Recipient identification and interfaces

Identify the engine and cylinder-head assembly, then establish the fitted valve’s head/stem/length, seat, cotter and spring-retainer interfaces. Guide selection also requires the head bore, guide form and installation position; seal selection requires the stem/guide interface and the applicable engine-number conditions. Supplier nominal dimensions do not supply wear, machining or assembly tolerances. Catalogue cross-references are not factory installation or supersession proof.[1]
