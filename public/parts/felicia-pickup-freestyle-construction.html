Felicia Pickup Freestyle — source-defined racing construction

The Felicia-based Freestyle cup car is a competition conversion. The circuit operator identifies enlarged engine capacity and aerodynamic load-platform bodywork; these are construction classes rather than complete physical product identities.[1]

It does not identify a road-engine code, the earlier Favorit engine or the power unit fitted to any later hill-climb entry. The source reports a maximum speed of 205 km/h; that performance statement is not a service or operating limit.[1]

Assembly selection

A shared Freestyle name does not establish identical engines, transmissions, bodywork or race preparation. Individual build records must establish the actual parts, mounting, revision and competition configuration. The separately listed Tuning Kabát body product has no established identity with this historic Air Design assembly.
