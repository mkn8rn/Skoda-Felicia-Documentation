Felicia market configurations

Trim names and engine pairings varied by country and offer date. Each dated offer applies to its stated market. Factory restraint choices also distinguish cars with the same body, grade and engine; the Czech 1998 airbag offers and Dutch 1999 grade matrix use different equipment combinations.[1][2]

The Dutch January 1995 LXi hatchback had the 40 kW single-point-injection engine. The 1999 Dutch LXi used the 50 kW multipoint-injection engine. A trim suffix such as “i” therefore does not define an engine output across countries and years.[3][2]
