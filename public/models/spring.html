Škoda Felicia Spring

Spring is a named Felicia limited edition. Škoda’s Polish retrospective lists the edition, while a Hungarian-language vehicle catalogue identifies a 1996 five-door 1.3 50 kW hatchback.[1][2]

Identification

The five-door passenger hatchback uses factory body family Š 791. The Spring name and a catalogue year do not select a full engine code, national approval or original equipment/build limits.[3]

Recognition features

The catalogue identifies alloy wheels, central locking, a split rear seat and head restraints. Exterior mirrors are adjustable from inside; the description does not identify an electrical drive.[2]
