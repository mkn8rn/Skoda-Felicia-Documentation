Škoda Felicia Spring

Felicia Spring was a named limited series in the Škoda Felicia range. The Polish importer later listed Spring among the special Felicia editions, and a 1996 Hungarian market specification records a five-door Felicia 1.3 Spring with 50 kW output.[1][2]

Dated configuration

The Hungarian model catalogue labels Spring as a 1996 five-door hatchback with a 1,289 cm³ petrol engine rated at 50 kW. It specifies front-wheel drive and a five-speed manual gearbox. This dated offer identifies the named model for that market and year; the car’s factory body type remains Š 791.[2][3]

Equipment

The same specification lists alloy wheels, central locking, a split rear seat, four head restraints and electrically adjustable exterior mirrors. These equipment features accompanied the Felicia’s existing body and powertrain; Spring did not introduce a separate body or engine type.[2]

Identification

The powertrain is a 1.3-litre 50 kW petrol version. The model name identifies the market series, Š 791 identifies the hatchback body, and the engine stamp identifies the installed power unit.[3]
