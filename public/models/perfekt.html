Škoda Felicia Perfekt

Perfekt, also written Perfect in the importer’s earlier list, is a Polish-market Felicia edition.[1][2]

Contents

  1. Dated offer identification
  2. Recognition equipment
  3. See also
  4. References

Dated offer identification

The 1999 Polish report distinguishes 1.3 MPI 50 kW and AEE 1.6 MPI 55 kW offers.[3]

Recognition equipment

The period account identifies ABS, four airbags, an alarm and belt pretensioners.[3]
