Škoda Felicia Perfekt

Felicia Perfekt was a Polish-market special edition. Škoda Polska’s 2014 history uses the spelling “Perfekt”; its 2003 limited-series list writes “Perfect.” A 1999 Polish report records 1.3-litre 50 kW and 1.6-litre 55 kW petrol offers under the Perfekt name.[1][2][3]

Name and market

The importer classifies Perfekt among the Felicia editions prepared for the Polish market. “Perfect” is a spelling variant used in another Škoda importer list for this series; the two spellings do not establish separate models.[1][2]

Dated powertrains

A Polish 1999 report associates Perfekt with a 1.3-litre petrol engine rated at 50 kW and a 1.6-litre petrol engine rated at 55 kW. The 1.3 MPI 50 kW and AEE 1.6 MPI 55 kW pairings are listed for the edition.[3]

Safety equipment

The contemporary Polish account associates the edition with ABS, four airbags, an alarm and belt pretensioners. These systems form the reported Perfekt equipment package; the engine and body codes continue to specify the Felicia underneath it.[3]
