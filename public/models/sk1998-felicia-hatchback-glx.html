Felicia hatchback GLX — 4/98 programme

The manufacturer programme identifies hatchback GLX by its passenger body and grade. Engine and fitted-restraint choices distinguish more specific offers.[1]

Recognition

Felicia and Felicia Combi are separately printed passenger-body columns. LX, LX special and GLX are distinct programme grades; a badge does not by itself identify engine, fitted extras or a delivery-country/build selection. The 4/98 imprint is a publication date.[1]
