Škoda Felicia — Greece, June 1996

The June 1996 Greek report distinguishes two tested GLXi 1.6 passenger bodies, a priced Combi LXi 1.6i offer and a separately tested ordinary 1.3 Pickup. ABS and steering assistance distinguish the two tested GLXi configurations; their equipment is not a selector for the LXi or Pickup.[1]
