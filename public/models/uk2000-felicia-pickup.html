Škoda Felicia Pickup — UK, February 2000

The February 2000 Škoda UK brochure identifies the ordinary commercial Pickup, with three named body finishes. It is distinct from the recreational Fun.[1]

Contents

Colour configurations

  • Dove White — T7T7
  • Frisian Green — 8Y8Y
  • Paprika Red — 3M3M

Identification limits

The brochure does not map the colours to engine, gearbox, steering-side, equipment-grade or chassis selections.[1]
