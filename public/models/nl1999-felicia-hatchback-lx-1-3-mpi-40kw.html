Škoda Felicia LX 1.3 MPI 40 kW (Netherlands, 1999)

Pon Mobiel’s 1999 Dutch Felicia specification lists the LX hatchback with the 1.3-litre multipoint-injection petrol engine at 40 kW. This is one exact market offer; the same badge appears with other engines and equipment grades in the same list.[1]

Factory specification

Dutch-market Felicia LX hatchback, 1999 specification
BodyFive-door hatchback, type Š 791[3]
Engine1,289 cm³ petrol, code 135M
Output40 kW
Fuel systemMultipoint injection (MPI)
Airbag equipmentNot available in this Dutch specification[1]
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive
Market and dateNetherlands; 1999 importer specification

Engine code 135M distinguishes the lower-output multipoint-injection version from single-point 135B and the 50 kW 136M. The shared 1.3-litre engine article describes the code family and construction.[1][2]

Equipment grade

In this brochure LX identifies the basic equipment package. The separate GLX grades add a visibly different factory fit, including body-side mouldings, electrically adjustable body-coloured mirrors, tinted glass, front airbags and central locking. LX 1.3 MPI 40 kW remains distinct from the other LX engine variants listed for this body.
