Škoda Felicia Bohemia

The Felicia Bohemia was a UK special edition offered as a hatchback and an estate. Škoda UK’s May 1996 brochure specifies a 1.3-litre engine advertised at 68 bhp and a five-speed gearbox in both offers. Its equipment package combined alloy wheels, body-colour exterior fittings, electric front windows and remote-controlled security equipment.[1]

Equipment

The brochure identifies alloy wheels with locking wheel hardware; colour-matched front and rear bumpers, mirrors and a rear spoiler incorporating a brake lamp; electric front windows; an ultrasonic alarm with remote central locking; an engine immobiliser; cloth seat upholstery; and a Blaupunkt stereo radio/cassette. These are specified edition features, rather than inferred equipment from another Felicia grade.[1]

Colours

The five listed finishes are Romantic Red, Royal Blue, Pearl White, Arctic Blue and Atlantic Green. The brochure gives colour names, without factory paint codes or colour-specific part identifiers.[1]

Applicability

The May 1996 imprint identifies Škoda UK. The offer date does not establish production or chassis boundaries. The brochure’s 68 bhp designation is retained as advertised; it does not supply a kW value, engine code, injection-system designation, gearbox product or original equipment part numbers. Sunroof, restraint and roof-rail fitment are not established by the enumerated Bohemia equipment.[1]
