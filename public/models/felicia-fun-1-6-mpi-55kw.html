Škoda Felicia Fun 1.6 MPI 55 kW

Škoda’s Fun history distinguishes a 1,598 cm³ multipoint-injection petrol version rated at 55 kW. The same account reports a top speed of 163 km/h, average combined consumption of 7.3 L/100 km under the then-applicable test standard, and a stated mass of 1,035 kg with a maximum payload of 445 kg.[1]

Identification limits

The Fun-specific account identifies the 1.6 MPI by displacement, injection system and output, but does not state an engine code, gearbox code, market-order code or chassis/VIN limit. The reported mass and payload are not accompanied by a weighing standard or a detailed equipment breakdown, so they should not be used to infer another version’s fitment.[1]

German-market offer

Škoda Auto Deutschland describes the Fun in Germany with a general 1.6 MPI pairing in its account of the 1999 offer. The retrospective gives no engine code or output for German builds, lists partial-leather upholstery as an option, and reports a new-car price of about €14,000 for 1999. Felicia Fun 1.6 MPI, Germany 1999.[3]
