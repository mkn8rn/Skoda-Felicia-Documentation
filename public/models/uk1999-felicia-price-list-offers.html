Felicia UK price-list offers — July and December 1999

The dated UK price lists distinguish hatchback and estate offers by sales grade. A grade, body and publication date together identify an offer; they do not specify an engine code or production interval.[1][2]

Offer recognition

The July list names L, LXi and GLXi hatchbacks and LXi, GLi D and GLXi estates. The December list also prices an LX D hatchback. A blank price cell does not establish that a configuration was never produced or sold at another date.[1][2]

The leaflets warn that illustrations can show left-hand-drive cars and optional equipment. A photograph therefore does not establish the steering side or fitted equipment of a priced UK offer.[1][2]
