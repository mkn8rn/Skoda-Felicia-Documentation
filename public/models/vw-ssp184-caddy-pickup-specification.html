Caddy Pickup July 1996 manufacturer selections

SSP 184 identifies the Volkswagen-branded two-seat commercial pickup by its separate load platform and AEE/AEF engine-code selections. Its July 1996 technical-status imprint is not a national grade, steering-side code or factory build cutoff.[1]
