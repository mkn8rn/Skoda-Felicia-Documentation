Škoda Felicia Vanplus

Felicia Vanplus was the factory commercial derivative of the Combi estate, introduced in 1995. Its enclosed load space and selectable two- or five-seat configuration distinguished it from the passenger Combi and the open-bed Pickup.[1][2]

Model identity

Škoda grouped Vanplus within the Felicia commercial range and began building it at Kvasiny in August 1995. Later vehicle type listings identify the body as type 795 VANPLUS, adjacent to passenger Combi type 795. “Vanplus” is therefore a commercial body derivative name, not an extra engine or equipment trim applied to every Combi.[1][3]

Body and seating

The Vanplus uses the estate’s enclosed rear body with a commercial cabin/load-space arrangement. Period Škoda market-history material describes versions for two occupants and for five, so seating count is part of the specific Vanplus configuration rather than a reliable model name by itself. This differs from the Pickup’s open platform and the Fun’s flexible four-seat leisure cabin.[2]

Powertrain and revision

Vanplus belongs to the Felicia range that used 1.3-litre petrol, 1.6 MPI petrol and 1.9 D diesel engines. The German approval schedule lists Vanplus as its own commercial body type but groups it with other Felicia types in some engine-power tables. A grouped power class does not by itself assign an engine to Vanplus.[1]

The 1998 Felicia facelift was applied during the family’s production run. It altered the front styling and body length but did not erase the distinction between the passenger 795 Combi and commercial 795 Vanplus.[4]
