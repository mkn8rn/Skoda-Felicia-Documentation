Škoda Felicia Vanplus

Felicia Vanplus was the factory commercial derivative of the Combi estate, introduced in 1995. Its enclosed load space and two- or five-seat factory configurations distinguished it from the passenger Combi and the open-bed Pickup.[1][2]

Model identity

Škoda’s 2019 history dates Vanplus production at Kvasiny from August 1995; its 2014 retrospective places commercial-body production at Vrchlabí without dating that factory attribution. Neither source establishes whether production moved or overlapped between plants, or which builds are associated with each location. They do not date the two seating layouts separately. Later type records identify the commercial estate as 795 VANPLUS, distinct from passenger Combi type 795.[1][3]

Body and seating

The Vanplus uses the estate’s enclosed rear body with a commercial cabin/load-space arrangement. Škoda’s 2014 Felicia retrospective reports two-seat and five-seat Vanplus forms. Seating count distinguishes the cabin layouts, but the retrospective does not associate them with specific engines, markets or build ranges. This differs from the Pickup’s open platform and the Fun’s flexible four-seat leisure cabin.[2]

A 2001 technical account groups the Combi and Vanplus as using extended body shells, distinct from the short hatchback and two-seat Pickup. It gives an adapted-shell start date for the Combi and Pickup, but does not assign that same production date or structural parts limit to Vanplus. The Vanplus body therefore cannot be classified by applying the Combi chronology alone.[5]

Seating configurations

The Vanplus was offered with two or five seats: the two-seat version and the five-seat version. The type schedule does not give each layout a separate type code.

Powertrain and revision

Felicia family sources record 1.3-litre petrol, 1.6 MPI petrol and 1.9 D diesel engines, but the cited approval summary does not map those powertrains to either Vanplus seating layout. A family-wide engine list therefore does not establish a Vanplus engine combination.[1]

Škoda dates the Felicia family facelift to February 1998. The retrospective does not identify the Vanplus body’s styling or structural changes, nor give a Vanplus chassis-number limit for the update. Its 795 VANPLUS designation does not by itself identify pre- and post-facelift parts fitment.[4]
