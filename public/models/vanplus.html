Škoda Felicia Vanplus

Felicia Vanplus is the commercial estate derivative. Its enclosed rear body and two- or five-seat layouts distinguish it from the passenger Combi and open-platform Pickup.[1]

Identification

The approval designation is 795 VANPLUS, distinct from passenger Combi type 795. The extended enclosed rear body differs from the open Pickup load platform and Fun’s movable 2+2 cabin. Seating count separates the two Vanplus cabin layouts; the approval schedule does not give them separate body codes.[2][1]
