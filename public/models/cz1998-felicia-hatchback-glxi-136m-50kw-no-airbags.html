Škoda Felicia GLXi Hatchback 1.3 MPI 50 kW (Czech Republic, 1998) without airbags

This is the Czech-market five-door Felicia hatchback configuration without optional airbags in the GLX line, specified in the manufacturer's February 1998 model and price data. The record distinguishes the body, grade and engine rating; its dated offer identifies the market configuration, not an individual vehicle's production or registration date.[1]

Model distinctions

GLX equipment included electrically adjusted and heated body-coloured mirrors, body-coloured exterior handles and lock rings, side mouldings, a four-spoke steering wheel, central locking and a height-adjustable driver seat. The 50 kW 1.3 version was designated GLXi.[1] The 1.3-litre 50 kW versions carried an “i” in the sales grade name; the 40 kW 1.3 did not. In this Czech offer the GLX line paired the 1.3 engine with 50 kW; the 40 kW 1.3 appears under LX and LX-special in the dated table.[1]

Equipment and safety

This baseline order has no airbag fitted. The contemporary Czech offer also listed two separately priced configurations for this otherwise identical GLXi hatchback: driver airbag and front airbags with belt pretensioners. The badge and engine code alone do not identify which restraint configuration was ordered.[1]
