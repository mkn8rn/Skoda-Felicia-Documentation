Škoda Felicia Lato

Felicia Lato was a Polish-market limited series named by Škoda Polska among the special Felicia editions prepared for that market. A later edition history reports a radio and rubber floor mats as Lato equipment.[1][2]

Market designation

The Polish importer distinguishes Lato from Genua, Kliff and Perfekt as one of the local Felicia series. Lato is an edition name applied to the Felicia range, not a body or engine code.[1]

Equipment

The reported Lato package included an in-car radio and rubber floor mats. A secondary model history describes Samba as derived from Lato with an alarm added, preserving Lato as a separate named base series.[2]

Vehicle identity

The Lato name does not replace Felicia body and engine identifiers.
