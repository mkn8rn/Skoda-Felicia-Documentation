Škoda Felicia — Greek period configurations

Greek period reports distinguish tested hatchback grades/equipment sets, two June 1996 GLXi body forms, a priced Combi LXi and an ordinary Pickup. Report dates identify the evidence period, not a production or parts cutoff.[1][3][4][6]
