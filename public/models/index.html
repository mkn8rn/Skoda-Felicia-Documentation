Models and identification

Models and identification is the index for model differences and vehicle identification needed to interpret parts and workshop references.

The engine-family overview is available. Detailed body, equipment, transmission, production-limit, and vehicle-identification articles remain unwritten.

Contents

  1. Engine families

  3. See also

Engine families

Engine construction, principal dimensions, and documented codes are recorded in the shared engine article:

  • 1.3-litre petrol — 135B, 135M, 136B, and 136M.
  • 1.6-litre petrol — AEE.
  • 1.9-litre diesel — AEF.

Body, market, production, and equipment application limits still require documentation. Engine displacement alone does not identify a compatible component.
