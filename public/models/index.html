Models and identification

The Škoda Felicia range comprised a five-door hatchback, a passenger estate and commercial pickup, with Vanplus and Fun derivatives. A factory body type, an engine code, a national sales grade and a homologation variant identify different aspects of a vehicle. None is interchangeable with the others: two Felicias with the same engine or broad type can have different cabin layouts, safety equipment and fitted parts.[1][2][3]

Body styles

The hatchback and Combi are passenger bodies. The ordinary Pickup has a two-seat cab and an open load platform. The factory passenger-body numbers Š 791 and Š 795 differ from national and EC approval identifiers.[2][3]

Factory derivatives and export designation

Vanplus changes the enclosed Combi body into a commercial vehicle. Fun changes the Pickup cabin into a recreational 2+2 arrangement. Combi and Vanplus share the base number 795; Pickup and Fun share 797. The derivative designation and the corresponding approval family distinguish them.[3][4][5]

Felicia FunLeisure pickup with a movable cabin partition and deployable rear seats; 2+2 seating[4]797; e11*96/79*0074[3]

Volkswagen Caddy was an export sales designation for Felicia-derived pickups in selected markets. It is not a fourth Felicia body style; the Felicia-derived vehicle must be distinguished from other Volkswagen vehicles carrying the Caddy name.[5]

Identification conditions

Body type, engine designation, market grade, restraint configuration and approval variant identify different selection conditions. Approval validity and brochure dates do not identify a manufacturing or parts-change boundary.[10]
