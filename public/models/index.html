Body types and derivatives

The hatchback and Combi are passenger bodies. The ordinary Pickup has a two-seat cab and an open load platform. The factory passenger-body numbers Š 791 and Š 795 differ from national and EC approval identifiers.[1][2]

Factory derivatives and export designation

Vanplus changes the enclosed Combi body into a commercial vehicle. Fun changes the Pickup cabin into a recreational 2+2 arrangement. Combi and Vanplus share the base number 795; Pickup and Fun share 797. The derivative designation and the corresponding approval family distinguish them.[2][3][4]

Factory commercial and recreational derivatives
DerivativeBody and cabin distinctionApproval identification
Felicia VanplusEnclosed commercial estate derived from the Combi; two or five seats[4]795 VANPLUS; H780[2]
Felicia FunLeisure pickup with a movable cabin partition and deployable rear seats; 2+2 seating[3]797; e11*96/79*0074[2]

Volkswagen Caddy was an export sales designation for Felicia-derived pickups in selected markets. It is not a fourth Felicia body style; the Felicia-derived vehicle must be distinguished from other Volkswagen vehicles carrying the Caddy name.[4]
