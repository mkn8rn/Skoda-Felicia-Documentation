Models and identification

The Škoda Felicia range included passenger hatchback and Combi bodies, commercial derivatives and the recreational Fun. Factory body types identify the principal constructions; national price lists and special-series specifications also distinguish equipment, engine, fuel system and market versions. The Felicia road range used multiple engine outputs and revisions, whose availability depended on the dated offer and body configuration.[1][2]

Production bodies and derivatives

The Felicia uses different identifiers for body construction and powertrain. Types 791 and 795 identify the hatchback and Combi; utility derivatives share broad approval families, so an approval type alone may not distinguish their body and use.[2][3]

1998 Czech Felicia GLXi airbag configurations

The same Felicia GLXi 1.3 MPI 50 kW hatchback could be ordered with no optional airbag, a driver airbag, or front airbags with belt pretensioners. All three configurations retained the same GLXi body, grade and powertrain; the distinction was the number and position of airbags and the associated belt pretensioners.[4]

Motorsport models

The Felicia Kit Car was a Škoda Motorsport competition car, not a passenger-production trim. Motorsport engine types and outputs belong to the competition specification and are separate from road-car engine combinations.

Engine families

Road Felicias used the 1.3-litre petrol engine at 40 or 50 kW with single-point or multipoint injection: codes 135B/136B denote single-point injection and 135M/136M denote multipoint injection. The 1.6-litre AEE was a 55 kW multipoint-injection petrol engine; the 1.9-litre AEF was a 47 kW indirect-injection diesel.[2][6]
