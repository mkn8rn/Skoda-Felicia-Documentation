Felicia Pickup Freestyle

Freestyle identifies a competition derivative of the Felicia Pickup, with aerodynamic load-platform bodywork. It is distinct from the ordinary commercial Pickup, recreational Fun and the earlier Favorit-based racing Pickup.[1]
