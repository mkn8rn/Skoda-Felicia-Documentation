Applicability

The complete approval, variant, execution and execution revision identify the homologation record. The records do not assign a national LX/LXi/GLX/GLXi grade, named edition, steering side, fitted restraint or other option set, manufacturer gearbox product, original parts list or production/VIN limits. Identical engines or similar execution strings do not establish physical part equivalence. Different approval envelopes are not converted into factory body-change dates.[1][2][3][5]
