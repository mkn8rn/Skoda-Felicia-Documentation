Škoda Felicia Morzine

Morzine is a French Felicia edition name. A January 1999 period notice identifies passenger and estate forms with portable temperature-control and booster-seat accessories. Later catalogue entries distinguish additional Morzine grade, engine-descriptor and body combinations; the name alone does not select one dated equipment package.[1][2][3]
