Škoda Felicia Fair Play — German designation

ADAC identifies Fair Play as a Combi 1.3 offer rated at 40 kW, within its April–August 1996 catalogue period. The name, body and output identify this source-defined offer; the period is not a production or parts-change boundary.[1]
