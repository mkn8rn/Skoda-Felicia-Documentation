J. Laureta body forms

J. Laureta denotes independently built vehicles based on the Felicia Pickup, with extended and purpose-specific coachwork. Converter identity, chassis identification and body use distinguish these from an ordinary Škoda Pickup trim. A Laureta name alone does not identify an engine or manufacturing approval.[1]

Recognition

The maker account identifies hearse, ambulance and service-vehicle forms. The separate ministry reference HP-0026 identifies replacement of a Felicia Pickup body by extended Laureta coachwork; it is not a universal type identifier for all J. Laureta manufacture.[1][2]
