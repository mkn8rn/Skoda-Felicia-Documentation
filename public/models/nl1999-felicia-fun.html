Škoda Felicia Fun — Netherlands, February 1999

The Netherlands Fun offer is identified by Pon Mobiel’s price list effective 1 February 1999, publication code 999 A 63. It specifies the recreational 2+2 cabin and separately priced safety, upholstery and rear-roof choices.[1]

Identification

The importer record gives no engine/output or steering-side identifier. The airbag options include belt pretensioners; the paired-airbag option also removes the glovebox. The leather and canvas-roof choices are separate equipment distinctions, without an established combined-option matrix.[1]

Offer and equipment configurations

  • Standard-equipment offer
  • Driver airbag and belt pretensioners
  • Front airbags and belt pretensioners
  • Yellow leather trim
  • Abs option
  • Load-bed canvas roof
