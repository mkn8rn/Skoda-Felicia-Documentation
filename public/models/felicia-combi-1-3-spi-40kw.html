Škoda Felicia Combi 135B approval configurations

The Felicia Combi variant SWE11 is recorded with engine designation 781.135B. Its populated later approval specifications give 1,289 cm³ and 40 kW. The execution suffix distinguishes individual approved configurations; it does not identify a universal national sales grade.[1][2]

Identification

The engine designation, full approval and execution revision must be selected together. An early record without a populated power or displacement field does not acquire those values solely from a later revision.[3]

Approved variant/execution configurations

These approval identities share the recorded engine designation. Their complete approval numbers and execution revisions retain separate technical conditions; no national grade or factory part equivalence is implied.[4]
