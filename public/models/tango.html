Škoda Felicia Tango

Felicia Tango was a named Škoda limited series introduced to the Polish market in March 1997. The Polish importer distinguishes this initial Tango series from the later Tango 2 edition in its list of Felicia limited models.[1][2]

Market introduction

The first Felicia limited edition offered in Poland was Tango. It was introduced in March 1997 as a named series within the Felicia range. The importer’s later roster treats the unnumbered Tango and Tango 2 as separate edition names.[1][2]

Model identity

Tango is an edition designation layered onto a production Felicia body and engine. Body type and engine code identify the underlying car; the edition name identifies its market series.
