Felicia Israeli national model catalogue

Israeli model selections use Škoda manufacturer code 676 together with an administrative class, national model code and recorded model-production year. Their matching importer-reported new-car entries establish the associated offer names. National model codes are separate from Škoda factory body codes and engine identifiers.[1][2]

To identify a selection, match the national manufacturer/model code and recorded year, then check the literal model and commercial designations. A blank body field does not identify hatchback, Combi or a seating layout. Numbers such as 613, 614 and 654 in a model name are not treated as body codes.[1]
