Identification

The Laurin & Klement edition was offered in hatchback and Combi bodies. Its documented appearance includes dark-blue pearl paint, body-colour bumpers and mirrors, retro-design alloy wheels and leather upholstery. Equipment and engine selections require the body and market specification; the edition name is not an engine or chassis code.[1][2]
