Škoda Felicia Laurin & Klement

The Felicia Laurin & Klement (L&K) was the range’s luxury equipment edition, introduced in 1995 for both the five-door hatchback and Combi. It marked the first modern Škoda production model to carry the historic L&K name. The edition celebrated the 100th anniversary of the company’s foundation and was positioned above the regular Felicia equipment versions.[1][2]

Body applications

L&K was available on the Felicia hatchback and estate body. The hatchback retains factory type Š 791; the Combi is Š 795. L&K is an equipment edition of those bodies, not a separate chassis or engine family. Manufacturer reporting identifies both body applications but does not treat the Pickup, Vanplus or Fun as L&K models.[2][3]

Distinguishing equipment

The edition’s identified exterior specification included dark-blue pearl paint, body-coloured bumpers and mirrors, and alloy wheels in a retro design. The interior used leather upholstery; heated front seats and front airbags were included, with ABS and electrically adjusted exterior mirrors among the documented equipment. These items distinguish the L&K presentation, while optional equipment and specific combinations varied by body and market.[2]

Production

Škoda reported 3,806 Felicia L&K cars: 1,477 hatchbacks and 2,329 Combi estates. The L&K treatment therefore spans two independently identifiable Felicia body types and does not replace their body-specific identification.[2]
