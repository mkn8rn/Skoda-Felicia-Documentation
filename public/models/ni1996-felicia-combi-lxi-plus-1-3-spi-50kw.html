Škoda Felicia Combi LXi Plus 1.3 SPI 50 kW (Northern Ireland, 1996)

The Felicia Combi LXi Plus was an individually named estate grade in the Northern Ireland price list dated 1 March 1996. It shared the 68 bhp, 50 kW 1.3-litre single-point-injection petrol configuration with the LXi and GLXi but had its own sales designation and wheel-trim specification.[1]

Configuration

Northern Ireland Felicia Combi LXi Plus, 1 March 1996 price list
BodyFive-door estate, factory type Š 795[3]
Engine1,289 cm³ petrol, code 136B
Rated output50 kW (68 bhp)
Fuel systemBosch Mono-Motronic single-point injection
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive
Offer dateNorthern Ireland, 1 March 1996

The matching technical specification gives electronic ignition, catalyst-controlled exhaust and a synchronised five-speed manual gearbox. Code 136B distinguishes this 50 kW single-point-injection engine from the lower-rated 135B.[1][2]

Grade distinction

The factory list gives LXi Plus a dedicated full-width wheel-cover design. The Combi’s estate body remains type Š 795; the Plus suffix identifies the named grade, not a different chassis family or engine.

Identification

The listed combination is specific to the Northern Ireland offer on 1 March 1996. Record market, grade, body type and engine code together; registration year alone does not establish the original factory specification.
