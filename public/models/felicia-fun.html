Škoda Felicia Fun

The Felicia Fun is a factory leisure pickup with a convertible 2+2 cabin, derived from the Pickup. Its model designation distinguishes it from the two-seat work pickup: a transverse movable rear bulkhead and fold-out auxiliary seating extend the cabin behind the front seats. Series production ran from October 1995 to August 2000 at Vrchlabí; the homologation type is 797.[1][2]

Body and cabin

The Fun retained an open pickup load bed and a yellow-accented exterior, but its cabin was designed for occasional rear passengers. In its normal position, the movable rear wall separates the two-seat cab from the load area. Sliding it rearward exposes a compact rear bench, producing a four-seat 2+2 arrangement; with the bench in use, the usable bed length falls from 1,370 mm to 850 mm. The stated exterior dimensions are 4,245 × 1,680 × 1,465 mm, with a 2,450 mm wheelbase.[1][3]

German-market offer

Škoda Auto Deutschland’s 2020 history discusses the Fun as offered in Germany and describes a 1.6 MPI pairing in its 1999 account. It reports a separate optional partial-leather interior. The German account does not state a German-specific engine code or build limit, or give an option code for the partial-leather upholstery.[6]

Production and type

The type-approval schedule separates Felicia Fun type 797 from the ordinary Felicia type 791, Combi 795 and Vanplus 795 VANPLUS. Pickup also appears under type 797 in the same schedule, so the number by itself cannot distinguish those two bodies; the 2+2 sliding-cab arrangement identifies Fun. Škoda Polska’s 2014 retrospective reports 3,992 Fun vehicles; a 2020 Škoda Czech press brief gives 4,016, while the 2019 Škoda history rounds the series to approximately 4,000. These publications therefore preserve different reported production totals.[2][4][5]

UK offer, March 2000

Škoda UK offered the Felicia Fun 1.6 MPI in March 2000. Its brochure identifies the 1,598 cm³ engine and publishes a separate UK mass/payload specification.[7]
