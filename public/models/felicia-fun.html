Škoda Felicia Fun

The Fun is the recreational pickup with a movable rear cabin wall and deployable auxiliary seats, producing a 2+2 cabin. This cabin distinguishes it from the ordinary two-seat commercial Pickup.[1]

Identification

The Fun has an open load bed and yellow-accented exterior. Its transverse rear cabin wall separates the normal two-seat cab from the bed; moving the wall rearward exposes two auxiliary seats. The German approval schedule lists Fun under type 797 and e11*96/79*0074. Ordinary Pickup also uses type 797, so the number alone does not identify Fun.[1][2]
