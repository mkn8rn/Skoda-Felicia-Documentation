Škoda Felicia Pacific

Pacific is a limited Felicia edition identified by Pacific green or Laguna blue metallic paint. The Czech-language leaflet bearing a September 1997 imprint presents both the hatchback and Combi.[1]

Identification

The leaflet names three engine choices without identifying them. The Pacific name and colour theme therefore do not select an engine, gearbox or a complete fitted-parts configuration. Its roof-carrier equipment is restricted to Combi; the roof-opening mechanism and mapping of two- or four-speaker equipment are not specified.[1]
