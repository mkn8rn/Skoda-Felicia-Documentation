Škoda Felicia Friend

Friend is a German Felicia equipment edition presented in a March 1999 brochure. The passenger hatchback is labelled Limousine; the estate is separately labelled Combi. The price entries distinguish LXi/LX grades, engine outputs and two 1.6 air-conditioning versions.[1]

Identification

Recognition equipment includes alloy wheels, front fog lamps, body-colour mirrors and bumpers, electric front windows, a centre console and remote security equipment. Roof rails belong to Combi. The air-conditioning offers have a restricted three-finish selection; the edition name alone does not select an engine, finish, gearbox or exact original parts.[1]
