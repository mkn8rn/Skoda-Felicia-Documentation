Škoda Felicia LXi 1.3 SPI 50 kW (Northern Ireland, 1996)

In Škoda UK’s Northern Ireland price list of 1 March 1996, LXi denotes a 68 bhp Felicia hatchback with the higher-output 1.3-litre petrol engine. The engine used Bosch Mono-Motronic single-point injection and produced 50 kW; the same mechanical specification was also sold under the separately named LXi Plus and GLXi grades.[1]

Fuel systemBosch Mono-Motronic single-point injection
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive

The table specifies electronic, microprocessor-controlled ignition and a three-way catalytic converter with this petrol range. It gives the LXi a higher compression ratio than LX. Code 136B identifies its 50 kW single-point-injection engine.[1][2]

Grade distinction

The LXi is the standard 68 bhp hatch grade in this list. LXi Plus shares its stated engine and body but is given its own sales designation and a dedicated wheel-trim pattern. GLXi is another distinct grade with a broader equipment package, including front fog lamps, tinted glazing, central locking and a sunroof in the illustrated list.

Identification

Here, “LXi” records a particular 1996 Northern Ireland trim-and-engine offer. LXi labels from another market or list date require their own market and configuration evidence.
