Škoda Felicia Magic Combi 1.3 MPI 40 kW (Czech Republic, 1998)

The Czech-market Felicia Magic Combi is documented with the 1.3-litre engine at 40 kW. Škoda’s press department described the two body names and two 1.3-litre outputs in its September 1998 launch announcement.[1]

Model distinctions

This model is the five-door Combi estate version with engine code 135M. The Magic series was distinguished by granite-metallic paint and added alloy wheels, front fog lamps, electric window operation and a split rear seat. The announced planned run was 3,000 cars for the domestic Czech market. The Combi announcement gives separate prices for both engine outputs; the series release also identifies the Felicia hatchback and the two engine choices.[1]

Market and date

The launch article dated 7 September 1998 reports sales beginning in the second half of September. The planned 3,000-unit series size is an announcement, not a verified completed production total.[1]
