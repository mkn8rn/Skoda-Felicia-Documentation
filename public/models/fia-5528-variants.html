Felicia FIA 5528 homologation classifications

A-5528 and N-5528 are distinct group identifiers. VF denotes a supply variant, ET a normal evolution, VO an option and VK a kit variant in the individual manufacturer forms. Extension numbers must be read with the group identifier.[1]
