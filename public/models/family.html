Identification

Family is the Czech February 2000 equipment series for the Š 791 hatchback and Š 795 Combi. Its identifying package includes body-coloured mirrors and bumpers with front fog lamps, distinctive grey-blue or grey-green seat fabric, electric front windows and a driver airbag. Hatchback luggage-side pockets and Combi roof rails distinguish the two body applications.[1][2]
