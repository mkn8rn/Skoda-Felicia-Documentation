Škoda Felicia Family

Felicia Family was a Czech-market limited equipment series announced in February 2000 for the five-door hatchback and Combi. Its name described a comfort and safety package applied to the two existing bodies, rather than a different chassis or engine family.[1][2]

Body and paint

The edition used the standard Felicia hatchback body, factory code Š 791, or the five-door Š 795 Combi estate. Its 2000 launch announcement listed Candy white, Rallye red, Petrol green, deep-blue metallic and diamond-silver metallic paint. The Combi retained its estate roof and passenger compartment; the named Family equipment did not turn it into the commercial Vanplus.[3]

Standard equipment

The published Family package combined body-coloured mirrors and bumpers with integrated front fog lamps, large wheel covers, side mouldings and tinted glass. Cabin equipment included edition-specific grey-blue or grey-green seat fabric, a centre console with removable storage, remote central locking with alarm, a driver’s airbag, a tachometer with digital clock, electrically operated front windows, a Škoda MS 201 radio and a release for the rear hatch at the driver’s position. The rear seat and backrest were split. The hatchback had side pockets in the luggage compartment; the Combi instead had longitudinal roof-rack rails. Both bodies were listed with load-restraint eyes and an elastic luggage net.[1]

Body and powertrain configurations

The February 2000 Czech offer listed the Family equipment series in separate hatchback and Combi versions. Its engine range included the 1.3 MPI at 40 and 50 kW, the AEE 1.6 MPI at 55 kW, and the AEF 1.9 diesel at 47 kW. The hatchback and Combi retained different rear body structures and luggage-area fittings.
