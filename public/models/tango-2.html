Škoda Felicia Tango 2

Tango 2 was a separately named Felicia limited edition in the Polish importer’s edition roster. The numeral distinguishes it from the earlier Tango series, which the same importer dates to its March 1997 Polish-market introduction.[1][2]

Edition designation

The 2003 importer history lists Tango 2 separately among Felicia limited series. A later model history describes it as an LXi-based Felicia with a Grundig radio, 13-inch alloy wheels and a revised centre console. These details distinguish the numbered series from the earlier Tango name.[1][3]

Equipment description

The reported additions comprise a Grundig radio, alloy wheels and a modified centre console on the LXi equipment basis. These are cabin and trim differences; the engine remains identified by its Felicia engine code and rated output.[3]

Vehicle identity

Tango 2 is an edition name rather than a body code. A specific Tango 2 vehicle retains its Felicia body type and engine identifiers.
