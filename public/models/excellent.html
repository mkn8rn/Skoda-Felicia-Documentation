Škoda Felicia Combi Excellent

Combi Excellent is an LX-based edition of the five-door estate, type Š 795, associated with the facelift-era range.[1][2][3]

Recognition equipment

Later descriptions identify front fog lamps, tinted windows, revised wheel covers, roof rails and metallic paint.[2]
