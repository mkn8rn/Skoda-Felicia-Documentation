Škoda Felicia Combi Excellent

Felicia Combi Excellent was a named equipment series for the Š 795 estate body. It appeared with the facelift-era Felicia and used the LX grade as its equipment basis. Škoda later listed Excellent among the range’s special editions.[1][2]

Body application

Felicia Combi Excellent was an equipment edition based on the five-door estate, not a separate chassis type. The facelift changed the exterior of the Felicia family in February 1998; Combi continued to use factory body code Š 795 and approval type 795.[1][3]

Equipment

Descriptions of the Combi Excellent identify front fog lamps, tinted windows, revised wheel covers, roof rails and metallic paint among its LX-based additions. The estate-specific roof rails distinguish its body equipment from hatchback editions with the same grade basis.[2]

Powertrain identity

Excellent is an equipment-series name; a Felicia Combi of this edition retained the Combi body type and its own engine and transmission identifiers.
