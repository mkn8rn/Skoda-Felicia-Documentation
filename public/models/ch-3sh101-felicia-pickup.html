Felicia Pickup — CH 3SH101 superstructure classes

CH 3SH101 identifies two-door ordinary commercial pickup with engine 781.136B. Its remarks distinguish load-platform superstructures by material or written name. This is the two-seat commercial body, not the four-seat Fun.[1]
