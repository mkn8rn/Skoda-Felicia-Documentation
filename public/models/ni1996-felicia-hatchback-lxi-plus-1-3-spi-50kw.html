Škoda Felicia LXi Plus 1.3 SPI 50 kW (Northern Ireland, 1996)

LXi Plus was a separately named Felicia hatchback grade in Škoda UK’s Northern Ireland price list dated 1 March 1996. The list assigns it the same 68 bhp, 50 kW 1.3-litre single-point-injection engine as LXi and GLXi while distinguishing it by grade-specific equipment.[1]

Offer dateNorthern Ireland, 1 March 1996

The engine panel specifies electronic ignition and catalytic exhaust treatment. Code 136B identifies the high-compression 50 kW 1.3 single-point-injection engine; 135B identifies the related 40 kW version.[1][2]

Grade distinction

The price-list equipment table marks an LXi Plus-specific full-width wheel-trim design. LXi Plus and LXi share the engine rating, but the additional Plus designation is an official sales-grade distinction in this list. GLXi remains a separate higher equipment grade.

Identification

The Plus suffix is meaningful for this particular 1996 Northern Ireland offer. A vehicle’s body and engine codes establish the underlying car and power unit; its factory equipment and original market record distinguish the sales grade.
