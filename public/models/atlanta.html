Identification

Atlanta is the Czech 1996 Olympic edition. Its LX-based package included 14-inch alloy wheels, a Blaupunkt radio, a driver airbag and a tachometer with digital clock; the listed finishes were green, white or red. The hatchback is type Š 791 and the Combi Š 795.[1][2]
