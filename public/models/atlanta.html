Škoda Felicia Atlanta

Felicia Atlanta was Škoda’s 1996 special edition associated with the Summer Olympic Games in Atlanta. The Czech-market specification combined an LX-based equipment package, the 50 kW 1.3-litre petrol engine and a limited palette of Olympic colours. It was sold as both the five-door hatchback and Felicia Combi.[1][2]

Olympic special edition

Felicia Atlanta was a Czech-market edition associated with the 1996 Summer Olympic Games. It used an LX-based equipment specification and was offered in green, white or red paint. Its added equipment included 14-inch alloy wheels, a Blaupunkt radio, a driver airbag, a tachometer with digital clock and central locking.[3]

Body-specific identification

The Atlanta edition was sold in the hatchback and Combi bodies. Their factory types remain Š 791 and Š 795 respectively. The 1996 Czech offer paired both body forms with the 1.3-litre BMM engine rated at 50 kW.[4]

Series designation

Atlanta is the edition name, not a body code or engine code. Its Olympic-themed equipment and colour offering distinguish the series from the standard Felicia LX, while body type and engine code identify each car’s particular configuration.
