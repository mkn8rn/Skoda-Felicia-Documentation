Škoda Felicia Sport Line

Sport Line was a Felicia equipment series with a sport-themed colour and trim package. It used the LX grade as its basis and was one of the named special editions later recorded by Škoda.[1][2]

Exterior and interior styling

A Felicia special-edition overview describes Sport Line in Rally red or Magic black paint, with contrasting coloured seat inserts. Red bodywork used red seat centres and belts; black bodywork used yellow interior accents. The car retained the normal Felicia body form and passenger layout.[2]

Equipment

The reported LX-based package added central locking, a driver’s airbag and electrically operated windows, together with a four-spoke sport steering wheel, alloy wheels and a chromed exhaust finish. The contrasting seat and belt colours were part of the edition’s visual identity. The listed equipment describes the series package; the Felicia’s body and engine codes remain the identifiers for its underlying vehicle configuration.[2]

Model relationship

Sport Line is a named Felicia road-car equipment version, distinct from the competition Felicia Kit Car. The factory press history classifies Sport Line among road-car special editions, while the rally Kit Car was a separate motorsport programme.[1][3]
