Škoda Felicia Sport Line

Sport Line is an LX-based Felicia road-car edition, distinct from the competition Kit Car.[1][2][3]

Contents

  1. Colour identification
  2. Recognition equipment
  3. See also
  4. References

Colour identification

The later equipment account describes Rally red bodywork with red seat centres and belts, or Magic black bodywork with yellow interior accents.[2]

Recognition equipment

Reported features include central locking, a driver airbag, electrically operated windows, a four-spoke sport steering wheel, alloy wheels and a chromed exhaust finish.[2]
