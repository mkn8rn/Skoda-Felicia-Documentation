Škoda Felicia Combi

The Felicia Combi is the five-door estate derivative of the Š 791 hatchback. Its factory model code is Š 795. Škoda presented the Combi in June 1995; production continued through the Felicia’s final production year in 2001.[1][2]

Identification

The Š 795 designation distinguishes the estate body from the hatchback type Š 791. Later homologation records list type 795 as Felicia Combi and 795 VANPLUS as the commercial Vanplus derivative. The shared base number therefore does not make the passenger estate and commercial van the same model.[2][6]

The Combi uses an extended roof and rear body to provide an enclosed estate compartment. Škoda’s 2024 retrospective gives its pre-facelift length as 4,205 mm and reports a 32 mm length increase with the February 1998 update.[1][3]

Factory configuration

The Combi entered the range with the 1.3-litre petrol engine. The AEE 1.6 MPI, rated at 55 kW, debuted in the Felicia range with the Combi at the 1995 Brno motor show. The naturally aspirated AEF 1.9 D is rated at 47 kW. Škoda Polska dates its Felicia introduction to 1996, while a 2019 Škoda retrospective groups the diesel with engines added in 1995; both sources place the 1.6 introduction in 1995.[4][1][7]

Production revision

Škoda’s 2014 Felicia retrospective places body-structure reinforcement within an initial 1996 change package for the model family. It does not list affected Combi panels or provide a Combi-specific chassis-number boundary; type Š 795 alone cannot identify a particular structural-parts revision.[7]

A 2001 technical account describes the Combi as using its own extended shell and states that Combi shells were produced with structural adaptations from their introduction in May 1995. The same account associates front and floor-area reinforcement with airbag provision and Volkswagen 1.6 MPI or 1.9 D engines. It does not identify Combi-specific panels or show whether the manufacturer’s 1996 change package was additional to these earlier shell adaptations. The production chronology does not supply a VIN or parts-number boundary.[9][7]

Škoda dates a major facelift to February 1998. Its 2024 history reports a 32 mm length increase for the estate body with that update. The Combi kept the 795 designation, but the unchanged code does not establish panel interchangeability or a production/VIN cutoff. Manufacturer totals record 351,905 Combi vehicles for 1995–2001.[1][3]
