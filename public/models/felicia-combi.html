Škoda Felicia Combi

The Felicia Combi is the five-door estate derivative of the Š 791 hatchback. Its factory model code is Š 795. Škoda presented the Combi in June 1995; production continued through the Felicia’s final production year in 2001.[1][2]

Identification

The Š 795 designation distinguishes the estate body from the hatchback type Š 791. Later homologation records list type 795 as Felicia Combi and 795 VANPLUS as the commercial Vanplus derivative. The shared base number therefore does not make the passenger estate and commercial van the same model.[2][6]

The Combi uses an extended roof and rear body to provide an enclosed estate compartment. Škoda’s 2024 retrospective gives its pre-facelift length as 4,205 mm and reports a 32 mm length increase with the February 1998 update.[1][3]

Factory configuration

The Combi entered the range with the 1.3-litre petrol engine. The AEE 1.6 MPI, rated at 55 kW, debuted in the Felicia range with the Combi at the 1995 Brno motor show. The naturally aspirated AEF 1.9 D is rated at 47 kW. Škoda Polska dates its Felicia introduction to 1996, while a 2019 Škoda retrospective groups the diesel with engines added in 1995; both sources place the 1.6 introduction in 1995.[4][1][7]

Škoda’s safety-rescue sheet identifies the model series as 6U; this service designation is distinct from the earlier factory body code Š 795.[5]

Production revision

The 1998 facelift changed the front-end styling and added body colours and equipment options. It did not rename the Combi or replace type 795. Manufacturer production totals record 351,905 Combi vehicles built between 1995 and 2001.[1]
