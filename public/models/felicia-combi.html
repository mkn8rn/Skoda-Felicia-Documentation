Identification

The Š 795 designation distinguishes the estate body from the hatchback type Š 791. Later homologation records list type 795 as Felicia Combi and 795 VANPLUS as the commercial Vanplus derivative. The shared base number therefore does not make the passenger estate and commercial van the same model.[1][2]

The Combi has an extended roof and enclosed estate rear compartment.[3]

Škoda’s Combi rescue sheet identifies the series as TMB-6U; this service-document identifier is distinct from factory body code Š 795.[4]
