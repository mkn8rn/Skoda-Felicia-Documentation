Approval recognition

The A/E number identifies the Danish vehicle record. It is distinct from an EC approval-family number printed within that record. An issue or reprint date does not by itself delimit production. Explicit model-year and July 1999 selections retain the wording and scope of their particular variant; they are not VIN boundaries.[8][4][6]

The Pickup 136 marking has no B/M suffix. Its separate ABS selections must not be assigned a single-point or multipoint injection system merely from the output or record date.[7]
