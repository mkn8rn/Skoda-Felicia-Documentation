Identification

Safe-line is the Czech 1999 safety edition, offered in red or white as a hatchback or Combi. It is distinguished by ABS and four airbags: a front and side airbag for each front occupant. The edition badge alone does not identify the engine/output, colour or fitted extra equipment.[1]
