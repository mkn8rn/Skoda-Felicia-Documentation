Škoda Felicia Safe-line

Felicia Safe-line was a Czech-market safety edition offered in hatchback and Combi estate forms. A report published on 4 March 1999 describes anti-lock brakes, four airbags and a choice of red or white paint for the series.[1]

Safety equipment

The Safe-line specification included an anti-lock braking system (ABS) and four airbags. The driver and front passenger each had a front airbag and a side airbag.[1]
