Škoda Felicia Kit Car

The Felicia Kit Car was Škoda Motorsport’s factory-developed competition derivative of the Felicia. It followed the production hatchback’s front-wheel-drive architecture but used purpose-built competition engines and body, suspension, braking and safety equipment. It was a rally car, not a road-production trim or a production Felicia powertrain option.[1][2]

Competition programme

Škoda developed the Kit Car in 1995 for the international Formula 2 and kit-car classes. Unlike a road Felicia, the competition car was homologated and prepared for rally regulations; its specifications cannot be transferred to ordinary type 791 hatchbacks or other road models.[1]

Competition engines

Factory histories describe 1.3-litre and 1.5-litre competition engines in the first Kit Car specification. Later cars used a 1.6-litre engine developed by Škoda Motorsport, with output reported up to 174 hp. These competition configurations were separate from road Felicias, which used 1.3 MPI, Volkswagen-group AEE 1.6 MPI and AEF 1.9 D power units.[1][2]

World Rally Championship results

The Kit Car competed in the World Rally Championship from 1995 through 1997. Škoda records a class third place in its first season, a 1996 RAC Rally third place overall and first in the Kit Car class up to 2.0 litres for Stig Blomqvist and Benny Melander, and a 1997 second place in its class in the championship. The Kit Car programme followed the Felicia road range but is counted separately from its production totals.[3]
