Škoda Felicia Combi GLX 1.9 D 47 kW (Netherlands, 1999)

The 1999 Netherlands specification includes a Combi GLX with the AEF 1.9-litre naturally aspirated diesel engine. The importer rates this combination at 47 kW and equips it with the five-speed manual transmission.[1]

Factory specification

Dutch-market Felicia Combi GLX, 1999 specification
BodyFive-door estate, type Š 795[3]
Engine1,896 cm³ diesel, code AEF
Output47 kW
Fuel systemIndirect-injection diesel
Airbag equipmentTwo airbags listed[1]
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive
Market and dateNetherlands; 1999 importer specification

The AEF code denotes the naturally aspirated indirect-injection diesel. In the Dutch matrix, this 47 kW engine is listed under both LX and GLX grades; the trim difference does not change its rated output.[1][2]

Distinguishing equipment

Compared with the Combi LX 1.9 D, GLX adds tinted glazing, two airbags, central locking, body-coloured handles, electrically adjustable mirrors, side mouldings and front fog lamps. The brochure specifies power steering on both AEF-powered grades. All seven Combi configurations in this Dutch brochure have a split, asymmetrically folding rear bench.[1]
