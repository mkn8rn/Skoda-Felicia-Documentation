Škoda Felicia Blu Sky

Blu Sky was a named Felicia market edition distinguished by a retractable fabric roof supplied by Webasto. Škoda Polska’s retrospective includes it among the Felicia special versions and identifies the roof as its notable feature.[1]

Roof configuration

The identifying feature was a soft roof panel that could be retracted, rather than a separate body shell or a change to the standard Felicia powertrain. The roof supplier’s name was Webasto. Its installation made Blu Sky visually distinct from the fixed-roof Felicia body.[1]

Vehicle identity

Blu Sky remained a Felicia road-car version. The body type and engine code identify the car’s underlying factory configuration; the Webasto roof identifies its special body equipment.
