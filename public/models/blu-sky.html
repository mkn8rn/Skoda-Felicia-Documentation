Škoda Felicia Blu Sky

Blu Sky is a Felicia special-edition designation.[1]

Roof identification

Its recognition feature is a retractable fabric roof supplied by Webasto.[1]

Germany: July 1998 Blue Sky selections

The German importer writes Blue Sky and identifies a 1.6 L GLX offer, separately priced alloy-wheel and ABS versions, and three body-colour choices. The shared recognition feature is a black Webasto electrically operated folding roof.[2]

  • Felicia Blue Sky 1.6 L GLX — Germany, July 1998[2]
  • Felicia Blue Sky 1.6 L with alloy wheels — Germany, July 1998[2]
  • Felicia Blue Sky 1.6 L with ABS — Germany, July 1998[2]
