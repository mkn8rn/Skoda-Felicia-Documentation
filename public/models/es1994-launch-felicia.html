Škoda Felicia — November 1994 launch selections

The Spanish-language launch account identifies a five-door hatchback and two engine/output columns, 135i and 136i. It names LX and GLX grades but does not pair each grade with a particular column. The report date does not establish delivery or production limits.[1]
