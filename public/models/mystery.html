Škoda Felicia Mystery

Felicia Mystery was a named Škoda special edition whose visual motif drew on Prague’s Astronomical Clock. It was an equipment and appearance version of the Felicia road-car range, not a separate body or powertrain family.[1][2]

Design theme

Škoda’s Felicia history identifies the Astronomical Clock motif as the characteristic design reference for Mystery. Contemporary descriptions associate the series with distinctive exterior colours and coordinated equipment, making the themed finish part of its identity.[1][2]

Reported equipment

An edition summary describes Mystery as LX-based and lists height-adjustable front seats, a centre console with removable storage, split rear seat, front fog lamps, driver’s airbag, remote central locking with alarm, electric front windows and electrically adjusted mirrors. It also reports two paint choices: green Máj and violet Esprit. These are appearance and cabin/equipment differences within the Felicia road range.[2]

Body and engine identification

The Mystery designation sits alongside the underlying Felicia body and engine identifiers. Hatchback and Combi body codes remain Š 791 and Š 795; the engine code identifies its fuel system and rated version.[3]
