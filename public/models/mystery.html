Škoda Felicia Mystery

Mystery is a Felicia edition with a Prague Astronomical Clock design theme.[1]

Contents

Colour identification

The later equipment account identifies green Máj and violet Esprit finishes.[2]

Recognition equipment

The same account describes an LX basis, height-adjustable front seats, a centre console with removable storage, a split rear seat, front fog lamps, a driver airbag, remote central locking with an alarm, electric front windows and electrically adjusted mirrors.[2]
