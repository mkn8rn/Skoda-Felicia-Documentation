Škoda Felicia — French catalogue designations

The French specification catalogue separates five-door passenger Felicias, labelled Berline 5 Portes, from five-door estates, labelled Break 5 Portes. Its grade and edition names are market descriptors, separate from a complete engine code or factory body/revision selector.[1][2]
