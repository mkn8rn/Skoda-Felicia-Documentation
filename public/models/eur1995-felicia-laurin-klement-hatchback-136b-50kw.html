Škoda Felicia Laurin & Klement Hatchback 136B 50 kW (Europe, 1995)

The European 1995 Felicia Laurin & Klement Hatchback is recorded with the 136B engine rated at 50 kW. This body-specific specification is one of the distinct L&K configurations.[1]

Model distinctions

This model is distinguished by body type Š 791, engine code 136B and the L&K equipment package. The L&K edition used dark-blue pearl paint, leather upholstery, heated front seats, front airbags, ABS, electrically adjusted mirrors and alloy wheels. Škoda reports 1,477 L&K hatchbacks and 2,329 L&K Combi cars. The hatchback configuration used a five-door body. The secondary automotive database lists this 136B hatchback under the 1995 L&K designation; Škoda’s retrospective confirms the edition and body count but does not publish an engine-by-body table.[2]

Market and date

Škoda dates the luxury L&K Felicia edition to 1995. The European 1995 listing does not identify a single national market, so equipment and engine availability in national L&K offers may have differed.
