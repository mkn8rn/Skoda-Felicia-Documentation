Škoda Felicia Combi LX SUPER — Czech 2/97 list

LX SUPER is a written Combi equipment-package heading in FCK 026 15 2/97. Its engine/output row and separately selected restraint equipment distinguish individual offers; the package name does not select an engine product or original-parts inventory.[1]
