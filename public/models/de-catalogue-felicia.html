Škoda Felicia — German catalogue configurations

ADAC’s later German catalogue distinguishes passenger hatchbacks, Combi estates, ordinary covered/superstructure Pickups, four-seat Fun and two-seat Vanplus. Its first-generation and facelift headings are catalogue groups; displayed model periods are not individual build or parts-change boundaries.[1][2]
