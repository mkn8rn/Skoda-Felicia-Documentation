Škoda Felicia — Czech July 2000 configurations

The Czech factory list valid from 1 July 2000 distinguishes the hatchback and Combi, the unsuffixed Felicia offer, Trumf, LX and GLX, and the following engine/output entries. The date identifies the sales list, not an individual manufacturing revision.[1] Three factory-priced restraint choices are available in every grade column for both bodies.[2]
