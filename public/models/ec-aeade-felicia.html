Škoda Felicia — Ecuadorian catalogue classes

AEADE’s May 2015 used-market guide identifies five Felicia commercial classes. Their written body/equipment abbreviations and model years distinguish the entries; they are not factory part, gearbox, approval or VIN selectors.[1][2]
