Škoda Felicia Color Line

Felicia Color Line was a named special edition in Škoda’s Felicia range. It used LX-based equipment and set itself apart through exterior paint and coordinated interior trim. Škoda’s later history recognises Color Line among the range’s special editions.[1][2]

Paint and interior

The contemporary Czech model overview describes Color Line cars in Fun yellow or Laguna blue. Upholstery followed the exterior theme, with yellow or blue seats; a multicoloured interior arrangement was also described. Body-coloured door handles and exterior mirrors continued the colour treatment across the bodywork.[2]

Equipment

Reported Color Line features included a four-spoke steering wheel, a split rear bench and front fog lamps. The edition retained Felicia LX as its equipment basis; its bright finish and coordinated trim, rather than a different engine family, provided the identifying theme.[2]

Identification

Color Line is an equipment and appearance designation. The chassis/body identity remains that of the underlying Felicia hatchback or Combi, and the engine code continues to identify the installed powertrain. The shared pages describe the hatchback, Combi and engines used in the Felicia range.[3]
