Škoda Felicia Color Line

Color Line is an LX-based Felicia edition distinguished by coordinated exterior and interior colours.[1][2]

Contents

  1. Colour identification
  2. Recognition equipment
  3. Dated manufacturer selections
  4. See also
  5. References

Colour identification

The 2022 retrospective describes Fun yellow or Laguna blue bodywork, corresponding yellow or blue seat trim, and a multicoloured interior arrangement. Door handles and mirrors follow the body colour.[2]

Recognition equipment

Reported features include a four-spoke steering wheel and a split rear bench. The dated manufacturer sheet distinguishes packages I and II; front fog lamps are standard in II and separately offered in I.[3][2]
