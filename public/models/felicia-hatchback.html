Škoda Felicia hatchback

The Felicia hatchback is the five-door passenger body, factory type Š 791.[1]

Identification

Š 791 is the factory model code for the five-door hatchback. In later German type-approval records it appears as Felicia type 791, with approval family G952 and EC approval e11*93/81*0011. The Combi is separately classified as 795. Pickup and Fun share the broad type number 797, but their approval and body designations distinguish the two derivatives.[2]

The body is a self-supporting five-door passenger shell with a rear liftgate. Škoda’s rescue sheet identifies the Felicia series as TMB-6U; it does not identify this hatchback as 6U1. TMB-6U is the rescue-document series identifier, separate from factory body code Š 791.[3]
