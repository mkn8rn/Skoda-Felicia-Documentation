Felicia DVSA body and engine selections

The DVSA reference distinguishes passenger hatchback, Combi, Vanplus, ordinary Pickup and Fun by their written body and engine designations. Its December 2017 publication date is not a vehicle model year or manufacturing boundary.[1]
