Škoda Felicia Combi Safe-line (Czech Republic, 1999)

The Felicia Combi Safe-line was the estate-body member of the Czech safety edition reported in March 1999. The contemporary description gives it ABS and four airbags: a front and a side airbag for each front occupant. It identifies Combi Safe-line separately from hatchback Safe-line, and reports red or white paint for the series.[1]

Model identity

Safe-line was the edition designation and Combi the estate body name. The Combi is factory type Š 795, distinct from the five-door hatchback type Š 791.[2] The hatchback and Combi were separate body forms under the Safe-line designation.[1]

Reported configuration

EditionSafe-line
BodyFive-door estate (Combi), factory type Š 795

AirbagsFour: driver and front passenger each had a front airbag and a side airbag
Reported exterior coloursRed or white
Market and evidence dateCzech Republic; contemporary report dated 4 March 1999

The published safety description assigns the side-airbag equipment to the driver and front passenger positions. It does not describe rear side bags. Four airbags therefore means two front airbags and two front-seat side airbags in this Combi, rather than one front airbag for each of four seating positions.[1]

Powertrain and price

The report gives an engine-dependent Combi price span of 313,900–375,900 CZK but does not identify the engine codes, outputs or fuel systems at each price. Its price range therefore cannot be used to assign a particular engine configuration to this body.[1]
