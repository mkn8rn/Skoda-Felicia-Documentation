Identification

The Czech September 1998 Magic edition uses granite-metallic paint with alloy wheels, front fog lamps, electrically operated windows and a split rear seat. It was offered in hatchback and Combi bodies; the launch report maps the separate 40 and 50 kW price entries to the Combi, but does not assign an output to the hatchback.[1]

Configurations

  • Felicia Magic Combi 1.3 MPI 40 kW
