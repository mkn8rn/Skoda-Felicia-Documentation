Škoda Felicia Magic

Felicia Magic was a Czech-market special edition offered as both the five-door Felicia and Felicia Combi. Škoda announced the series for the second half of September 1998 with a planned run of 3,000 cars. Its defining paint was granite metallic, and the announced 1.3-litre petrol engines had outputs of 40 or 50 kW.[1][2]

Czech series launch

Škoda announced Felicia Magic for the Czech domestic market in the second half of September 1998. The press announcement set a planned series of 3,000 vehicles and identified granite metallic as the series paint. It named both Felicia and Felicia Combi; the contemporary launch notice documents both bodies and assigns Combi prices to the 40 and 50 kW outputs.[1]

Shared edition equipment

The Magic package added alloy wheels, front fog lamps, electrically operated windows and a split rear seat to the standard Felicia specification. Those equipment features and the granite metallic finish distinguish the series from an ordinary Felicia of the same body and engine. The launch article announced 1.3-litre engines in 40 and 50 kW ratings; the launch report identifies hatchback and Combi bodies and lists two 1.3-litre engine outputs, while assigning individual prices only to the Combi versions.[1]

Model identification

Magic was an appearance and equipment series rather than a separate body structure. The Felicia hatchback retained type Š 791 and the Combi retained type Š 795. Engine code and body type identify the mechanical and body versions; the Magic name identifies the Czech edition package.[3]
