Felicia — German rough-road type keys

The rough-road wheel-approval selection is identified by the complete vehicle type key, the matching vehicle type/approval and the recorded height condition. Its superscript footnote number is not an equipment-package code.[1]

Recognition

Read the full type key in the vehicle document, rather than only its first three characters. The approval table associates the listed full keys with the rough-road condition and a document height of at least 1,465 mm. Height, wheel size or engine output alone does not identify this selection. The vehicle type and approval must also match.[1]

Keys beginning 310, 311, 315 and 317 are associated with type 791; 312, 313, 314 and 316 with type 795. These are hatchback and passenger Combi classifications, respectively. They do not identify a trim, injection revision or physical body-shell product.[2][1]
