Felicia Classic — UK

Classic is a UK Felicia grade named in the July 2000 period account. The report distinguishes its hatchback and estate availability from the Popular grade’s hatchback-only offer. The publication date does not select an individual manufacturing year, powertrain or factory equipment package.[1]
