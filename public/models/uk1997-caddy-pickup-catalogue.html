Volkswagen Caddy Pickup — UK January 1997 catalogue

Volkswagen’s issue 01/97, 765/1191.10.25, identifies the 1997-model-year Caddy Pickup. Its illustrated commercial cab and open load platform distinguish it from the high-cube Caddy van.[1]
