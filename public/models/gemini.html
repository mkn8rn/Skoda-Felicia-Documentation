Škoda Felicia Gemini

Gemini was a limited Felicia edition listed by Škoda Auto Polska among the special series sold in Poland. A later historical overview also identifies Gemini as a Polish-market Felicia edition, separate from the other named series in that market.[1][2]

Edition designation

The Polish importer identifies Gemini as a Felicia limited series. The designation is distinct from the listed Genua, Perfect, Clif, Saga, Color Line, Milena, Samba and Tango 2 editions. It is a national market model name, not a separate Felicia body type or engine family.[1]

Vehicle identification

Gemini identifies the named sales series. A car’s chassis/type approval identifies its Felicia body, while the engine code and rated output identify its power unit; the series name does not replace those mechanical identifiers. The common hatchback, Combi and engine articles describe those shared Felicia systems.
