Felicia Croatian catalogue configurations

Jomologia distinguishes Felicia body, engine description and equipment package with a full JOM catalogue identifier. The final package suffix is part of that identifier. Several technically equivalent vehicles can share a JOM; it is not an individual chassis number or an EC type-approval code.[1]

Catalogue identification

CVH describes Jomologia as a catalogue of vehicles officially sold in Croatia. Its JOM combines vehicle group, make, record number and equipment package. Additional equipment can change a vehicle’s valuation without changing its JOM, so a shared identifier does not prove identical installed options.[1][30]

The broad year labels do not establish that every listed engine and body was available throughout the interval. An LXi or GLXi grade label does not, by itself, identify a B/M engine suffix, injection system or factory option code.
