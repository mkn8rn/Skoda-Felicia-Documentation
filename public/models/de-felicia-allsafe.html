Škoda Felicia All Safe — German designation

ADAC writes All Safe as a Combi designation and identifies three engine/output offers. Its entries list front side-airbag equipment as standard. The German designation does not establish equivalence to Czech Safe-line, and the catalogue model period is not a manufacturing or parts boundary.[1][2][3]
