Felicia configurations — August 1998 owner’s manual

The manufacturer’s August 1998 specification distinguishes Felicia, Combi, Vanplus and Pickup by body and engine output. The Czech-language publication does not identify a sales country, trim or manufacturing interval for every combination.[1]
