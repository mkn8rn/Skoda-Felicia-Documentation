Felicia combi — German type key 8004/314

Manufacturer/type pair 8004/314 identifies the 55 kW petrol class written as FELICIA COMBI by KBA. The separate published vehicle-type association is 795, combi. The three-character key does not alone distinguish the rough-road variant, trim, injection revision or original equipment.[1][2]
