Felicia — original FIA A-5528 configuration

The original A-5528 manufacturer form identifies a 1,289.4 cm³ Felicia hatchback for FIA Group A, with validity beginning on 1 December 1994. It records a five-seat steel unitary body and Bosch single-point injection. This is a homologation configuration, separate from a dated national sales grade.[1]

Recorded equipment

The form specifies one plastic fuel tank below the floor under the rear seats, one 12 V battery and a mechanically operated clutch with one driven plate. The original gearbox and final drive have the A-5528 ratio set; a gearbox product code is not identified.[1]

Article 902 records steel doors, bonnet and tailgate, toughened rear and side glazing, and polypropylene front and rear bumpers. These material descriptions do not identify panel, glass or bumper part numbers.[1]
