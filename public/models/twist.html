Škoda Felicia Twist

Twist is a Felicia sales designation used in Polish dealer offers in 2000. The adverts name central locking, an alarm and a Blaupunkt radio, with different descriptions of the additional display equipment.[1][2]

Identification

The edition name alone does not select a body, powertrain or production range. Neither advert establishes whether its added equipment was installed by the factory or dealer. Their display descriptions do not prove either identical devices or a hardware change between offers.[1][2]
