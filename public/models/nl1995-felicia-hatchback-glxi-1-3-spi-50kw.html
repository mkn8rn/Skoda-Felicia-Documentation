Škoda Felicia GLXi 1.3 SPI 50 kW (Netherlands, 1995)

The Dutch-market Felicia GLXi listed by importer Pon Mobiel from 1 January 1995 was the five-door hatchback with the 50 kW, 1,289 cm³ single-point-injection petrol engine. The named grade and engine rating describe the importer’s dated Netherlands offer, not a universal Felicia grade-to-engine rule.[1]

Factory configuration

Felicia GLXi listed for the Netherlands, 1 January 1995
BodyFive-door hatchback, factory type Š 791[3]
Engine1,289 cm³ OHV petrol, code 136B
Rated output50 kW
Fuel systemBosch Mono-Motronic single-point injection
TransmissionFive-speed manual
DriveFront wheels
Market and dateNetherlands; price list effective 1 January 1995

In the listed Dutch range, GLXi is the 50 kW version, while LXi is listed at 40 kW. The source’s technical specification identifies the same 1.3-litre single-point injection family, five-speed manual gearbox and front-wheel drive. Code 136B identifies the higher-compression version of this early injection system.[1][2]

Identification

This row records the specification sold from the stated list date. It does not date the manufacture or first registration of an individual car. Other market and later Felicia configurations use the GLXi name with different engine and equipment combinations.
