Škoda Felicia Samba

Felicia Samba was a Polish-market limited series and one of the most widely sold named Felicia editions in the importer’s account. Škoda Auto Polska reported that nearly 3,500 Samba cars had been sold by September 2003.[1][2]

Market identity

The Polish importer lists Samba among the Felicia limited series and records its sales as nearly 3,500 units. A later model history describes it as derived from the Lato edition, adding an alarm. This relationship identifies Samba as a distinct named package rather than an alias for Lato.[1][2]

Equipment relationship

The reported Samba package retained the Lato base equipment, including the radio and rubber floor mats, with an alarm added. Body and engine identity remain those of the Felicia version to which the edition was applied.[2]

Vehicle identification

Samba is an edition designation; hatchback, Combi and installed engine versions retain their own identifiers.
