Škoda Felicia Trumf Combi 1.3 MPI 50 kW (Czech Republic, 2000)

The Czech-market Felicia Trumf Combi was announced with a 1.3 MPI engine rated at 50 kW. The contemporaneous model release priced this body separately from the matching Trumf model with the other body type.[1]

Model distinctions

This is the five-door Combi estate Trumf with engine code 136M. Standard Trumf equipment included body-coloured mirrors and door handles, large wheel covers, tinted glazing, a four-spoke steering wheel, a compact centre console and central locking. Its body and package distinguish it from the separately specified Trumf configuration for the other body.

Market and date

Škoda’s limited-series announcement was published on 28 February 2000 for the Czech market. The date identifies the model offer, not an individual car’s production or registration.
