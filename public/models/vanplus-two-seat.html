Škoda Felicia Vanplus — two-seat category

The two-seat Vanplus is the commercial enclosed estate with two occupant positions. The approval designation is 795 VANPLUS, distinct from the passenger Combi; the number alone does not select its engine or parts.[1][2]

Identification

The two seating positions distinguish this cabin from the separately described five-seat Vanplus form. Market, engine, equipment and applicable production/option limits are additional selectors.[1]
