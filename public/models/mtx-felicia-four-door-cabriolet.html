MTX Felicia Cabrio

MTX Cabrio is the Felicia-based four-door convertible with a 2+2 seating layout. Its open body and transverse roof hoop distinguish it from the ordinary hatchback.[1]

Cabrio recognition

The manufacturer leaflet names MTX RS as the converter and Škoda Felicia as the donor vehicle. Its four doors, two front and two rear seating places and convertible roof identify the described body class. An MTX badge alone does not identify the donor engine, trim or converter build.[1][2]
