Škoda Felicia Milena

Felicia Milena was a named limited edition recorded in Škoda Auto Polska’s list of Felicia series. A later edition history describes Milena as LX-based, with a Blaupunkt radio, alarm and black-finished B-pillars among its reported distinguishing items.[1][2]

Edition identity

The Polish importer names Milena as one of the Felicia’s limited series. It is a distinct edition name within the model range, not another Felicia body type or engine family.[1]

Equipment

The reported LX-based package added a Blaupunkt radio and alarm, with black-finished B-pillars as an exterior detail. These features distinguish the Milena presentation from the regular LX grade.[2]

Underlying vehicle

Milena retains the body and engine identifiers of the Felicia vehicle to which the edition package was fitted.
