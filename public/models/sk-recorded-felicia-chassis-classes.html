Felicia — Slovak recorded chassis classes

The recorded prefixes distinguish a Combi GLX AEE class from a hatchback LX 136 class. Both encode model year W/1998 and Z shell preparation; their plant characters differ. Preparation alone does not identify installed restraints.[1][2]
