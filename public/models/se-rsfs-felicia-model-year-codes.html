Škoda Felicia — Swedish model-year identifiers

The Swedish authority’s vehicle classifications distinguish Felicia, Combi and Pickup records by year class and complete administrative model code. These identifiers concern the commercial model; they are not manufacturer part numbers or VINs.[1]

Identification

The tables explicitly name Combi and Pickup, while their unqualified Felicia entries do not provide a factory body code. The 1996 names use LXi/GLXi for both 1.3 and 1.6 offers; later 1.6 records use LX/GLX. The labels do not establish an identical equipment or engine assembly across year classes. Steering side and original option/build/chassis selectors remain Unconfirmed.[1]
