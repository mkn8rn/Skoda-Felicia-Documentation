Felicia Combi LX 1.9 D 47 kW — Slovak-language 4/98 programme

The 4/98 Slovak-language manufacturer programme identifies Combi LX 1.9 D 47 kW. Grade, body and written engine/output distinguish this offer; fitted extra equipment distinguishes its more specific configurations.[1]
