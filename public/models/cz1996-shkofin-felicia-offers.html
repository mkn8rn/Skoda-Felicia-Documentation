The dated illustration distinguishes Felicia, Combi, Pickup and Vanplus offers by written grade and engine label. Identification boundaries

The source writes GLX for its 50 kW higher-grade offers; that label does not by itself establish equivalence with a GLXi grade. Combi, PICK-UP and VANPLUS are explicitly separate. The bare Felicia designation does not give a factory body code, and the Vanplus rows do not specify seating. Equipment, national delivery, steering side and production/option/chassis selectors remain Unconfirmed.[1]
