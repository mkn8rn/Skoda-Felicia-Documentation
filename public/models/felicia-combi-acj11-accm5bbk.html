Škoda Felicia Combi ACJ11/ACCM5BBK

The Felicia Combi variant ACJ11, execution ACCM5BBK, is an estate configuration recorded under EC approval e11*93/81*0019*08, execution revision 1. RDW associates it with the 781.136M petrol engine at 50 kW. The recorded execution became valid on 30 April 1999.[1][2][3][4]

The approval prefix e11 identifies the United Kingdom approval authority. The execution-validity date identifies an approval event; it does not establish the car’s manufacturing date or a production change in a fitted part.[7]

RDW defines gearbox type as the method of selecting gears. The letter M and the five-gear count do not identify a Škoda gearbox assembly or its individual gear ratios.[7]

These are values and ranges for the approved execution. The recorded door-count field is four; the passenger Combi’s five-door sales description includes its rear liftgate. The height and running-order mass ranges must not be replaced with a single assumed value for every car.[4][7][1] [9]

The fuel-consumption and carbon-dioxide figures are homologation test values, rather than a guarantee of consumption in everyday use.

Applicability

The ACJ11/ACCM5BBK identifiers specify an approval configuration. The RDW records do not assign it to a Dutch LX, LXi, GLX, GLXi or named-edition offer, nor identify steering side, an equipment-option set or a production/VIN range. A shared engine designation does not establish that any particular sales grade used this execution.[1][4][2]
