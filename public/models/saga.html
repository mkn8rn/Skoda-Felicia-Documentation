Škoda Felicia Saga

Felicia Saga was a Polish-market limited series. Škoda Auto Polska’s retrospective lists the Saga name among the limited Felicia editions offered by its national sales organisation; a later survey of Felicia editions also places Saga among the Polish special series.[1][2]

Edition designation

The Saga label identifies a named Felicia sales series rather than a separate chassis family. Its limited-edition classification comes from the Polish importer’s Felicia series list. The name is distinct from the similarly marketed Gemini, Genua and other Polish-edition names recorded in the same national range.[1]

Vehicle identification

A Saga badge describes the market series; it does not replace the underlying body and engine identifiers. Identify a particular car by its Felicia body type and approval, engine code and output, and its original Polish sales specification. Shared articles describe the hatchback, Combi and common engine families.
