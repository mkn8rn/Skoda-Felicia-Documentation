Škoda Felicia Pickup 1.6 MPI with partial-leather upholstery (Germany, 1999)

Škoda Auto Deutschland’s account describes the German-market Felicia Pickup in 1999 as generally paired with a 1.6 MPI petrol engine and lists partial-leather upholstery as an optional €639 extra. The publication does not name a factory option code or identify the affected seat-cover components.[1][2]

Optioned configuration

1999 German-market Felicia Pickup 1.6 MPI with partial-leather upholstery option
Market and dateGermany; the press account prices the Pickup as a 1999 new vehicle.[1]
Body and approval typeTwo-seat commercial Pickup; approval type 797. Type 797 is also used for the separate Felicia Fun body.[2][3]
Engine offer1.6 MPI petrol; the Germany retrospective does not state a Pickup engine code, displacement or rated output.[1]
Interior optionPartial-leather upholstery; listed as an extra-cost option for €639. No factory option code or seat-cover identifiers are given.[1]

Identification limits

The German retrospective confirms the optional trim and market engine pairing but gives no chassis/VIN boundary, steering-side identifier, gearbox code, order code, seat-cover material split or part numbers. Its general engine pairing is not a vehicle build record. Engine construction and the Felicia AEE description are in the shared 1.6-litre engine article.
