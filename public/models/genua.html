Škoda Felicia Genua

Felicia Genua was a Polish-market limited series. Škoda Polska names Genua among the editions prepared specifically for its national offer; a later history associates it with LX-level equipment and a yellow exterior finish.[1][2]

Market and grade

The Polish importer identifies Genua as a Felicia limited edition intended for the Polish market. A later automotive history describes it as LX-based, distinguishing its equipment foundation from the engine and body identifiers used to specify an individual car.[1][2]

Appearance features

The Genua description records yellow body paint and sport-themed trim, including a three-spoke Momo steering wheel, black B-pillar finish and a chromed exhaust tip. These appearance details distinguish the edition; the installed engine remains identified by its code and rated output.[2]

Vehicle identity

Genua remains a Felicia passenger-car equipment series. Body type and installed engine code identify the individual vehicle beneath the market edition name.
