Felicia Blue Sky 1.6 L with ABS — Germany, July 1998

The German importer writes Felicia Blue Sky 1.6 L with ABS for this July 1998 folding-roof offer. Its common colour list gives Nautic blue, Hot-Chilli red metallic and Pazific green metallic, with a black folding roof. The named ABS equipment distinguishes it from the other separately priced offers.[1]

Recognition limits

The body and roof colour labels identify an advertised choice, not a full factory paint or roof product. The folding roof does not make this hatchback a Felicia Fun or a full convertible body. A named ABS or alloy version does not establish the absence of that equipment in a differently labelled offer.[1]
