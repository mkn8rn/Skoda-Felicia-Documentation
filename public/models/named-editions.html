Named editions

Škoda and its market representatives offered named Felicia editions with equipment and appearance distinctions. These editions retained their underlying production body and powertrain identities.[1]

Named Felicia editions and their distinguishing features
EditionDistinction
Felicia AtlantaCzech special edition associated with the 1996 Olympic Games; the hatchback and Combi used a 1.3 BMM engine rated at 50 kW.[2]
Felicia Blu SkyNamed edition distinguished by a retractable fabric roof.
Felicia BohemiaUK edition offered in hatchback and estate forms with alloy wheels, colour-matched fittings and remote security equipment.[3]
Felicia Color LineLX-based paint and interior treatment; the later Color Line II is a separate designation in retrospective lists.
Felicia Combi ExcellentCombi estate equipment edition.
Felicia FamilyCzech 2000 edition in hatchback and Combi forms, with 1.3 MPI engines at 40 or 50 kW, 1.6 MPI at 55 kW, and 1.9 diesel at 47 kW.
Felicia GeminiPolish-market limited edition designation.
Felicia FriendGerman March 1999 equipment edition with separately listed hatchback/Combi and 1.6 air-conditioning offers.
Felicia GenuaPolish-market edition; later accounts describe yellow paint and selected equipment.
Felicia KliffPolish edition name; Clif/Kliff spellings are treated as aliases.
Felicia LatoPolish-market edition designation.
Felicia Laurin & KlementLuxury edition introduced in 1995 and produced in hatchback and Combi forms.
Felicia MagicCzech 1998 edition with granite-metallic paint; the contemporary notice names hatchback and Combi bodies and gives separate Combi prices for the two 1.3-litre outputs.
Felicia MilenaPolish-market limited edition designation.
Felicia MysteryNamed edition; Škoda identifies its Prague Astronomical Clock motif.
Felicia PacificPacific green/Laguna blue metallic edition; hatchback and Combi identified in a September 1997 Czech-language leaflet.[4]
Felicia PerfektPolish edition; Perfekt and Perfect are spelling variants in retrospective records.
Felicia Safe-lineCzech 1999 safety edition with ABS and four airbags; sold in hatchback and Combi body forms.
Felicia SagaPolish-market limited edition designation.
Felicia SambaPolish-market limited edition designation.
Felicia Sport LineNamed appearance and equipment edition; “Sportline” is an orthographic variant.
Felicia SpringHungarian-market Felicia Spring specification from 1996.
Felicia TangoFirst limited edition recorded by the Polish importer, introduced in March 1997.
Felicia Tango 2Separate numbered Polish edition name, distinct from Tango.
Felicia TrumfCzech 2000 edition offered as hatchback and Combi with the 1.3 MPI engine rated at 50 kW.
