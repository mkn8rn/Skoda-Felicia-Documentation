Škoda Felicia Fun 1.6 MPI with partial-leather upholstery option (Germany, 1999)

Škoda Auto Deutschland’s retrospective describes the Felicia Fun as offered in Germany and identifies a general 1.6 MPI pairing for its 1999 account. The Fun is the leisure pickup with a movable cabin partition and auxiliary rear seating. The article also lists partial-leather upholstery as a paid option; it is not standard equipment on every car represented by the offer.[1][2]

Configuration

Felicia Fun with the German-market partial-leather option
Model and bodyFelicia Fun recreational pickup with movable rear partition and occasional 2+2 seating; type 797 is also used for the distinct two-seat Pickup derivative.[2][3]
Market and dateGermany; a retrospective reports the related new-car price for 1999. This sales date does not define a production interval or chassis/VIN cutoff.[1]
Engine offer1.6 MPI petrol in the German Fun account; that record does not give an engine code or a German-specific rating. Fun technical data separately lists the 1.6 MPI at 1,598 cm³ and 55 kW.[1][4]
UpholsteryPartial-leather interior listed as a €639 option, not as standard fitment. No order code, seat-specific parts or build boundary is identified.[1]
Series transmission and driveFive-speed manual gearbox and front-wheel drive are stated for the Fun series; no German-specific transmission code is given.[4]

Identification limits

The German sales account does not state whether the upholstery option was tied to a trim, engine, steering side or individual option code. It supplies no vehicle-level production/VIN limit or upholstery-component identifiers. A 1999 market offer cannot identify every car’s fitted interior or full parts set.
