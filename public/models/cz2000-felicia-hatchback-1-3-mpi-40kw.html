Škoda Felicia Hatchback 1.3 MPI 40 kW (Czech Republic, 2000)

The Czech launch announcement priced the standard Felicia Hatchback as a 1.3 MPI model rated at 40 kW. Unlike the Trumf and Family editions, this was the standard Felicia hatchback designation.[1]

Rated output40 kW
Fuel systemMultipoint injection (MPI)
DriveFront-wheel drive
Market and offer dateCzech Republic; launch announcement 28 February 2000

Model distinctions

This specification combines the named body with the 40 kW engine code 135M. It is separate from the 50 kW Trumf and from Family cars whose equipment package included additional comfort and safety equipment.

Market and date

The dated source records the Czech offer on 28 February 2000 and describes the promotional models as limited-duration series. That offer date is not a vehicle production or registration date.
