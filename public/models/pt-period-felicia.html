Portuguese period Felicia selections

The Portuguese advertisement distinguishes Felicia hatchback and Felicia Break estate, each with its own 1.3 GLXi and 1.9 GLXD caption. Both are described as five-door passenger bodies. The captions do not supply a factory body code or production revision.[1]
