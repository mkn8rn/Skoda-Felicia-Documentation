Škoda Felicia GLXi 1.3 MPI 50 kW (Netherlands, 1999)

The Dutch-market GLXi hatchback in Pon Mobiel’s 1999 specification uses the 50 kW 1.3-litre MPI engine. It has the higher equipment package, unlike the separately listed LXi version that shares its engine rating.[1]

Factory specification

Dutch-market Felicia GLXi hatchback, 1999 specification
BodyFive-door hatchback, type Š 791[3]
Engine1,289 cm³ petrol, code 136M
Output50 kW
Fuel systemMultipoint injection (MPI)
Airbag equipmentTwo airbags listed[1]
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive
Market and dateNetherlands; 1999 importer specification

Code 136M denotes the higher-output Felicia 1.3 multipoint-injection engine. It is distinct from the 40 kW MPI 135M and the earlier single-point-injection 136B.[1][2]

Distinguishing equipment

The brochure marks GLXi with body-coloured handles and electrically adjustable exterior mirrors, side mouldings, tinted glazing, two airbags, central locking, front fog lamps and an interval rear wiper. Its interior has velour trim and a split rear bench. These listed features distinguish GLXi from the LX/LXi specification even when both use 136M. The Dutch importer lists two airbags for this GLXi configuration; airbags were unavailable on the same-body LXi with the same engine and output.[1]
