Felicia Safe-line hatchback — Czech 1999

Safe-line identifies the Czech safety edition in hatchback form. It has ABS and front/side airbags for both front occupants; the edition/body name alone does not identify the engine, paint or fitted extra equipment.[1][2]

Body and edition recognition

The five-door hatchback is factory type Š 791 and is distinct from the estate-bodied Combi.[3] Rallye red and Candy white are the manufacturer’s two paint choices. Front and side airbags at each front seating position distinguish Safe-line from a driver-only or two-front-airbag configuration.[1][2]
