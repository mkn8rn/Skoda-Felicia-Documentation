Škoda Felicia Pickup

The ordinary Felicia Pickup is the two-seat commercial derivative, with an open load platform behind the cab. It is listed as type 797, a number also used for the distinct Fun derivative.[1]

Body identification

The cab and forward body follow the Felicia design, with a separate open load platform behind the two-seat cabin. Type 797 also appears in the type approval for the recreation-oriented Felicia Fun; that shared type number does not make the Fun an ordinary pickup trim. The Fun changes the cabin into a convertible 2+2 arrangement and has its own homologation record.[1][2]

Export designation

Škoda sold commercial derivatives in Latin America using the Volkswagen Caddy name. A Caddy-branded export pickup therefore belongs to the Felicia commercial family where its type and manufacturer identification confirm the relationship; a badge by itself is not proof of a Felicia-derived vehicle.[3]
