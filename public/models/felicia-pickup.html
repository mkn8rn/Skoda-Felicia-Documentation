Škoda Felicia Pickup

The Felicia Pickup is the factory two-seat commercial pick-up derived from the Felicia platform. Škoda’s 2019 history dates Pickup production at Kvasiny from August 1995; its 2014 retrospective places commercial-body production at Vrchlabí without dating that factory attribution. Neither source establishes whether production moved or overlapped between plants, or which builds are associated with each location.[1][7] European type records identify the passenger car as type 791, the estate as 795, and the Felicia pickup as type 797; export records may use another sales name for the same pickup vehicle.[2]

Body and type

The cab and forward body follow the Felicia design, with a separate open load platform behind the two-seat cabin. Type 797 also appears in the type approval for the recreation-oriented Felicia Fun; that shared type number does not make the Fun an ordinary pickup trim. The Fun changes the cabin into a convertible 2+2 arrangement and has its own homologation record.[2][3]

A 2001 technical account distinguishes the two-seat Pickup shell from the short passenger shell and the longer Combi/Vanplus shell group. It states that Pickup shells used the adapted structure from the model’s August 1995 introduction; it also describes front and floor-area reinforcement for airbag equipment and Volkswagen 1.6 MPI or 1.9 D engines. Škoda’s retrospective separately places body-structure reinforcement in a 1996 change package. The sources do not identify Pickup-specific panels or reconcile whether the 1996 package describes an additional revision, so neither type 797 nor a sales date establishes a complete Pickup parts application.[6][7]

Powertrain and revisions

Škoda’s histories record 1.3-litre petrol, 1.6 MPI petrol and 1.9 D diesel engines in the Felicia family, but do not map every output to the Pickup. The 2022 German approval schedule groups body types when listing engine-power classes, so those entries do not establish a Pickup-only application. Vehicle-specific engine and factory identity require more than the family-wide range.[1] March 1998 Pickup engine offers.

Production continued into 2001. Škoda recorded 124,565 Pickup vehicles for the 1995–2001 period.[1]

Export designation

Škoda sold commercial derivatives in Latin America using the Volkswagen Caddy name. A Caddy-branded export pickup therefore belongs to the Felicia commercial family where its type and manufacturer identification confirm the relationship; a badge by itself is not proof of a Felicia-derived vehicle.[4]

Fun and Pickup distinction

Škoda Auto Deutschland’s 1999 German-market 1.6 MPI and upholstery account appears in its history of the Felicia Fun.[5] The Fun’s movable cabin partition and auxiliary seating distinguish that recreational derivative from this two-seat commercial Pickup; the German account does not establish a Pickup engine or upholstery offer.[5] The two derivatives share approval type 797, but their body and cabin layouts differ; see Felicia Fun for its configuration evidence.[3]
