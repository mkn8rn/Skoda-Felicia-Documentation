Felicia Federation fleet VIN classifications

The Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina government’s 14 November 2007 trade-in decision records seven Felicia vehicles. Four first-eleven-character classes distinguish the listed engine/transmission, emissions, shell-preparation, body, version, model-year and plant fields. The five-door hatchback and LX codes follow the Felicia VIN legend; this is not a national new-car sales programme.[1][2]

Recognition limits

The dots omit six individual serial characters. The report’s production years do not decode model-year Y or 1, which lie outside the inspected year legend. Emissions H is also unlisted there. Preparation 6/Z identifies an airbag-prepared shell, not fitted airbags; 135 is a bare engine class without a B/M/L suffix. Original national delivery, steering side and equipment-package selection are Unconfirmed.[1][2]
