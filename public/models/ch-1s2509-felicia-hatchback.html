Felicia hatchback — CH 1S2509 final-drive classes

CH 1S2509 identifies five-door hatchback with engine 781.136B. The record distinguishes two final-drive alternatives, without identifying a gearbox product.[1]

Recognition

The written type-level chassis template is TMBEFF.1.......... It is not an individual chassis number or a production boundary.[1]

The same recorded prefix covers the approval’s two final-drive alternatives, 4.12 and 4.17. It does not select between them or identify a gearbox stock number.[2]

VIN fields and earlier Swiss approval prefixes distinguish the masked recognition fields from an individual vehicle’s data-label selections.
