Felicia — Russian period sales classes

The November 1997 sources name LX, LXi and GLX body/capacity classes; the road test identifies an LX hatchback separately from the catalogue offers.[1][2]

A year in the dealer table is an advertised production-year label, not an exact manufacturing/chassis boundary or proof of an original equipment package.[3]
