Škoda Felicia Family Combi 136M 50 kW (Czech Republic, 2000)

The Czech-market Felicia Family Combi with the 50 kW engine is a distinct body-and-powertrain entry in Škoda’s February 2000 limited-series offer. Its factory body type is Š 795; its engine code is 136M.[1]

Model distinctions

The Family package included a driver airbag, remote central locking with alarm, electrically operated front windows, front fog lamps, tinted glazing, body-coloured exterior mirrors and bumpers, side mouldings, large wheel covers, a centre console with removable storage and a Škoda MS 201 radio. The seat fabric was offered in grey-blue or grey-green. The hatchback had luggage-compartment side pockets; the Combi used roof-rack rails and did not have those side pockets. The 136M code and Š 795 body type distinguish this car from the other Family specifications; the same Family name does not identify a single body or engine combination.

Market and date

The dated source is a Czech launch announcement of 28 February 2000. It describes the Family series as a limited-duration offer; this announcement date is not a production date, model year or first-registration date.
