Škoda Felicia Trumf

Felicia Trumf was a Czech-market limited equipment series introduced in February 2000 as a higher-comfort alternative to the basic Felicia. The contemporary announcement listed both hatchback and Combi bodies, each with a 1.3 MPI petrol engine rated at 50 kW.[1][2]

Czech series launch

Škoda announced the limited Felicia Trumf series in February 2000. The model was positioned between the basic Felicia and the more extensively equipped Family series. Its standard package added body-coloured exterior mirrors and handles, large wheel covers, tinted glazing, a four-spoke steering wheel, a compact centre console and central locking.[1]

Distinguishing equipment

Trumf is identified by this standard equipment package. Its exact engine and body form are separately specified: the dated Czech announcement lists a hatchback and a Combi, each with the 1.3 MPI engine rated at 50 kW. The equipment list does not identify optional equipment as standard fitment.[1]

Model identification

The body type remains Š 791 for a Felicia hatchback and Š 795 for a Combi. Engine code 136M identifies the 50 kW 1.3 MPI; Trumf identifies the dated Czech equipment series.[2][3]

Body and engine configurations

The February 2000 Czech offer specifies hatchback and Combi Trumf models with the 1.3 MPI engine rated at 50 kW.[1]

  • Felicia Trumf hatchback 1.3 MPI 50 kW
  • Felicia Trumf Combi 1.3 MPI 50 kW
