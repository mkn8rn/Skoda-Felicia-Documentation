Identification

Trumf is the Czech February 2000 equipment series, offered as a hatchback or Combi with the 50 kW 1.3 MPI. Recognition features include body-coloured mirrors and handles, large wheel covers, tinted glazing, a four-spoke steering wheel, compact centre console and central locking.[1]
