Škoda Felicia — Turkish period models

The period import-sales classifications distinguish the written Felicia and Felicia Combi engine labels. The nominal names identify sales classes without a complete factory body, engine or parts selector.[1]

Identification

Hürriyet’s May 1998 Turkish launch report identifies the revised Felicia front and names hatchback and estate bodies, with Yüce Auto as distributor. It does not give a Turkish body/grade/engine offer matrix, and its date is not a manufacturing or parts cutoff.[2]

The later sales table explicitly names Combi, while its unqualified Felicia names give no factory hatchback code or exact door count. The 2001 accounting year does not select a vehicle model year, steering side, original equipment or chassis range.[1]
