Italian Felicia configurations, 1995–1998

The Italian advertisements distinguish LX and GLX passenger offers as Felicia hatchback and Felicia Wagon estate. A dated grade/engine offer identifies a sales selection; its publication date does not define a manufacturing revision or original equipment package.[1][2][3]
