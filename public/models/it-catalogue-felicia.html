Škoda Felicia — Italian catalogue configurations

The Italian archive distinguishes commercial grades, indexed year ranges and hatchback, Wagon and Fun entries. Wagon identifies the estate body. The family-level equipment aggregation does not identify equipment fitted to any one of the five-door passenger entries or the two-door, four-seat Fun record.[1]
