Škoda Felicia LXi 1.3 MPI 50 kW (Netherlands, 1999)

The Dutch importer’s 1999 Felicia offer specifies the LXi hatchback with a 50 kW 1.3-litre multipoint-injection engine. In the same market record, the LXi designation marks this higher-output 1.3 rather than the 40 kW engine listed under LX.[1]

Factory specification

Dutch-market Felicia LXi hatchback, 1999 specification
BodyFive-door hatchback, type Š 791[3]
Engine1,289 cm³ petrol, code 136M
Output50 kW
Fuel systemMultipoint injection (MPI)
Airbag equipmentNot available in this Dutch specification[1]
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive
Market and dateNetherlands; 1999 importer specification

Code 136M identifies the high-output multipoint-injection 1.3. It is mechanically distinguished from the related single-point 136B and lower-output MPI 135M.[1][2]

Equipment grade

In the Dutch specification the LXi is grouped with the LX equipment level, while the GLXi is listed separately with body-colour exterior details, tinted windows, front airbags, electric mirror adjustment and central locking. The LXi’s 50 kW engine is the feature that separates this offer from the 40 kW LX version. The Dutch importer marks airbags unavailable for this LXi configuration; its same-body GLXi counterpart with the same engine and output is listed with two airbags.[1]
