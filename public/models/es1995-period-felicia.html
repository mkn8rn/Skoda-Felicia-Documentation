Spanish 1995 Felicia period selections

The February comparison identifies a five-door GLX. The May list distinguishes LXi, LXi Plus and GLXi by their written output/grade labels but does not name their bodies. The June launch identifies the Combi body and three powertrain columns, without an engine/grade pairing or confirmed Spanish delivery.[1][2][3]
