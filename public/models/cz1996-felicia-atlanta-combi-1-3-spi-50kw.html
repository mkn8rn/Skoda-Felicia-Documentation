Škoda Felicia Atlanta Combi 1.3 BMM 50 kW (Czech Republic, 1996)

The 1996 Czech-market Felicia Atlanta Combi paired the five-door Combi estate body with the 50 kW BMM 1.3 petrol engine. It was an LX-based Olympic special edition.[1]

Model distinctions

The 136B code identifies the 50 kW single-point-injection engine. Atlanta cars were offered in green, white or red with 14-inch alloy wheels, a Blaupunkt radio, driver airbag, tachometer with digital clock and central locking. The Combi edition also had rear loudspeakers; its factory body type is Š 795.[1][2]

Market and date

Atlanta commemorated the 1996 Summer Olympic Games in Atlanta and was sold in the Czech market. The cited retrospective identifies it as a 1996 edition; it does not give an individual production date.
