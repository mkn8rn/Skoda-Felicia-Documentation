Škoda Felicia Combi LX 1.9 D 47 kW (Netherlands, 1999)

Pon Mobiel’s 1999 offer lists a Felicia Combi LX with a 1.9 D rated at 47 kW.[1] Škoda describes the Felicia 1.9 D as naturally aspirated with indirect injection; the technical specification identifies the 1.9-litre Felicia diesel as AEF.[3][2]

Dutch-market Felicia Combi LX, 1999 specification
BodyFive-door estate[1], type Š 795[3]
Engine1,896 cm³ diesel, code AEF[2]
Output47 kW[1]
Fuel systemIndirect-injection diesel[3]
Airbag equipmentNot available in this Dutch specification[1]
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive[1][2]
Market and dateNetherlands; 1999 importer specification[1]

The brochure's LX 1.9 D column marks power steering as standard, and its technical page describes the five-speed manual transmission as standard equipment for the range.[1]

Engine and grade

The Dutch 1999 Combi list includes both LX 1.9 D and GLX 1.9 D versions, each rated at 47 kW. The equipment matrix assigns additional safety and exterior equipment to GLX; the engine output alone does not determine grade.[1]
