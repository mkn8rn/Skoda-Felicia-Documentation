MTX Felicia conversions

MTX RS offered Felicia-based Cabrio bodies and named conversion packages. Converter identity, donor body and the written package name distinguish these selections from ordinary Škoda factory trims. MTX Family is a Combi conversion designation, separate from the Czech factory Family edition.
