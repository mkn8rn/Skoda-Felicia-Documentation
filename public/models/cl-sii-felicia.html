Škoda Felicia — Chilean SII classes

The Chilean SII classifications distinguish Felicia LX/GLX passenger and estate classes by administrative code, written equipment label and model year. These selectors distinguish assessment entries; they do not establish a manufacturer engine/gearbox, option package or production boundary.[1]
