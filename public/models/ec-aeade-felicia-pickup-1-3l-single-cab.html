Škoda Felicia Pick up 1.3L P/U C/S — Ecuadorian designation

The Ecuadorian guide distinguishes this written commercial designation by model year. P/U C/S identifies a pickup with a single cab, without an exact factory derivative or seat-count mapping.[1][2]
