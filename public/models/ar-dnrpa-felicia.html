Felicia — Argentina registration classes

The 2002 national valuation annex distinguishes five Felicia body/grade designations. Its make, type and model identifiers are registration-table codes, separate from factory model and component identifiers.[1]

Classification recognition

Five-door passenger and estate entries have different type codes. The estate LX 1.9D entry omits Combi in its name, but retains the source’s estate body field. A registration class or GLX/GLXI badge alone does not identify engine, equipment or build revision.[1]
