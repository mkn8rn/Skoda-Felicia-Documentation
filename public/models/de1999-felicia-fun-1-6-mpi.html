Škoda Felicia Fun 1.6 MPI — Germany, 1999 offer

The German manufacturer account identifies a 1999 1.6 MPI Fun offer. Fun’s recreational movable-wall cabin and auxiliary rear seating distinguish it from the ordinary two-seat commercial Pickup. Partial-leather upholstery is a separately specified choice.[1]

Identification

The manufacturer’s market account supplies the Fun name, 1.6 MPI designation and 1999 offer context; it does not give original product codes or build boundaries. ADAC separately lists a 55 kW German Fun catalogue entry with four seating positions and the series number 797.[1][2]

Selection conditions

The 1999 sales reference and ADAC’s July 1999–February 2001 catalogue period are not individual manufacturing or VIN limits. Upholstery choice, actual powertrain products and factory option codes remain separate selectors.[1][2]
