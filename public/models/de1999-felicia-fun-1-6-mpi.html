Škoda Felicia Fun 1.6 MPI (Germany, 1999)

Škoda Auto Deutschland’s retrospective discusses the Felicia Fun in Germany and associates its 1999 offer with a 1.6 MPI petrol engine. In that context the vehicle is the recreational Fun, with a movable rear cabin wall and auxiliary seating, rather than the two-seat commercial Pickup. The retrospective gives a new-car price of about €14,000 for 1999.[1][2]

Factory and market configuration

Felicia Fun, German-market 1999 offer
Model and bodyFelicia Fun recreational pickup, with a sliding rear cabin partition and occasional 2+2 seating; approval type 797, also used for the distinct Pickup derivative.[2][3]
Market and dateGermany; the manufacturer retrospective reports a new-car price for 1999. This is not a production date or chassis-number range.[1]
Engine offer1.6 MPI petrol. The German account does not state an engine code, displacement or output. A separate Fun technical brief lists its 1.6 MPI version as 1,598 cm³ and 55 kW, without giving a German-specific code or build boundary.[1][4]
Series transmission and driveFive-speed manual gearbox and front-wheel drive for the Fun series; the source does not give a German-specific gearbox code.[4]
Reported 1999 priceAbout €14,000 in the manufacturer’s retrospective; price is not a vehicle identifier.[1]

Optional upholstery

The German account lists partial-leather upholstery as an optional extra for €639 but gives no option code or upholstery-part identifiers. Felicia Fun 1.6 MPI with the partial-leather option, Germany 1999.[1]

Identification limits

The German account identifies a dated market offer but does not give a Fun-specific engine code, gearbox code, steering-side identifier, order or option code, or production/VIN limit. The 1999 sales reference therefore does not define the manufacturing interval or a vehicle-specific factory parts set. The 1.6-litre petrol engine is described in the Felicia 1.6-litre engine article.
