Škoda Felicia Comfort — Italian designation

The Italian archive distinguishes Comfort LX and Comfort GLX designations in hatchback and Wagon forms. Body, commercial engine/output and the catalogue year range distinguish the selections; Comfort does not establish equivalence to another market’s grade.[1]
