Škoda Felicia Combi LX 1.6 MPI 55 kW (Netherlands, 1999)

The LX 1.6 MPI is a separately specified Felicia Combi configuration in Pon Mobiel’s 1999 Dutch offer. It combines the five-door estate body with the AEE 1.6-litre petrol engine rated at 55 kW.[1]

Factory specification

Dutch-market Felicia Combi LX, 1999 specification
BodyFive-door estate, type Š 795[3]
Engine1,598 cm³ petrol, code AEE
Output55 kW
Fuel systemMultipoint injection (MPI)
Airbag equipmentNot available in this Dutch specification[1]
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive
Market and dateNetherlands; 1999 importer specification

The Dutch brochure specifies power steering on the LX AEE Combi and a five-speed manual transmission. The 55 kW AEE power unit is specified here in the Dutch Combi LX estate.[1][2]

Equipment grade

The Dutch importer lists this 1.6 under LX, not GLX. Its equipment grade differs from the separately listed GLX 1.6 Combi, which adds the higher trim package while retaining the same engine family and rated output.
