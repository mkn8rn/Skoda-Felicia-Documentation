Škoda Felicia GLXi 1.3 SPI 50 kW (Northern Ireland, 1996)

The GLXi was the upper equipment grade in the Felicia hatchback columns of Škoda UK’s Northern Ireland price list dated 1 March 1996. It retained the 68 bhp, 50 kW 1.3-litre single-point-injection engine used by LXi grades, with a larger standard equipment set.[1]

Configuration

Northern Ireland Felicia GLXi hatchback, 1 March 1996 price list
BodyFive-door hatchback, type Š 791[3]
Engine1,289 cm³ petrol, code 136B
Rated output50 kW (68 bhp)
Fuel systemBosch Mono-Motronic single-point injection
Transmission and driveFive-speed manual; front-wheel drive
Offer dateNorthern Ireland, 1 March 1996

The 136B code identifies the higher-output single-point-injection engine, not the equipment grade. Škoda’s technical panel pairs this version with a five-speed synchronised manual transmission and front-wheel drive.[1][2]

Distinguishing equipment

Compared with LX and LXi, the list gives GLXi front fog lamps, tinted glass, body-side protective mouldings, central locking, velour upholstery, an electric tilt sunroof and a removable centre console. It also specifies the GLXi wheel-trim design and coded Blaupunkt audio equipment. These are factory-listed grade features for this dated market offer.

Identification

GLXi identifies both an equipment grade and, in this price list, the 50 kW hatchback configuration. The type plate and 136B engine code provide the body and mechanical identifiers; GLXi alone does not encode a universal engine fitment.
