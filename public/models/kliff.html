Škoda Felicia Kliff

Felicia Kliff was a Polish-market special edition based on the LXi grade. The spelling varies in later records: Škoda Polska used “Kliff” in its 2014 history, while its 2003 limited-series list used “Clif.” The records identify one edition under variant spellings, not two separate model names.[1][2]

Name and market

The 2014 importer account identifies Kliff among the editions prepared specifically for the Polish market. The earlier Polish importer article lists Clif in its Felicia limited-series roster. The spellings “Kliff” and “Clif” denote the same Polish edition in the importer records.[1][2]

Equipment

A model history describes Kliff as LXi-based and lists a Blaupunkt São Paulo radio, front fog lamps, alloy wheels and tinted glass among its additions. The grade name describes the market equipment version; the engine and body remain independently identified by their factory codes.[3]

Vehicle identity

Kliff is not a body derivative. A Felicia’s factory body type, engine code, market plate and fitted equipment identify the individual vehicle beneath the edition name.
