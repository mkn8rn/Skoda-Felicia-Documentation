Škoda Felicia Kliff

Kliff and Clif are variant spellings for the Polish Felicia edition in the importer’s 2014 and 2003 accounts.[1][2]

Recognition equipment

The later equipment account describes an LXi basis with a Blaupunkt São Paulo radio, front fog lamps, alloy wheels and tinted glass.[3]
