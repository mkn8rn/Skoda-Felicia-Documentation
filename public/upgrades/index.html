Documented upgrades

Documented upgrades is the index for index for evidenced owner modifications, conversions and retrofits.

Mechanical modifications
Published engine, transmission, braking, suspension, steering, and exhaust modifications.
Electrical and equipment changes
Documented control-unit, instrumentation, lighting, comfort-equipment, and wiring changes.
Body and interior changes
Published body, trim, seating, and storage modifications.
Case-study evidence
Original build reports, exact vehicle and donor components, fabrication and wiring changes, measurements, follow-up results, and reported failures.

A documented modification records what an identified builder did and reported. Factory compatibility, long-term reliability, and suitability for another vehicle require their own evidence; legal requirements must be attributed to a jurisdiction and date.
