About this guide

Škoda Felicia Documentation is an independent, open-source reference project. Its subject is the 1994–2001 Felicia range and its historical context. The aim is exhaustive, verifiable documentation preserved within this repository. The present edition contains subject indexes, an engine overview, and a source bibliography; most technical articles remain unwritten.

Scope

The guide covers history, production, model identification, systems and individual components, evidenced compatibility, repair, maintenance, everyday use, and documented upgrades. It will include manufacturing and component-level details where reliable evidence is available.

Hard rule: the repository must be the one-stop reference for all Felicia information and remain useful if every cited website disappears. Articles contain verified facts and explanations in original, compact, standard technical English. An external link supplies evidence, never missing technical content. Unwritten information is a documented gap.

Article format

Articles use a descriptive title, a short introduction, a contents list where useful, section headings, cross-links, and numbered references. Related internal articles appear under “See also”; external technical links belong in references or the separate source bibliography. A stub is explicitly labelled, and subjects awaiting articles are ordinary text rather than broken links.

A part has one shared article across all applicable Felicia versions. Model and repair entries link to that article, which records its documented identifiers and applications.

Sources and evidence

Prefer manufacturer records, identified technical publications, and component-makers’ documentation. Attribute supplier application claims and owner observations to their sources. Preserve disagreements and missing information rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

Specifications and procedures must identify the applicable variant. Part-number substitutions, interchangeability, ECU components, production totals, and upgrade results each require evidence for the exact claim. Online citations should include the publisher, title, URL, and access date; print citations need the consulted edition and a precise locator.

Copyright

Public articles describe verified information independently; source text is neither copied nor directly quoted. Referenced manuals, diagrams, product photographs, scans, and catalogue tables are not reproduced. Public accessibility is not permission to redistribute a work, and a purchased copy remains subject to its publisher’s rights.

The editing guide describes article structure, citations, stable links, and checks before publication. Corrections and new evidence can be proposed through the repository.
