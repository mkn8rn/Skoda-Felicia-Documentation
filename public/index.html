Škoda Felicia

Škoda Felicia Documentation is an independent reference to the Felicia range produced by Škoda Auto between 1994 and 2001.[1] Its scope includes history and production, model identification, components, repair, maintenance, everyday use, and documented modifications.

This guide concerns the 1994–2001 range. The earlier car bearing the Felicia name belongs in the historical background.

The aim is a complete technical and historical reference preserved here, usable without access to its sources. Articles record information in original technical English; citations identify the evidence. The guide is at an early stage, and unwritten subjects are marked explicitly.

Parts

Original parts groups factory systems and components by vehicle system. Compatible parts covers evidenced alternatives and interchanges. Engines records the production engine families, principal dimensions, and construction. Each component has one shared article across all versions that use it.

Relevant diagram sources belong in each component article’s references; protected illustrations are not reproduced.
